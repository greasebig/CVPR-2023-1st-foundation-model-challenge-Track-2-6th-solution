## 1. BAL: Bidirectional Autoregression in Latent Space for Learning Human Movement Representations

**中文标题**: BAL: Bidirectional Autoregression in Latent Space for Learning Human Movement Representations

**作者**: Genki Kinoshita, Ko Nishino

**发布日期**: 2026-11-13

**arXiv ID**: [2611.18559v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18559v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18559v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Learning semantically meaningful human movement representations from 3D pose sequences is essential for human behavior modeling. Existing self-supervised methods derive supervision from artificial assumptions, e.g., invariance to augmentation within a certain range or recoverability of masked regions at a certain temporal scale, introducing heuristic hyperparameters that make the learned semantics sensitive to their settings. We propose BAL, a self-supervised framework that instead exploits supervision intrinsic to human movement: the natural ordering structure of everyday behavior. BAL autoregressively predicts in a semantically abstract latent space, analogous to how humans anticipate others' actions rather than their precise joint configurations. Bidirectional autoregressive prediction, both forward and backward in time, fully leverages these ordering constraints while enabling representation extraction using both past and future context at inference. To suppress representation collapse, BAL decomposes each prediction target into a global component capturing sequence-level semantics and a local component capturing context-independent movement. We prove that the local targets retain nontrivial angular diversity unless the representations are completely collapsed, preserving a meaningful training signal. Experiments on large-scale datasets demonstrate that BAL's representations are highly effective for motion captioning, forecasting, and interpolation, outperforming baselines on most evaluation metrics.

</details>

## 2. JVT: Just Video Transformer makes pixel-space video diffusion simple

**中文标题**: JVT: Just Video Transformer makes pixel-space video diffusion simple

**作者**: Jianjun Zhou, Hua Geng, Haoyu Guo

**发布日期**: 2026-11-13

**arXiv ID**: [2611.18549v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18549v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18549v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Direct diffusion modeling of high-dimensional raw video data is challenging, and existing pixel-space methods often rely on specialized architectures. We introduce JVT (Just Video Transformer), a simple large-patch DiT-style Transformer for pixel-space video diffusion, without a pretrained autoencoder, auxiliary detailers, cascaded stages, or U-Net-style skip connections. We systematically examine video patch embeddings, register tokens, and adaptive layer normalization to identify design choices that balance generation quality and computational efficiency. The resulting JVT design achieves generation quality competitive with latent diffusion baselines in both class-conditioned and unconditional settings. The pixel-space formulation of JVT also naturally supports joint generation of pixel-aligned modalities. We demonstrate this capability through camera-conditioned RGB-disparity generation, which improves camera following relative to RGB-only baselines and yields explicit scene geometry without an additional reconstruction model at inference. These results highlight the potential of simple DiT-style Transformers for pixel-space video diffusion, and we hope our findings offer useful insights and practical guidance for future research.

</details>

## 3. StreamEmbed: Real-Time Indexing from Pixels for Screenshot Retrieval

**中文标题**: StreamEmbed: Real-Time Indexing from Pixels for Screenshot Retrieval

**作者**: Dayoon Ko, Yichuan Wang, Soyeong Jeong, Joseph E. Gonzalez, Gunhee Kim et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-13

**arXiv ID**: [2611.18516v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18516v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18516v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.IR, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Screen history can serve as searchable visual memory for people and agents, but unlike an offline corpus, it must be encoded and indexed as each screenshot arrives. OCR-based indexing is fast but loses visual information; visual encoding takes over two seconds. Yet screens change incrementally: most pixels repeat within and across screenshots. This raises a question: do visual encoders need to recompute their representations from scratch for every new screenshot? We present StreamEmbed, a system for incremental visual embedding that exploits this redundancy. Guided solely by pixel differences, it combines spatial token pruning, which skips blocks that repeat a neighbor, with temporal KV reuse, which reuses cached keys and values for blocks unchanged across screenshots. A small learned adapter compensates for pruning and reuse to preserve fidelity to full-screenshot embeddings. On ScreenMemEval, our benchmark of 10,426 questions over 184 hours of screen histories from two humans and a computer-use agent, StreamEmbed sets a new accuracy-latency Pareto frontier on an M2 Pro GPU: it encodes up to 3.7x faster than full-screenshot encoding without sacrificing accuracy and improves accuracy over OCR-based indexing by up to 6.3 Recall@10 points at matched latency. A memory-efficient vector index stores a simulated year of screenshots in 0.13 GB of RAM. Together, these results make real-time indexing of screen history in its native visual form practical, laying a foundation for fully local visual assistants.

</details>

## 4. AI-Based Multi-View Echocardiographic Image Synthesis using Dynamic Shape Control

**中文标题**: AI-Based Multi-View Echocardiographic Image Synthesis using Dynamic Shape Control

**作者**: João Freitas, Anca Balinisteanu, Ahmed Youssef, Vitor Hugo Pereira, Jan D'hooge et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-13

**arXiv ID**: [2611.18375v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18375v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18375v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Although automated methods have improved echocardiographic workflows, progress remains constrained by the need for large, well-annotated datasets. As the development of clinical databases is often limited by ethical and logistical challenges, synthetic data generation has emerged as a promising alternative. However, existing approaches commonly rely on left ventricle shape-based supervision or are tailored to specific cardiac views, limiting the generation of additional standard and unconventional imaging planes and the preservation of multi-view consistency across cardiac chambers. To address these limitations, we propose a novel framework for shape-controlled synthesis of multi-view echocardiographic sequences. First, multi-view cross-sections are extracted from dynamic whole-heart meshes. Ultrasound-like appearance is then introduced through a two-stage AI-based pipeline. In Stage 1, an unpaired GAN-diffusion model translates these cross-sections into realistic echocardiographic frames. As this translation is performed independently at the frame level, Stage 2 employs masked video reconstruction to improve temporal coherence and cardiac motion. Evaluation demonstrated high frame-level realism for both stages, while the integration of Stage 2 substantially improved video-level motion quality and temporal consistency. Overall, the proposed framework enables the generation of realistic multi-view echocardiographic sequences from diverse cardiac models, providing a scalable source of annotated data for dataset augmentation and the development of multi-view cardiac assessment methods.

</details>

## 5. MaskSCD: Bitemporal Mask Classification for Semantic Change Detection in Remote Sensing Images

**中文标题**: MaskSCD: Bitemporal Mask Classification for Semantic Change Detection in Remote Sensing Images

**作者**: Weikang Yu, Pedram Ghamisi

**发布日期**: 2026-11-13

**arXiv ID**: [2611.18350v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18350v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18350v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/EricYu97/MaskSCD.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Semantic change detection (SCD) identifies where the land surface has changed and which land-cover transition has taken place. Most deep SCD networks predict the change map and the land-cover map of each date with separate per-pixel decoders. These outputs need not agree, so a pixel can be labeled as changed although it has the same class at both dates, and per-pixel decisions fragment objects. We present MaskSCD, which formulates SCD as the mask classification of a bitemporal label-pair image. Each bitemporal segment, a region of constant land-cover class pair, is predicted as one mask shared by both dates together with one class per date, and the change map is derived from the predicted class pair. This formulation keeps the change and semantic maps consistent and the label space linear in the number of classes. A bitemporal masked-attention transformer decoder implements it by reading each segment in both images and relating the two readings, and a bitemporal fusion pyramid provides per-date and change-aware features. MaskSCD is evaluated on SECOND, Landsat-SCD and Hi-UCD, which span 0.1 to 30 m resolution and four to nine land-cover classes, against fourteen recent SCD models re-trained under one protocol. MaskSCD achieves the best separated kappa, semantic change $F_1$ and transition $F_1$ on all three datasets and exceeds the strongest competitor by about five points in mean separated kappa. It produces no contradictory change pixels, which account for nearly 30% of the predicted change of some multi-task networks. The code is available at https://github.com/EricYu97/MaskSCD.

</details>

## 6. Unlocking Cross-Attention in Label-Scarce Regimes: A Weakly Supervised Approach for Video Semantic Segmentation

**中文标题**: Unlocking Cross-Attention in Label-Scarce Regimes: A Weakly Supervised Approach for Video Semantic Segmentation

**作者**: Andrea Marelli, Alessandro Ardenghi, Giacomo Boracchi, Luca Magri

**发布日期**: 2026-11-13

**arXiv ID**: [2611.18324v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18324v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18324v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Weakly Supervised Video Semantic Segmentation (WS-VSS) aims to learn temporally consistent semantic object masks in videos using only video-level labels. While fully supervised models successfully learn temporal relationships by means of cross-attention, training these temporal weights under weak supervision is unstable and even pushes the model below its single-frame baseline. Therefore, existing WS-VSS methods exploit temporal consistency by relying on external priors, such as optical flow. Unfortunately these cues are non-semantic, computationally expensive and noisy, degrading the supervisory signal. Instead of supervising attention weights directly or using external priors, we propose MemCAM, a WS-VSS transformer framework that learns temporal cross-attention directly through feature-space self-supervision. MemCAM introduces a CAM Propagation Algorithm that synthesizes a supervisory signal by propagating past CAMs according to patch-level feature similarities in the latent space. Specifically, we constrain their induced CAMs through a consistency objective between predicted and propagated maps, stabilizing temporal attention learning. Experiments on the YouTube-Objects v2.2 and WS$^2$ datasets demonstrate that MemCAM consistently outperforms the temporal localization and segmentation performance of WS-VSS alternatives, showing that cross-attention can be effectively trained in label-scarce video settings through internal self-supervision.

</details>

## 7. Pose-based Neural Clothing for Animated Characters

**中文标题**: Pose-based Neural Clothing for Animated Characters

**作者**: Julian N. Heidenreich, Vinicius C. Azevedo, Jakob Buhmann, Lento Manickathan, Arnold Moon et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-13

**arXiv ID**: [2611.18253v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18253v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18253v1)

**类别**: cs.GR, cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Traditional garment simulation for animated characters is a time-consuming bottleneck for artists due to slow simulations and tedious parameter tunings.Recent data-driven methods offer a powerful alternative, capable of learning from a dataset of simulated data to generate high-quality deformations. A lightweight network design allows data-driven systems to achieve a real-time inference speed. This significantly improves the artist workflow, as they can interactively pose a character and see high-quality folds and wrinkles at animation time, thereby allowing to make creative decisions ahead of time. However, existing neural clothing methods have presented appealing results on datasets based on realistic human models (\eg SMPL), but when trained on animated character datasets, they struggle to generalize to unseen poses. In this paper, we aim at designing neural networks and training strategies to generalize better to unseen poses in real-time to enable the interactive posing applications. We focus on a production-oriented setting where the model is trained for a single garment in a quasi-static setup. Technically, we leverage a smooth, expressive latent space by first training a Variational Auto-Encoder (VAE). We then progressively train the VAE encoder to transition from reconstruction to pose-driven prediction. To guide this prediction, we condition the network on both low-dimensional pose parameters and character body surface displacements. Finally, we introduce a novel local gradient histogram loss that mitigates the over-smoothing effect of reconstruction losses. Extensive evaluations on production-level assets and public datasets demonstrate that our method produces sharp, realistic wrinkles, smooth large-scale drape, and temporal coherence in real-time, offering a robust and easily integrable solution for interactive posing workflows.

</details>

## 8. D2ToF: One-Step Diffusion for Depth Super-Resolution from Lightweight Single-Photon LiDAR Waveforms

**中文标题**: D2ToF: One-Step Diffusion for Depth Super-Resolution from Lightweight Single-Photon LiDAR Waveforms

**作者**: Pallabjyoti Deka, Lewis Wilson, Stephen McLaughlin, Jonathan Leach, Abderrahim Halimi

**发布日期**: 2026-11-13

**arXiv ID**: [2611.18243v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18243v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18243v1)

**类别**: eess.IV, cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Single-photon LiDAR (SPL) sensors offer exceptional temporal resolution and single-photon sensitivity for depth imaging. However, cost-effective SPL arrays typically suffer from extremely low spatial resolutions (e.g. $8\times 8$ pixels), limiting their real-world utility. In this paper, we propose a novel, one-step super-resolution framework, D2ToF, Distilled Diffusion for Time-of-Flight, that bridges the gap between high-fidelity generative modeling and real-time execution. Our approach uses an amortized variational distillation strategy that allows for a measurement-based data consistency term to be incorporated directly into the one-step training objective. This encourages that the synthesized high-resolution depth maps are consistent with the captured low-resolution Time-of-Flight (ToF) histograms. To overcome the severe ill-posedness of upsampling extremely degraded inputs, we introduce a multimodal guidance strategy. By leveraging a foundational monocular depth model to extract structural priors from a high-resolution reflectivity image, our network is guided by a rich triad of the ToF histogram, reflectivity map, and relative depth estimate. Experimental results outperform evaluated baselines, achieving $16\times$ spatial super-resolution (from $8\times 8 \times 100$ histograms to $128\times 128$ depth maps) at an accelerated inference speed of approximately $83$ milliseconds.

</details>

## 9. SemRAG: Semantic-Guided Sentence-Level Retrieval-Augmented Sign Language Production

**中文标题**: SemRAG: Semantic-Guided Sentence-Level Retrieval-Augmented Sign Language Production

**作者**: Xiao Liu, Shiwei Gan, Yafeng Yin, Peishuo Liu, Bowen Guo et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-13

**arXiv ID**: [2611.18189v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18189v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18189v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Sign language production (SLP) aims to generate sign pose sequences that faithfully express spoken-language text while preserving the rhythm and coordination patterns of signing. Common SLP methods learn text-to-pose mappings from training data but often suffer from semantic inaccuracies in long or complex sentences. Retrieval augmentation offers a way to introduce realistic signing priors to provide more explicit guidance for text-to-pose generation. However, existing retrieval-based SLP methods typically operate at the gloss or word level, relying on gloss-pose dictionaries or isolated sign segments that require costly annotations and fail to preserve continuous sentence-level dynamics. To bridge this gap, we propose SemRAG, a semantic-guided sentence-level retrieval-augmented framework that retrieves full-sentence signing templates as priors without constructing gloss/word-level sign dictionaries. While such templates preserve local motion patterns and global rhythm, they also introduce a semantic mismatch problem: irrelevant motions in the retrieved sentence may interfere with the target semantics. To address this issue, we introduce an SLT-guided template semantic localization module that identifies target-relevant temporal regions within the retrieved template while suppressing irrelevant signing content. To adaptively incorporate the template features into the generator, we further introduce layer-specific learnable gates that regulate their contribution across generator layers. Extensive experiments show that SemRAG improves semantic consistency and motion quality over baselines under both back-translation and motion accuracy metrics.

## 10. TelePhysica: Agentic Reconstruction of Physically Interactive Scenes

**中文标题**: TelePhysica: Agentic Reconstruction of Physically Interactive Scenes

**作者**: Ruiqi Shu, Xufeng Zhan, Yuanzhi Liang, Haibin Huang, Chi Zhang et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-13

**arXiv ID**: [2611.18078v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18078v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18078v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Direct image-to-video diffusion models entangle pixel synthesis with implicit latent motion, precluding persistent 3D mechanical equilibrium, mid-rollout intervention, and counterfactual force control. Conversely, single-pass programmatic 3D reconstruction from an uncalibrated photograph leaves monocular depth and support clearance underconstrained, causing reconstructed scenes to interpenetrate or collapse under gravity. We present TelePhysica, a closed-loop framework that reconstructs an executable, multi-regime physical world from a single photograph to drive stateful 3D interaction and trajectory-grounded video synthesis. A vision-language coding agent synthesizes separable visual meshes and declarative physical models within a two-stage verification loop that pairs pixel-aligned silhouette critique with a zero-force gravitational equilibrium preflight. The verified world compiles into a unified solver coupling rigid bodies, articulated chains, co-dimensional cloth shells, and 3D tetrahedral elastodynamics, whose simulated rollouts condition pretrained video diffusion models through pixel-aligned structural streams. On a 100-scene single-image benchmark, closed-loop execution raises equilibrium-verified world recovery from $0\%$ in single-pass synthesis to $81\%$. Controlled constitutive ablations show over an order-of-magnitude larger localized corner displacement and out-of-plane pleating in finite-element cloth than in matched rigid-plate proxies. Conditioning video diffusion on simulated trajectories increases dynamic motion-mask overlap by $2{\times}\text{--}5{\times}$ and preserves motion direction under counterfactual force reversals where direct image-to-video models fail.

We propose a machine learning technique for predicting nuclear masses and charge radii based on gated recurrent units (GRU) that integrates learnable product-unit mappings into the candidate state to model multiplicative dependencies among neighboring nuclei. Computations are performed in the complex domain to jointly capture amplitude and phase dynamics. Across three data partitions and three training seeds per partition, AM-PU-GRU achieves a mean mass interpolation RMSE of 0.2698 MeV and a temporal RMSE of 0.1905 MeV. Configuration comparisons support the integration of learned products into gated recurrence. Without mass inputs, MI-PU-GRU predicts charge radii with an RMSE of 0.019947 fm, 14.40% below the fixed WS* prior. Matched radius controls further show how candidate fusion shapes the benefit of learned products. Paired errors, nuclide-resolved maps, and inference measurements characterize predictive gains and computational cost. ## 21. RiverScan: Learning to Accumulate on River Networks

**中文标题**: RiverScan: Learning to Accumulate on River Networks

**作者**: He Zhu, Zhiyi Gao, Youhuan Yang, Han Quan, Ming Li

**发布日期**: 2026-11-13

**arXiv ID**: [2611.18005v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18005v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18005v1)

**类别**: cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Accurate runoff prediction across large river basins requires models to capture heterogeneous local hydrological responses and their accumulation downstream. River-network connectivity provides a natural structure for this task, but using it effectively requires learning how to combine contributions from subcatchments of different sizes. We frame this challenge as learning to accumulate and introduce RiverScan, a spatial operator based on area-weighted selective state-space recurrence. RiverScan recursively combines local temporal representations with accumulated upstream states in topological order. Contributing-area fractions account for subcatchment size, while learned retention controls how upstream information persists downstream. Experiments on 608 stations in the Danube basin show that RiverScan outperforms the evaluated temporal and graph baselines in overall predictive performance. Structural controls and ablations demonstrate that composing heterogeneous subcatchment representations in a manner aligned with hydrological accumulation substantially improves runoff prediction, with the benefits of correct topology growing along the river-network hierarchy. These findings establish physically structured accumulation as an effective inductive bias for hydrological modeling and motivate finer-grained, fully distributed AI-based hydrological models.

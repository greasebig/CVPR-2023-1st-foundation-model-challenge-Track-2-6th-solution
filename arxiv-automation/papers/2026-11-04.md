## 1. DreamX: Video Diffusion Model is a Universal Latent Action Learner

**中文标题**: DreamX: Video Diffusion Model is a Universal Latent Action Learner

**作者**: Jisoo Kim, Chaehyun Kim, Junsu Kim, Jiho Park, Seongchan Kim et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-03

**arXiv ID**: [2611.05377v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05377v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05377v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.RO

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Learning a universal action representation from video requires modeling dynamics that are consistent across different executions of the same action yet invariant to changes in embodiment and appearance. Existing latent action models infer actions from visual transitions, but their representations often entangle the underlying action with irrelevant visual variations, limiting transfer across embodiments. We introduce DreamX, which repurposes a pretrained video diffusion model as a universal latent action learner. Our approach extracts compact latent actions from the video model's rich spatiotemporal features, compressing each latent video frame by $\boldsymbol{300\times}$ ($\boldsymbol{1{,}200 \rightarrow 4}$ tokens). In addition, by leveraging the video model's generative prior, these latent actions capture action-relevant dynamics disentangled from visual appearance. The resulting latent space serves as a shared action interface across embodiments (human and robot), environments, and action parameterizations. On LARY-Bench, DreamX achieves state-of-the-art performance among latent action models. Moreover, actions encoded in one setting remain effective in another: the latent space supports any-to-any video transfer and zero-shot action transfer across diverse embodiments and environments. Furthermore, this shared interface also enables policy learning in a compact latent action space, reducing the computational cost of video-diffusion-based policies.

</details>

## 2. GSCV++: Lightweight 3D Gaussian Splatting Sequence Compression via Standard Video Codecs

**中文标题**: GSCV++: Lightweight 3D Gaussian Splatting Sequence Compression via Standard Video Codecs

**作者**: Qi Yang, Yingzhan Xu, Kai Zhang, Li Zhang

**发布日期**: 2026-11-03

**arXiv ID**: [2611.05359v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05359v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05359v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

This paper presents GSCV++, a lightweight pipeline for 3D {G}aussian {S}platting (GS) sequences {C}ompression with standard {V}ideo codecs. To meet the requirements of low-complexity and hardware compatibility from the industry, GSCV++ jointly improves preprocessing, 3D-to-2D mapping, and practical video compression configuration of GS compression. First, for preprocessing, energy-adaptive inter-frame spherical harmonic (SH) alternative current (AC) dimensionality reduction (EA-SHDR) is proposed to produces a compact and stable temporal SH AC feature space. It can reduce the data volume of following mapping and practical compression, as well as less complexity while eliminating redundant information. Second, for 3D-to-2D mapping, prior-based Inter-MiniPLAS (P-MiniPLAS) is designed to generate smooth 2D feature maps through prior-based weighting factor inference and small scale remapping. Compared with vanilla Parallel Linear Assignment Sorting (PLAS) and Morton scan, P-MiniPLAS ensures the generated 2D maps are more suitable for the video codec while maintaining a low level of computational complexity. Third, for practical compression, content-aware generalized compression (CA-GC) is developed to use a non-uniform bit-depth split for coordinate map generation, and a coding unit size selection is introduced for efficient compression complexity control, which fully exploits the performance of video codecs without involving normative changes. Experiments demonstrate that GSCV++ reports over 20\% in intra- and 60\% in inter-prediction rate-distortion gains compared with state-of-the-art (SOTA) methods. GSCV++ also achieves faster 2D map generation, encoding, and decoding than other SOTA methods using the same type of video codecs. Ablation studies verify the effectiveness and robustness of the proposed modules and provide insights for subsequent research.

</details>

## 3. Möbius: A Unified Motion-Language Model Bridging Understanding and Generation

**中文标题**: Möbius: A Unified Motion-Language Model Bridging Understanding and Generation

**作者**: Jiazhi Shu, Chengfeng Zhao, Jinyi Wang, Yubo Zhao, Zhiyang Dou et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-03

**arXiv ID**: [2611.05279v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05279v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05279v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

We aim to build a truly unified motion model that can understand, generate, and edit human motion within a single framework. Existing motion UMMs remain only partially unified: motion understanding is handled by a language model, while generation is often delegated to a separate diffusion module operating over learned motion latents. We introduce Möbius, which brings the entire diffusion process into the same multimodal representation space used for motion understanding. Clean motion, noisy motion, and text jointly participate in shared attention, while role-specific parameters process understanding and generation streams. Importantly, Möbius operates directly on continuous frame-level raw motion, requiring neither discrete tokenization nor a pretrained motion VAE. This simple formulation naturally supports motion captioning, text-to-motion generation, temporal completion, and instruction-guided editing within a single model. On HumanML3D, Möbius achieves state-of-the-art performance among unified motion models. Scaling to 1.7B parameters and 1,700 hours of motion data further demonstrates that our framework is both effective and scalable.

## 4. Route Anything: A Study of Model and Policy Routing for General-Purpose AI Systems

**中文标题**: Route Anything: A Study of Model and Policy Routing for General-Purpose AI Systems

**作者**: Pengfei Zhou, Yixing Ma, Hexin Wang, Weidong Tang, Zhenglin Wan et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-03

**arXiv ID**: [2611.05275v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05275v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05275v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Artificial general intelligence (AGI) requires a method that can understand, generate, reason, and act across diverse domains and modalities. Rather than relying on a single model to master every capability, a promising path toward AGI now is to orchestrate a heterogeneous ecosystem of specialized models, tools, and policies (candidates) into a unified AI system. At the heart of this vision lies a fundamental question: how can an AI system dynamically select and coordinate the most capable candidate for each task while balancing performance and computational cost? In this work, we present Route Anything, a unified framework for studying model and policy routing across general-purpose AI systems. We develop a unified theoretical foundation that characterizes optimal routing under quality--cost trade-offs and establishes how the information available to a router determines its attainable performance. To systematically investigate routing across diverse capabilities, we first construct an omni-modal trajectory matrix spanning 25 benchmarks across language understanding, image, video, and speech generation, coding and workplace agents, and robotic manipulation, comprising 374,401 scored runs from 452 candidate configurations. Building on this framework, we train and test different routers to investigate the potential of omni-modal model routing, the capabilities of learned routers, and the conditions under which routing improves efficiency. Our results reveal substantial opportunities for capability orchestration: per-input oracle routing outperforms the best fixed candidate by 5.4 points, and learned routers outperform it by 0.3 points across 16 benchmarks, by 2.1 points on video generation, and by 1.1 points on robot manipulation while using 24% less policy time. More importantly, routing raises efficiency by 8.82x on average compared with the reference model.

## 5. Diffusion Plug-in Priors for Self-Supervised Ultrasound Video Restoration

**中文标题**: Diffusion Plug-in Priors for Self-Supervised Ultrasound Video Restoration

**作者**: Chu Chen, Mingduo He, Yining Li, Yinhao Wu, Wei Tang et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-03

**arXiv ID**: [2611.05206v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05206v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05206v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

\textit{Objective:} Ultrasound videos are limited by hardware-constrained spatial resolution and signal-dependent speckle, while paired low- and high-resolution acquisitions for supervised restoration are rarely available. We aim to jointly super-resolve and despeckle ultrasound videos without paired training data or network retraining. \textit{Methods:} We propose UltraDiff, which uses a frozen pre-trained diffusion model (DM) as a plug-in prior and restores each frame by optimizing the latent seed of the DM sampler under a range-constrained maximum a posteriori formulation. We derive a speckle-aware data-fidelity term from a Rayleigh scale-family model and characterize its relative-residual and variance-adaptive weighting properties. We further introduce a stabilized fidelity that is bounded below and strictly convex in log intensity for positive observations in its unclamped form. A total-variation regularizer complements the DM prior, and a temporal seed update warm-starts each frame from the optimized seed of its predecessor. \textit{Results:} On EchoNet-LVH videos with simulated speckle and additive noise, UltraDiff with a natural-image prior improves PSNR by 1.24\,dB over the strongest baseline, and ultrasound-related priors improve it further. On real clinical echocardiography videos, UltraDiff reduces the mean absolute error of zero-shot ejection fraction (EF) prediction from 7.31 to 6.37 EF points relative to DUP, the strongest baseline. \textit{Conclusion:} Combining a speckle-aware fidelity with frozen diffusion priors enables ground-truth-free ultrasound video restoration that benefits downstream measurement. \textit{Significance:} UltraDiff needs neither paired data nor retraining and is not tied to a specific diffusion checkpoint. Code associated with this work will be made publicly available upon acceptance.

</details>

## 7. Trajectory Evaluation via Rollout-Free World Model for End-to-end Autonomous Driving

**中文标题**: Trajectory Evaluation via Rollout-Free World Model for End-to-end Autonomous Driving

**作者**: Junzhe Wang, Jinqing Zhang, Qingjie Liu

**发布日期**: 2026-11-03

**arXiv ID**: [2611.05072v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05072v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05072v1)

**类别**: cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Although existing candidate trajectory evaluators have substantially improved end-to-end planning accuracy, their underlying mechanism remains suboptimal. Model-based evaluators typically make scoring future-aware by explicitly predicting candidate-conditioned future states over the planning horizon, which we term rollout. However, rollout incurs high inference cost and accumulates prediction errors. We argue that planning does not require reconstructing a full future scene for each candidate, but only future-relevant information for reliable comparison. We therefore propose RFWorld, a Rollout-Free World model for end-to-end trajectory evaluation. RFWorld constructs a shared scene memory and shapes it into a future-readable representation via lightweight training-time temporal readout with semantic supervision, keeping training overhead low. At inference time, candidate trajectories directly query the shared memory via sparse spatial readout for scoring, reducing computation by roughly the planning horizon times the number of candidate trajectories, while avoiding recursive rollout errors. Experiments on NAVSIM and Bench2Drive show that RFWorld learns a more planning-aligned world representation and achieves state-of-the-art performance.

## 8. FloMo: 3D Scene Flow as a World Action Model Intermediate for Learning from Human Video

**中文标题**: FloMo: 3D Scene Flow as a World Action Model Intermediate for Learning from Human Video

**作者**: Jeremy Collins, Namra Patel, Ayush Agarwal, Shitij Govil, Animesh Garg

**发布日期**: 2026-11-03

**arXiv ID**: [2611.05062v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05062v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05062v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Pretrained video generation models distill internet-scale motion priors into robot policies, but full video is a redundant signal for action prediction. We argue that dense 3D motion is the medium through which video pretraining best transfers to robot actions, and that predicting motion rather than video affords better sample-efficiency for action prediction. FloMo is a 5B video-generation model fine-tuned to predict 3D scene-flow and action tokens. Rendering scene flow as RGB and encoding it into the same latent space as video enables a pretrained video generation backbone to efficiently adapt to predict 3D scene flow. Co-trained on action-free human video and action-labeled robot teleoperation data, our model benefits from human-video pretraining and generalizes to unseen objects, motion primitives, and tasks. Replacing motion prediction with a video prediction objective degrades out-of-distribution generalization, while adding a video prediction target alongside motion lowers policy success rather than improving it. When motion, video, and action are predicted jointly, action tokens attend far more to motion than to video, suggesting that predicting video wastes model capacity. Together, these results indicate that motion effectively mediates the transfer from video pretraining to action. More information can be found at flomo-wam.github.io.

## 9. On the Learnability of Footwear Robustness: Footstep Recognition Under Unseen Conditions

**中文标题**: On the Learnability of Footwear Robustness: Footstep Recognition Under Unseen Conditions

**作者**: Robyn Larracy, Erik Scheme

**发布日期**: 2026-11-03

**arXiv ID**: [2611.05049v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05049v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05049v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, eess.SP

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Footstep patterns, captured using pressure or force-sensitive walkways during natural walking, offer a convenient and passive approach to biometric recognition. However, footwear variability poses a major challenge to practical deployment, as it impacts both the foot-sensor interface and the user's underlying gait mechanics. This study considers the particularly difficult scenario where users enroll under one fixed footwear condition but must later be recognized in arbitrary, unseen footwear. Using the StepUP-P150 dataset, we show that footwear-induced shift, while substantial and heterogeneous across individuals, contains shared structure that can be learned from a training population and transferred, zero-shot, to previously unseen users and footwear styles. We do this using BiStepNet, a novel architecture that decomposes spatial and temporal identity cues into independently trainable streams and outperforms compact 3D spatiotemporal baselines for cross-footwear recognition. We demonstrate that cross-footwear recognition performance scales systematically with the volume and diversity of footwear representation in the training set, with gains transferring to footwear styles not directly observed during training. Moreover, we show that auxiliary gait datasets with only partial modality overlap (incorporated through the architecture's decomposed design) can be leveraged to further improve robustness to unseen footwear conditions.

## 10. Behind Video Generation: Observations and Insights on Multimodal Understanding from Kling

**中文标题**: Behind Video Generation: Observations and Insights on Multimodal Understanding from Kling

**作者**:  Kling Team, Dong Chen, Jialu Chen, Xinlong Chen, Yifan Dai et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-03

**arXiv ID**: [2611.04916v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04916v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04916v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video generation has progressed from isolated clips to coherent short-form audiovisual sequences, and the bottleneck is shifting from rendering to semantic control. This report summarizes the observations and insights on multimodal understanding gained from successive generations of Kling. We argue that generation quality is the projection of multimodal understanding through the entire data--conditioning--alignment--evaluation chain, and we examine this chain module by module. <summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Latent-space path planning (LSPP) compresses the configuration space of a robotic manipulator into a compact, differentiable manifold learned by a variational autoencoder, enabling fast gradient-based planning without explicit inverse kinematics. Existing LSPP frameworks enforce obstacle avoidance with a binary collision classifier, whose saturated gradients near obstacle boundaries and lack of temporal structure cause the optimizer to stall and provide no forward-invariance guarantee. We replace the classifier with a neural control barrier function (CBF) learned directly in the latent space. The barrier is trained with a three-term objective that combines safe and unsafe sign constraints with a discrete-time decrease condition evaluated on transitions of an obstacle-blind nominal planner, and it is enforced at planning time by a closed-form, first-order correction that projects each goal-directed latent step back onto the CBF constraint. On a 7-DoF Franka Panda manipulator, the CBF planner achieves a 75.6\% success rate on a fixed-obstacle task, 12.0 percentage points above the best of 35 tuned classifier configurations, and 83.8\% on a multi-position task. A sweep of the decay rate $α$ characterizes the safety--performance trade-off, with the best operating points in $α\in [0.10, 0.20]$. In a fully general random-obstacle setting, success drops to 33.0\%, which we trace to covariate shift between the obstacle-blind training transitions and the states visited by the corrected planner.

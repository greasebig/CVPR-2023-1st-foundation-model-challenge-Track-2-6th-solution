## 1. VibeVideoBench:Evaluating Multimodal Agents on Code-Driven Video Generation

**中文标题**: VibeVideoBench:Evaluating Multimodal Agents on Code-Driven Video Generation

**作者**: Muxi Diao, Wenbin Xu, Lele Yang, Xuyang Ma, Yuzhe Chen et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35864v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35864v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35864v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Diffusion video models produce striking clips, but they cannot reliably render an exact formula, a correct chart, a ruleconsistent game or narration synchronized to on-screen events. A different route is emerging: multimodal agents that write code to render every frame and synthesize every sound. We introduce VibeVideoBench, a benchmark for evaluating agents on code-driven video generation. Its 239 self-contained tasks in English and Chinese span 12 categories, from data stories and music-driven pieces to classroom lessons and paper explainers; each asks an agent, running in its native harness inside a sandbox, to deliver a finished MP4 with sound within one hour. Evaluation is hierarchical and evidence-based: program checks of the delivery spec, about 13k task-specific rubric checks traced to the requirements they enforce, and five quality dimensions anchored per task, judged by three VLM judge panels and validated with blind human preferences. Across nine frontier agent systems and 2,151 scored rollouts, the benchmark is far from solved: GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 lead clearly, yet even the best system fails a core requirement on about a quarter of tasks. Whether a video arrives at all matters as much as its quality, and three quarters of the lost videos are final renders started too late, run too slowly or never started. The judges agree with each other and with human preferences on system rankings, but only moderately on individual videos.

</details>

## 2. FL4N: Feed-forward Latent Model for 4D Novel View Synthesis

**中文标题**: FL4N: Feed-forward Latent Model for 4D Novel View Synthesis

**作者**: Eunbeen Hong, Hyeonseo Yu, Honggyu An, Jooho Son, Hyunah Ko et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35854v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35854v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35854v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

4D novel view synthesis (NVS) aims to render dynamic scenes across viewpoints and timestamps, which requires jointly inferring 3D structure and temporal dynamics. Existing monocular feed-forward methods often rely on explicit 4D Gaussian representations, which are prone to temporal inconsistency and severe rendering artifacts. We present FL4N, the first feed-forward latent 4D NVS model. Instead of relying on explicit 4D primitives, FL4N encodes a 4D scene prior into a unified latent representation across space and time. Our core insight is that learning temporal dynamics as residuals on top of a pretrained static latent prior enables effective 4D modeling with limited dynamic training data. To this end, we introduce a lightweight dynamic encoder that leverages static scene features to disentangle object motion from camera-induced parallax. Trained solely with an image synthesis objective, FL4N achieves state-of-the-art 4D NVS quality while rendering in real time. Its learned latent representation generalizes to real-world videos and disentangles viewpoint from time.

</details>

## 3. Multimodal Concert Video Editing: System Design and Exploratory Evaluation

**中文标题**: Multimodal Concert Video Editing: System Design and Exploratory Evaluation

**作者**: Lennart Schiweck, Adrian Kempf, Michelle Retig, Moritz Feucht, Tim Mangliers et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35816v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35816v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35816v1)

**类别**: cs.MM, cs.CV, cs.SD

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Editing multi-camera concerts requires coordinated decisions about musical relevance, visual quality and cut timing. We present a modular framework that combines instrument activity from multitrack audio, visible instruments and image sharpness with beat-based timing and probabilistic camera selection. An interpretable scoring model links these components to the choice of views over complete shot intervals. Component tests and an exploratory expert study on a four-camera concert yield mixed assessments across the editing variants. The findings motivate extending local audiovisual relevance with contextual and sequence-level criteria. The architecture provides a basis for testing these extensions and studying how component behaviour affects the quality of complete edits.

## 4. VidEdit3D: Flexible 3D Gaussian Editing with Video Diffusion

**中文标题**: VidEdit3D: Flexible 3D Gaussian Editing with Video Diffusion

**作者**: Yanming Yang, Chenxi Song, Tong Zhao, Juntao Xu, Wenhao Rao et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35756v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35756v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35756v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Recent advancements in text-guided 3D editing, particularly with 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS), have shown promise but still lag behind the flexibility of 2D editing tools. Existing methods face a critical trade-off between preserving multi-view consistency and allowing structural modifications. Image-based editors can also struggle to apply the intended edit to objects viewed from unusual angles. To address these limitations, we introduce VidEdit3D, a framework that reformulates 3D scene editing as a trajectory-based video editing task. By drawing a parallel between camera movement in static 3D scenes and temporal dynamics in video, we leverage the robust spatiotemporal priors of pre-trained Video Diffusion Models (VDMs) to encourage semantic and structural coherence across views. Specifically, we propose Trajectory-Guided Rendering (TGR) to select informative keyframes and construct smooth video clips aligned with VDM input requirements. To propagate edits from an anchor view to these clips, we develop Flow-Aligned Video Editing (FAVE), a training-free framework incorporating an Editing Consistency Guidance (ECG) mechanism. ECG contrasts source and edited image conditions at a shared latent state, using scheduled source-image amplification to strengthen adherence to the edited reference while maintaining original scene context. Finally, we introduce Region-Selective 3DGS Refinement (RS-GS) to strictly confine geometric and appearance updates to target primitives, reducing background attribute leakage. Experiments demonstrate stronger instruction alignment and better multi-view consistency than the baselines.

</details>

## 5. Triage: Skip, Skim, or Refine Attention Tiles in Video Diffusion Transformers

**中文标题**: Triage: Skip, Skim, or Refine Attention Tiles in Video Diffusion Transformers

**作者**: Yeonjae Kim, Omin Kwon, Seong Hoon Seo, Jae W. Lee

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35706v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35706v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35706v1)

**类别**: cs.LG, cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Attention over tens of thousands of visual tokens dominates the inference of video diffusion transformers (DiTs). Existing methods reduce this cost through sparsity, low precision, or both, but use a single precision for all kept tiles. We observe that the output error introduced by approximation varies widely across tiles. Many tiles can be skipped with little error, others must be computed but gain little beyond low precision, and a few retain substantial error even at low precision. We present Triage, a training-free attention method that assigns each tile one of three approximation tiers: skipping it by replacing it with its block means, skimming it in NVFP4, or refining it with NVFP4 residual correction. A lightweight router predicts each tile's error under each tier from block statistics and moves a tile to a more accurate tier only when the predicted error reduction justifies the extra compute, with a single price setting this trade-off. A fused kernel executes all three tiers in a single pass on the FP4 tensor cores of RTX Blackwell GPUs. Across Wan2.2, HunyuanVideo, and LTX-2.5, Triage accelerates end-to-end generation by up to 2.32x over dense FlashAttention, and in each of five settings it runs faster than every baseline while reaching 0.3-3.0 dB higher PSNR than the highest-PSNR baseline.

</details>

## 6. OmniShowcase: Omnidirectional Subject-Consistent Video Generation from Multi-View Images

**中文标题**: OmniShowcase: Omnidirectional Subject-Consistent Video Generation from Multi-View Images

**作者**: Junyeong Ahn, Geonwoo Kim, Kinam Kim, Gihwi Kim, Hoonjin Jung et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35684v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35684v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35684v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Generating videos of a specific subject from a few reference images remains challenging when the camera reveals surfaces that are not visible in the input. Early subject-to-video (S2V) methods condition on a single reference image and must therefore infer the subject's unseen appearance and geometry as the viewpoint changes. To address this limitation, recent methods extend S2V to multiple reference views, providing more information about the subject from different viewpoints. However, they do not explicitly specify the 3D structure that connects these observations. In addition, existing training data do not guarantee that viewpoint changes preserve the same underlying 3D subject. We present OmniShowcase, built on two components: (i) a fully automated data synthesis pipeline that produces training videos along diverse camera trajectories, with viewpoint variation driven by underlying subject geometry, even spanning full 360-degree orbits; and (ii) a geometry-conditioning method that provides the video model with a turntable of surface-normal maps reconstructed from the reference views without requiring temporal alignment with the generated frames. We construct Showcase-4K, a corpus of 3D-asset-based videos covering twelve camera motions across 88 trajectory configurations with reconstruction-derived surface-normal conditioning, and ShowcaseBench, a held-out evaluation set with the source meshes and camera parameters used to render each video. Experiments show state-of-the-art subject identity and 3D consistency while maintaining competitive video quality and motion. Project page: justin4ai.github.io/OmniShowcase

</details>

## 7. Beyond Distribution Matching: Self-Supervised Representation Forcing for Few-Step Video Generation

**中文标题**: Beyond Distribution Matching: Self-Supervised Representation Forcing for Few-Step Video Generation

**作者**: Junyi Chen, Haiyu Zhang, Zhoujie Fu, Tengfei Wang, Chunchao Guo

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35594v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35594v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35594v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Few-step distillation is essential for deploying modern video diffusion models. Among existing approaches, Distribution Matching Distillation (DMD) has emerged as a highly promising paradigm by achieving strong output-space fidelity without adversarial training, while remaining flexible across various student architectures. We observe, however, that DMD fundamentally relies on an output-space objective, lacking explicit regularization on the model's internal representations. As the distillation step count aggressively shrinks, this output-only supervision becomes insufficient, causing the distilled student to degrade in both fine-grained spatial structures and coherent temporal dynamics. To address this limitation, we introduce Self Flow Forcing (SF$^2$), which incorporates a predictive representation loss into the DMD framework without altering its output objective. By providing the student and an Exponential Moving Average (EMA) teacher with heterogeneous noise levels, we establish an information asymmetry. This formulation forces the student to predict the teacher's internal representations from a more corrupted input, effectively integrating self-supervised representation alignment into distribution matching. Extensive experiments demonstrate that SF$^2$ effectively mitigates temporal flickering and consistently improves both spatial fidelity and motion quality. Furthermore, the proposed mechanism is architecture-agnostic - seamlessly supporting both bidirectional and causal models - and requires no external feature extractors, introducing minimal computational overhead.

</details>

## 8. EARS: Evidence-Aware Retrieval via Sound and Motion for Budgeted Video Reasoning

**中文标题**: EARS: Evidence-Aware Retrieval via Sound and Motion for Budgeted Video Reasoning

**作者**: Shih-Yao Lin

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35536v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35536v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35536v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/mikeslin-ir/EARS

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Uniform frame sampling can miss brief evidence in sparse-event videos when the VLM sees only K frames. We propose EARS (Evidence-Aware Retrieval via Sound and motion), a training-free, query-free front-end that ranks frames using optical-flow motion and audio energy, then applies temporal non-maximum suppression for diversity. The evidence score alone has frame-level AP 0.39; used only to select frames for the VLM, EARS with temporal diversity reaches localization AP 0.94, compared with 0.88 under Uniform sampling. In the updated matched study, at K = 1 EARS reaches XD-subset F1 0.660 versus 0.614 for centered Uniform (the paired interval includes zero); earlier backbone studies use separate protocols. On mostly-silent UCF-Crime, however, EARS slightly trails Uniform. A single configuration is used across both benchmarks; the new controls do not repeat the original held-out search. EARS therefore targets low-budget videos whose salient evidence aligns with motion or audio; on dense narratives or stealthy events, uniform or semantic selection remains preferable, and cheap modality statistics cannot reliably route between them. Code: https://github.com/mikeslin-ir/EARS

</details>

## 9. Learning High-Quality Pseudo-Labels for Point-Supervised Temporal Action Localization

**中文标题**: Learning High-Quality Pseudo-Labels for Point-Supervised Temporal Action Localization

**作者**: Zhenying Fang

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35516v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35516v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35516v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/zhenyingfang/LHQP

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Point-supervised temporal action localization (PTAL) aims to recognize and localize action instances in untrimmed videos using only point-level annotations. Existing pseudo-label-based methods have made substantial progress in PTAL. However, confidence-based initialization can overlook well-localized proposals. Meanwhile, existing methods either keep pseudo-labels fixed during detector training or update them without fully exploiting alternative boundary evidence from the original proposal pool. These limitations constrain supervision quality, thereby hindering detection performance. To learn high-quality pseudo-labels, we propose a framework that integrates prior-guided initialization with consensus-guided refinement during detector training. Our framework comprises two modules: Prior-Guided Initialization (PGI) and Consensus-Guided Refinement (CGR). PGI combines proposal confidence with point-derived priors to establish instance-specific reliability for initial pseudo-label selection and candidate retention. CGR continually uses the retained original candidates to construct boundary targets, combining their consensus with evolving detector predictions to refine pseudo-labels throughout training. Experiments on four benchmarks demonstrate that our method achieves competitive detection performance compared with existing methods. In particular, our method achieves 68.5\% average mAP over tIoU thresholds 0.1--0.7 on THUMOS14, surpassing PseR by 3.7 percentage points. Code is available at https://github.com/zhenyingfang/LHQP

</details>

## 10. Omni-InteractScope: Benchmarking Proactive and Agentic Interaction in Continuous Omnimodal Streams

**中文标题**: Omni-InteractScope: Benchmarking Proactive and Agentic Interaction in Continuous Omnimodal Streams

**作者**: Yinsong Yan, Ling Wang, Yuxuan Wang, Yifan Yang, Ruiyang Xu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35439v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35439v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35439v1)

**类别**: cs.AI, cs.CV, cs.HC

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Omnimodal large language models (Omni-LLMs) are increasingly expected to assist users while observing continuous audiovisual streams. However, existing evaluations provide limited insight into how reliably these models connect stream evidence and user needs to timely, sustained interventions. We introduce \textbf{Omni-InteractScope}, a benchmark of 1,479 human-annotated, cross-reviewed interactions with 1,790 temporal targets across eight task groups. It links input modalities, event triggers, intervention types, and user needs, covering event-timed tool invocation and query-free assistance alongside notifications, warnings, state tracking, and continuous responses. Our experimental results on the proposed benchmark reveal three critical limitations of current Omni-LLMs: (1) \textbf{Timely tool invocation remains unreliable}: all 12 evaluated models produce an on-time first response for at most 40\% of tool-invocation targets, and joint timing-and-tool accuracy reaches at most 32.0\%. (2) \textbf{Query-free reminders remain poorly covered}: all evaluated models achieve lower timely reference-target recall on query-free reminders than on explicit questions, measured on their respective target sets. Reminder recall never exceeds 23.0\%. (3) \textbf{End-to-end guidance coverage remains limited}: nine models cover none of the 26 guidance sequences in full, and the remaining three cover only one to three sequences each. Targeted controls further show that tool-invocation scores alone do not establish event grounding and that reminder coverage depends on response history and frequency. These findings motivate developing and evaluating streaming assistants for timely action, initiative under general user needs, and coverage of entire response sequences.

</details>

## 11. Joint Latent Reasoning and Observation for Agentic Video Understanding

**作者**: Junzhang Liu, Hao Yuan, Qi Zhang, Yiming Wu, Long Chen et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35229v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35229v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Effective video agents must learn not only how to answer, but also where to look next. Transferring this capability to smaller models is challenging because each observation serves two purposes: supporting an answer and guiding the search for further evidence. Textual descriptions may capture what is currently visible while omitting cues needed for subsequent acquisition. We introduce Latent Agentic Reasoning for Video Analysis (LARVA), a framework that distills multi-round video agents through jointly learned latent reasoning and visual observations. Rather than communicating through captions, an observer passes compact continuous representations to a reasoner that selects the next acquisition action or produces the final answer. Crucially, action and answer supervision propagate through this interface, training the observer to communicate information useful for downstream decisions. Observation reconstruction and reasoning-distribution alignment provide auxiliary supervision from textual teacher trajectories. Across five video-understanding benchmarks, LARVA achieves strong performance with 4B and 7B backbones. On VideoHolmes, Video-MME, and LVBench, the 7B model outperforms a textual CoT student trained on the same teacher trajectories by 5.1, 2.5, and 3.7 percentage points, respectively. These results highlight the value of jointly learning how agents represent evidence and act on it when distilling video-understanding capabilities.

## 12. GaussianSpline: Adaptive Bézier Splines for Highly Dynamic 4D Gaussian Splatting

**中文标题**: GaussianSpline: Adaptive Bézier Splines for Highly Dynamic 4D Gaussian Splatting

**作者**: Tom Yachil, Andrei Sharf

**发布日期**: 2026-10-26

**arXiv ID**: [2610.35224v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35224v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35224v1)

**类别**: cs.GR, cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

We present GaussianSpline, a dynamic 4D Gaussian Splatting method that models per-Gaussian motion using adaptive, non-uniform piecewise cubic Bézier splines. Unlike approaches based on global deformations, fixed-order polynomials, or uniformly parameterized splines, GaussianSpline jointly learns the Bézier control points and the temporal partition of each Gaussian's trajectory. This adaptive representation allocates greater temporal capacity to highly dynamic regions while maintaining compact trajectories for near-static regions, enabling high-fidelity reconstruction of complex and rapidly changing motion. To jointly achieve globally smooth motion and local adaptivity, we enforce $G^1$ continuity between consecutive Bézier segments using a reflection-based constraint on their control points. This produces smooth, fully differentiable trajectories while preserving the flexibility of non-uniform temporal parameterization. Experiments on the Neural 3D Video benchmark, the Google Immersive dataset, as well as on synthetic scenes demonstrate that GaussianSpline substantially improves reconstruction quality in scenes with highly non-uniform and challenging dynamics, while maintaining competitive performance on general dynamic scenes compared with state-of-the-art 4D Gaussian Splatting methods.

<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Recent advances in generative video models have improved visual realism, making detection increasingly dependent on subtle temporal inconsistencies. However, these signals are weak and spatially localized, while most of the video content is redundant. This creates a fundamental mismatch: existing detectors rely on heavy backbones to model full videos, which leads to high computational cost and limited scalability. In this work, we rethink AI-generated video detection from a representation perspective. Instead of modeling entire videos, we restructure them into compact temporal slice representations that isolate informative temporal evolution. <summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Infrared thermal wakes, generated by hydrodynamic displacement that modulates the cold-skin layer, offer a non-acoustic, non-magnetic alternative even without cooling-water discharge. <summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Diffeomorphisms provide a flexible, topology-preserving mathematical tool for modeling complex spatial or temporal deformations and are used in various scientific fields. However, making these deformations simultaneously highly expressive and computationally efficient remains challenging. Continuous Piecewise-Affine Based (CPAB) transformations (Freifeld et al., ICCV 2015; IEEE TPAMI 2017) offer an attractive solution by parameterizing a family of diffeomorphisms via continuous velocity fields that are piecewise affine with respect to a chosen tessellation of the domain. Importantly, $d=\dim(θ)$, the dimension of the parameter vector $θ$, depends on the fineness of the tessellation rather than on the data resolution. Despite this compact parameterization, CPAB optimization has long been hindered by the tight coupling between trajectory integration and gradient computation with respect to $θ$. We introduce FG-CPAB, a factorized-gradient formulation for scalable CPAB transformations. By separating integration from parameter-space projection, our method reduces gradient computation complexity from $\mathcal{O}(dTN)$ to $\mathcal{O}(TN+dC)$, where $T$ is the number of integration steps, $N$ is the number of transformed points, and $C$ is the number of tessellation cells. This reformulation yields speedups of up to four orders of magnitude (e.g., $\approx 10^4\times$ in 2D), substantial memory savings (e.g., $\approx 500\times$ reduction in peak GPU memory usage in 2D), and dramatically higher expressiveness. This makes fine-tessellation CPAB practical in 2D and 3D for the first time, enabling highly expressive parametric diffeomorphisms.

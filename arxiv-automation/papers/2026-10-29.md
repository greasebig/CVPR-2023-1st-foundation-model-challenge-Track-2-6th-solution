## 1. Repurposing Video Diffusion Transformers for Cross-View Temporal Object Correspondence

**中文标题**: Repurposing Video Diffusion Transformers for Cross-View Temporal Object Correspondence

**作者**: Siyoon Jin, Dahyun Chung, Honggyu An, Sangbeom Lim, Wonjin Nam et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-27

**arXiv ID**: [2610.37633v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37633v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37633v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Cross-view object correspondence identifies the same object across views. However, existing methods often rely on frame-wise matching or sequential tracking, which can propagate correspondence errors under appearance changes or occlusions. Therefore, we introduce cross-view temporal object correspondence, which requires preserving object identity across both views and time from a query mask. To address this, we propose XTrack, a unified framework which repurposes a pretrained video DiT with semantic and spatiotemporal priors for RGB-to-mask latent transport, jointly modeling cross-view matching and temporal tracking. However, naive video DiTs do not guarantee accurate cross-view grounding and often attend to semantically similar yet incorrect objects across views. Thus, we introduce focal cross-view attention alignment, hard negative conditioning, and local context crop strategies to ensure reliable grounding. Finally, beyond these architectural enhancements, we also tackle the lack of dense annotation by introducing a pseudo-label curation pipeline that converts sparse Ego-Exo4D annotations into dense supervision to enable effective video-level training. Our results demonstrate the effectiveness of XTrack as a unified framework, ensuring robust temporal consistency and identity preservation.

</details>

## 2. Perceiving and Predicting Dynamic Sports: Spatiotemporal Intelligence in Vision Models for Egocentric Tennis Video

**中文标题**: Perceiving and Predicting Dynamic Sports: Spatiotemporal Intelligence in Vision Models for Egocentric Tennis Video

**作者**: Tudor Braicu, Joseph DelPreto, Jared Boyer, Mónika Farsang, Maximilian Stölzle et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-27

**arXiv ID**: [2610.37619v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37619v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37619v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.RO

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Agile robots require spatiotemporal intelligence to interpret scenes, reason about interactions, and anticipate future events. Yet it remains unclear how well foundation vision models support these capabilities, particularly from a moving egocentric camera, which is a natural view for robots. To investigate this, we collect an annotated tennis dataset and train a suite of vision models on spatiotemporal tasks. Our dataset contains egocentric video synchronized with five allocentric videos, estimated global positions, labeled contact times, and annotated stroke types. We then evaluate seven prediction tasks from egocentric images: 2D ego player and 3D ball localization, future player and ball trajectories, forehand or backhand stroke anticipation, time to the next ego player ball contact, and 3D ball position at that contact. We compare image encoders, video encoders, and vision-language models with jointly trained prediction heads. The best performing models achieve mean current-frame localization errors of 0.14 m for the ego player and 0.54 m for the ball, future forecasting errors of 0.34 m for the ego player and 0.91 m for the ball over 1.1 s, and a mean contact-time prediction error of 0.14 s. Auxiliary depth information substantially improves 3D ball localization, suggesting that inferring metric depth remains a challenge. Longer image histories do not improve ball forecasting, indicating that representations exploit scene context rather than rely solely on inter-frame motion. Finally, we propose a conditional diffusion model to generate whole-body kinematic tennis motion from vision encoder predictions on egocentric video at real-time rates, adapting movements as predictions are refined. These results move towards a deeper understanding of how foundation vision models can support real-time dynamic robots using egocentric video.

</details>

## 3. VideoLVR: Latent Visual Reasoning over Grounded Video Evidence

**中文标题**: VideoLVR: Latent Visual Reasoning over Grounded Video Evidence

**作者**: Shilong Li, Xingyuan Bu, Hao Shi, Yun Xing, Weixin Feng et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-27

**arXiv ID**: [2610.37613v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37613v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37613v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Chain-of-thought reasoning has substantially advanced video understanding in multimodal large language models (MLLMs). However, language-based reasoning traces do not provide a reliable mechanism for models to revisit relevant visual evidence, leading to visual forgetting and impairing their ability to reason over video content. Existing approaches maintain visual grounding during reasoning in two main ways: they either refer to evidence using textual coordinates, which specify symbolic locations rather than the visual content itself, or reinsert cropped regions or frames into the multimodal context, preserving visual details at the cost of substantial context expansion. To address these limitations, we introduce VideoLVR, which constructs latent visual evidence from local regions at relevant video moments, thereby providing visual support for intermediate reasoning steps. To supervise latent visual evidence generation, we introduce a visual token prediction objective that formulates visual token reconstruction as prediction over a vocabulary derived from the input video, thereby unifying visual evidence reconstruction and text generation within the same token prediction framework. This shared prediction framework enables MLLMs to reason over interleaved text tokens and latent visual states while integrating evidence from different moments. We further extend VideoLVR to reinforcement learning by treating sampled visual tokens as actions alongside text tokens and jointly optimizing the policy using final-answer rewards. By efficiently revisiting task-relevant visual information during reasoning, VideoLVR improves video understanding without substantially expanding the multimodal context. Experiments on multiple video benchmarks demonstrate its effectiveness, especially on tasks requiring fine-grained visual evidence.

</details>

## 4. Zero-Shot Multimodal Event Spotting in Soccer Broadcasts

**中文标题**: Zero-Shot Multimodal Event Spotting in Soccer Broadcasts

**作者**: Sadia Kamal, Mahmudul Hasan, Tony Braskich

**发布日期**: 2026-10-27

**arXiv ID**: [2610.37583v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37583v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37583v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.LG, cs.MM

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Automatic soccer event spotting supports highlight generation, video indexing, and sports analytics, but existing methods depend on large annotated datasets and soccer-specific training. We present a zero-shot framework that detects, classifies, and temporally localizes events in full-length soccer broadcasts without task-specific parameter training. Each match is segmented into overlapping clips represented by a compact visual storyboard and the aligned commentary transcript, which a general-purpose multimodal model processes jointly. On three manually annotated matches containing 130 events, the best configuration achieves 72.0% F1@5s and outperforms SoccerNet-trained CALF and NetVLAD++ in a cross-dataset setting, while the storyboard representation reduces inference cost by 72% compared with individual frames. We further analyze the effects of model family, input representation, and prompt optimization, and introduce an evidence-grounded excitement-scoring stage that ranks detected events with natural-language explanations for highlight selection.

## 5. As the Story Unfolds: Watching a Film and Identifying Characters as a Human Does

**中文标题**: As the Story Unfolds: Watching a Film and Identifying Characters as a Human Does

**作者**: Zhongrui Gui, Junyu Xie, Tengda Han, Weidi Xie, Andrew Zisserman

**发布日期**: 2026-10-27

**arXiv ID**: [2610.37579v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37579v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37579v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Films introduce characters incrementally as they progress. We study the problem of identifying principal characters and assigning their names directly from the movie itself, without using external cast lists, actor photographs, or pre-built character banks. We introduce the character naming task: given a name mentioned in dialogue and the associated video context, the model must determine which visible character bears that name. This task is challenging because name resolution often depends on cinematic cues such as shot-reverse-shot structure, self-introduction, direct address, third-party reference, gaze, and temporal continuity. We provide a dataset and annotations for evaluating character naming and first character appearances, develop a video-and-dialogue model for the task, and compare it with proprietary multimodal large language models. We further introduce a progressive framework for building a character bank as the film unfolds. Together, these contributions support online, human-like movie understanding in which character identities are inferred from the film itself.

</details>

## 6. ReferStream: Interactive Subject Injection for Streaming Video Generation

**中文标题**: ReferStream: Interactive Subject Injection for Streaming Video Generation

**作者**: Shuolin Xu, Guanyuan Li, Boyu Li, Kelu Yao, Xiaogang Xu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-27

**arXiv ID**: [2610.37566v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37566v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37566v1)

**项目主页**: https://xushuolin.github.io/ReferStream/.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Injecting new subjects naturally during streaming video generation requires integrating newly supplied visual references into the existing video while preserving established subjects and the scene. Encoding references independently of the current scene provides little guidance for this integration, while keeping their features in persistent memory can interfere with established context. We propose ReferStream, a framework that requires no additional training and combines reference construction using the current scene with transient cache injection. Joint Latent places reference latents in target regions of the latest scene latent, allowing reference features to be extracted in context. Cache Restoration uses these features to condition the injection chunk, then restores the overwritten global cache entries while retaining the newly generated history. This procedure incorporates new visual conditions without retraining the generator or revising emitted frames. We further introduce ReferStream Bench, a new benchmark with scheduled reference arrivals, evolving instructions, and explicit annotations of subject presence for InShot and MultiShot generation. Evaluation follows the event schedule and measures both the fidelity of new subjects and the preservation of established subjects, alongside video quality, text adherence, and count accuracy. Experiments on ReferStream Bench show higher scores for text adherence, subject similarity, and count accuracy than the compared baselines, including offline methods given all references in advance. Project page: https://xushuolin.github.io/ReferStream/.

</details>

## 7. GroundControl: Satellite-Conditioned Multi-View Diffusion for Reconstruction-Free Novel View Synthesis

**中文标题**: GroundControl: Satellite-Conditioned Multi-View Diffusion for Reconstruction-Free Novel View Synthesis

**作者**: Akshay Krishnan, Matias Turkulainen, Jamie Wynn, Zawar Qureshi, Mohamed Sayed et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-27

**arXiv ID**: [2610.37545v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37545v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37545v1)

**项目主页**: https://nianticspatial.github.io/groundcontrol

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Multi-view or video diffusion models have recently emerged as state-of-the-art approaches for novel-view synthesis from sparse image collections. While they can produce high quality images, they tend to hallucinate unrealistic spatial layouts when synthesizing views outside the input views (scene extrapolation). We propose to address this challenge for outdoor scenes by using satellite images as an additional information source to condition the multi-view generative model. We introduce a new satellite-conditioned multi-view diffusion model that combines conditioning from a satellite image and street-level input images when generating novel-views. Our cross-attention based conditioning allows us to retain pretrained multi-view diffusion priors to generate seamless, realistic, novel views from a handful of source views, while also adhering to the scene layout as seen from satellite images. Our model does not use 3D scene reconstruction, and only relies on posed street and satellite views. Our experiments on outdoor datasets show that our model is better than both satellite-conditioned or street-view conditioned baselines on novel-view synthesis, especially on challenging cases where target views have very little overlap with source views. Project page: https://nianticspatial.github.io/groundcontrol

</details>

## 8. PDRVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Model with Physical Dynamics Representations

**中文标题**: PDRVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Model with Physical Dynamics Representations

**作者**: Hanqing Wang, Zhaotian Cai, Ruichuan An, Bohan Zeng, Tao Zhang et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-27

**arXiv ID**: [2610.37381v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37381v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37381v1)

**类别**: cs.RO, cs.CV, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Vision--Language--Action (VLA) models generate robot actions from visual observations and language instructions, but their understanding of physical dynamics remains limited. Video world models can capture scene dynamics, yet generating future videos introduces high latency into the control loop. We introduce PDRVLA, whose lightweight Physical Dynamics Module extracts physical tokens from vision--language features to condition action generation without online video generation. The module learns through direct prediction of future RGB, depth, and optical-flow features, with auxiliary supervision through a frozen pretrained video world model. Training relies entirely on publicly available data, expanded through human-to-robot synthesis, subtask annotation, and embodied chain-of-thought (ECoT) annotation. Across four simulation benchmarks, PDRVLA achieves success rates of 99.3% on LIBERO, 89.9% on LIBERO-Plus, 93.6% on RoboTwin~2.0, and 81.5% on SimplerEnv. Ablations show improved future prediction with world-model supervision and evaluate how subtask conditioning, future object-pose supervision, and ECoT training affect action success.

## 9. TimeLineXR: An Interactive Exploration of Time in Vision-Language Models

**中文标题**: TimeLineXR: An Interactive Exploration of Time in Vision-Language Models

**作者**: Nidham Tekaya, Markus Passecker, Matthias Zeppelzauer

**发布日期**: 2026-10-27

**arXiv ID**: [2610.37349v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37349v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37349v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/sterngefeuert/timeline-xr.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Recently, it has been shown that vision-language models (VLMs) have a certain intrinsic understanding of time. They can estimate the temporal origin of objects and encode time along a low-dimensional, non-linear manifold inside their embedding space~\cite{tekaya2025matter}. That result is quantitative and reproducible, but the structure itself stays out of reach for end users: they cannot see it, query it with their own images, or inspect where a query lands relative to other years on the time axis. We present TimeLineXR, an interactive demonstration that turns this time axis into an explorable 3D scene. A web demo lets users upload or select images and see each placed on a color-coded 3D time axis; a Three.js WebXR scene renders the same time axis as a room-scale representation that can be grabbed, scaled, inspected, and queried by throwing image cards into it. Both clients run the same prediction pipeline against the same cached temporal index: encode the query with CLIP, project it through cosine-kernel KPCA, and snap it to the Bézier time axis. The system turns each image into a point on the time axis, so users can compare where different objects land and read off their estimated first year of appearance.Code source and reproduction steps can be found in: https://github.com/sterngefeuert/timeline-xr.

</details>

## 10. CodeCap-Bench: Can Code Serve as a Good Video Caption?

**中文标题**: CodeCap-Bench: Can Code Serve as a Good Video Caption?

**作者**: Jiafu Tang, Yifan Xu, Eric Tang, Junjie Zhao, Shipeng Luo et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-27

**arXiv ID**: [2610.37341v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37341v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37341v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video captioning aims to preserve visual information through compact descriptions, predominantly using natural language. However, precisely expressing fine-grained spatial structures and temporal dynamics in natural language often leads to lengthy and potentially ambiguous descriptions. In this work, we explore Code as Video Caption, a paradigm where executable programs serve as a complementary representation of visual content through graphical primitives, layout rules, and functions of time. To examine whether code captions effectively preserve video information, we compare them with natural language captions on downstream question answering and video reconstruction tasks. Our results show that code captions achieve superior reasoning accuracy and reconstruction fidelity. Despite these advantages, realizing the potential of code captioning depends on whether current multimodal large language models (MLLMs) can generate faithful code captions. To systematically assess this capability, we introduce CodeCap-Bench, which comprises 323 videos spanning five categories of structured 2D content, along with fine-grained annotations and a multi-level evaluation framework. Extensive experiments reveal that while leading proprietary MLLMs demonstrate promising code captioning capabilities, they still struggle to faithfully capture complex temporal dynamics, with open-source models lagging substantially behind.

</details>

## 11. Hierarchical Sparse Graph for Multi-dimensional Assignment in Event-based Tracking

**中文标题**: Hierarchical Sparse Graph for Multi-dimensional Assignment in Event-based Tracking

**作者**: Muxi Zha, Yang Shang, Wenlong Zhang, Qifeng Yu, Banglei Guan

**发布日期**: 2026-10-27

**arXiv ID**: [2610.37321v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37321v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37321v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Existing event-based tracking methods typically operate in an online manner, associating tracklets across adjacent batches. However, this limited temporal window often leads to failures in challenging scenarios, such as long-term occlusions or missed detections, where broader temporal context should be incorporated for high-precision tracking. To achieve robust and accurate tracking, this paper concentrates on the event-based multi-dimensional assignment that associates tracklets across multiple batches simultaneously. Unlike image-based detectors, event-based detectors can generate multiple correct yet spatially distinct tracklets for a single object per batch. This violates the one-to-one rule in classic data association, making event-based association an underdetermined many-to-many matching problem. **作者**: Min Zeng, Xin Wen, Xi Chen, Yichen Zhang, Wen-Xin Zhou

## 1. EgoExplorer: A Vision-Motion-Touch Observatory for Human Loco-Manipulation In Situ

**中文标题**: EgoExplorer: A Vision-Motion-Touch Observatory for Human Loco-Manipulation In Situ

**作者**: Yunsong Zhou, Xuekun Jiang, Haiqi Shen, Hangxu Liu, Chaojian Shi et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-13

**arXiv ID**: [2610.18872v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18872v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18872v1)

**类别**: cs.RO, cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Learning humanoid loco-manipulation from human demonstrations rests on observing how locomotion, posture, and object interaction evolve together as tasks unfold. Scaling collection across diverse environments, in turn, favors lightweight, portable capture systems. Yet, existing datasets provide only partial views of this coordination. Thus, we introduce EgoExplorer, a 100-hour vision-motion-touch observatory of human loco-manipulation built with a wearable system. With an emphasis on professional activities, its continuous recordings align egocentric video with reconstructed whole-body motion, fine-grained finger articulation, and tactile observations, accompanied by step-level descriptions. To achieve this coverage with sparse body sensing, we develop the Calibrate-Complete-Constrain pipeline. Concretely, reusable calibration fixes participant geometry and sensor attachments before motion inference to disentangle mounting offsets from articulation. A temporal prior then completes unobserved joint motion, while layered constraints promote measurement agreement, temporal continuity, and geometric consistency, properties that motion plausibility alone does not guarantee. Against tracker-fitted references, the method reduces body-point error by 71.9% relative to the strongest baseline; matched evaluations also show gains in hand stability and contact estimation. Preliminary studies explore multimodal generation and conditioning alongside humanoid retargeting with whole-body control in simulation.

</details>

## 2. Visual Tokens on Demand: Visual Budgeting for Scalable and Efficient Multi-Image Reasoning in Multimodal LLMs

**中文标题**: Visual Tokens on Demand: Visual Budgeting for Scalable and Efficient Multi-Image Reasoning in Multimodal LLMs

**作者**: Aimon Rahman, Joymallya Chakraborty, Vidit Bansal, Abhishek Dan

**发布日期**: 2026-10-13

**arXiv ID**: [2610.18858v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18858v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18858v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Multimodal large language models have advanced rapidly, but most remain optimized for single-image inputs. Multi-image reasoning is costly because each image adds many visual tokens, increasing encoding and attention demands. Existing techniques that address the above issue, including visual token pruning, token merging, visual token compression, learned resampling, hierarchical compression, retrieval-augmented visual reasoning, and memory-based architectures, have largely been designed for single-image scenarios, where redundancy is primarily spatial and local. Multi-image reasoning introduces additional challenges: redundancy may occur across images, task-relevant evidence may be sparse or distributed, and aggressive per-image compression can remove details needed for comparison, counting, temporal reasoning, retrieval, or evidence grounding. Moreover, existing token-reduction methods are often either (i) training-free but inference-inefficient, typically relying on accessing attention matrices to get token-importance that may be impractical to access with memory-efficient attention implementations such as FlashAttention, or (ii) training-dependent, requiring the base MLLM to be retrained or adapted to a new compressed-token paradigm. In this work, we study scalable multi-image reasoning and understanding without retraining the base model and without accessing attention matrices at inference time. To this end, we propose mmVB, a lightweight plug-and-play module that assigns query-conditioned per-image token budgets via cross-image context, adding only 1.6M trainable parameters while reducing visual input tokens by 9x. This reduction yields 5x faster time-to-first-token and 7x fewer prefill FLOPs, while maintaining 94.4% of full-resolution accuracy across eight multi-image reasoning benchmarks.

</details>

## 3. RepProbe: Can Video Language Models Count Repeated Actions? A Diagnostic Benchmark

**中文标题**: RepProbe: Can Video Language Models Count Repeated Actions? A Diagnostic Benchmark

**作者**: Mahsa Khoshnoodi, Sarah Adel Bargal

**发布日期**: 2026-10-13

**arXiv ID**: [2610.18844v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18844v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18844v1)

**项目主页**: https://arenaa.github.io/RepProbe-webpage/

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Repetition counting requires identifying and enumerating action cycles over time. While state-of-the-art video language models describe actions fluently, they fail to reliably count how many times an action repeats. To investigate this limitation, we introduce RepProbe, a benchmark of 42K real and synthetic video clips containing exactly k in [1, 20] complete repetitions, with controls for clip position, temporal coverage, and duration. These controls allow us to study the counting failure directly and locate where in the model it arises. Counting accuracy collapses at high counts across the video language models we evaluate, and controlled experiments show this collapse is not explained by frame sampling alone. We compare model outputs with linear probes trained on the final-layer representations used to generate those outputs. We find a consistent representation-to-output dissociation: substantial count information remains linearly decodable even when models fail to report the correct count. Further analysis shows this failure arises primarily at the output stage, where answers concentrate on a small set of preferred numbers rather than the available count information. The dissociation holds on both real and synthetic videos and is not explained by clip duration, position, or scene content. These results indicate that models encode repetition structure but do not reliably report it in their outputs. Project page: https://arenaa.github.io/RepProbe-webpage/

</details>

## 4. Plan-Bench: Can Existing VLMs Go Well Beyond Perception to Reason and Plan in Embodied Worlds?

**中文标题**: Plan-Bench: Can Existing VLMs Go Well Beyond Perception to Reason and Plan in Embodied Worlds?

**作者**: Juntong Feng, Qingan Liu, Jingru Dong, Yue Zhu, Weiming Zheng et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-13

**arXiv ID**: [2610.18836v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18836v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18836v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/MinneFeng/Plan-Bench.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have achieved strong performance in visual perception and spatial reasoning, leading to growing interest in using them as high-level planners for embodied AI. However, physical tasks require models to integrate spatial information, reason over temporal states, and generate multi-step plans under various constraints, raising the question of whether current VLMs can reliably support such reasoning and planning independently of execution. We introduce Plan-Bench, a benchmark for evaluating the high-level reasoning and multi-step planning capabilities of VLMs relevant to embodied worlds, while isolating the influence of action execution. It covers three complementary dimensions, spanning 16 subclasses and 699 curated samples. Extensive evaluation of 28 VLMs reveals room for improvement, with even the strongest model achieving only 75.0% accuracy. We further find that model scaling generally improves these capabilities. Ablation studies also indicate larger models show increasing potential as high-level planners. Overall, Plan-Bench provides a lightweight probe for evaluating VLMs' reasoning and planning capabilities relevant to embodied environments and assessing their potential as decision makers for physical tasks. Our benchmark and code are available at https://github.com/MinneFeng/Plan-Bench.

</details>

## 5. OmniCompass: A Holistic Benchmark for Omni-Modal Video Grounding and Understanding

**中文标题**: OmniCompass: A Holistic Benchmark for Omni-Modal Video Grounding and Understanding

**作者**: Xuyang Liu, Shihao Wang, Xinyu Wei, Minghan Li, Zeqing Wang et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-13

**arXiv ID**: [2610.18829v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18829v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18829v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.CL, cs.MM

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

With the rapid development of multimodal large language models (MLLMs), evaluating their video understanding capabilities has become increasingly important. Existing video understanding benchmarks cover only part of the evaluation dimensions, limiting systematic diagnosis of omni-modal video understanding. We introduce OmniCompass, a holistic benchmark for omni-modal video grounding and understanding. OmniCompass defines two evaluation tracks: OmniCompass-VL assesses vision-language models on tasks requiring visual evidence, while OmniCompass-AV assesses omni-modal models on tasks requiring joint audio-visual evidence. Each track includes three complementary tasks: temporal grounding (TG), spatial grounding (SG), and question answering (QA), assessing when a referenced target appears, where it is, and what happens. QA covers five perception and three reasoning categories in OmniCompass-VL, and three perception and two reasoning categories in OmniCompass-AV. OmniCompass-VL contains 820 videos and 2,460 instances across domains and three duration types: short, medium, and long. A narrative- and audio-rich subset forms OmniCompass-AV with 240 videos and 720 instances. Each instance is evaluated under two paired settings: text-only and text+image, specifying the same target through text and visual-language guidance. All instances are manually annotated with multi-round quality verification. Evaluations of 50 MLLMs show that open-source models still lag behind proprietary counterparts, particularly in audio-visual understanding. Precise temporal and spatial grounding remains challenging even for leading models, and their predictions still exhibit inconsistencies across the paired target specifications.

</details>

## 6. GAD: Geometry Attention Distillation for Consistent Video Generation

**中文标题**: GAD: Geometry Attention Distillation for Consistent Video Generation

**作者**: Junfeng Ni, Ziyu Zhu, Fan Jiang, Yu Liu, Chiyu Wang et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-13

**arXiv ID**: [2610.18779v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18779v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18779v1)

**项目主页**: https://fantasy-amap.github.io/GAD/.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video diffusion models generate visually compelling short-term sequences but often struggle to preserve scene geometry and object structure over time. In this paper, we first demonstrate that current video attention cannot identify reliable correspondences across frames, with responses dispersed across multiple regions even after recognizable scene structure has emerged. We argue that reliable cross-frame associations are essential for information exchange as scene structure forms in video generation. Thus, we introduce Geometry Attention Distillation (GAD), which transfers correspondences from a frozen geometry model's attention to video attention. Extracted from clean videos, these correspondences link the same static surface or moving object across frames. Since structure formation begins in deeper layers at higher noise and shallower layers at lower noise, we apply attention distillation at a student layer selected according to the noise timestep. After training, video attention focuses more consistently on corresponding locations. Experiments across all three video backbones demonstrate improved long-range scene consistency and dynamic temporal consistency, with geometric gains across generation lengths. On Wan2.2 5B, GAD improves cross-frame matching accuracy by at least 30% over the Wan baseline on both long-range benchmarks. Notably, GAD achieves these gains without additional inference modules, offering a promising approach to efficient and consistent video-based world simulation. Project page: https://fantasy-amap.github.io/GAD/.

</details>

## 7. ST-GRU PredRNN-V2: A GRU-Based Reformulation of PredRNN-V2 for Video Prediction

**中文标题**: ST-GRU PredRNN-V2: A GRU-Based Reformulation of PredRNN-V2 for Video Prediction

**作者**: Augusta van Haren, Hanna Hoogen, Luca Pattavina, Quincy Rommen

**发布日期**: 2026-10-13

**arXiv ID**: [2610.18645v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18645v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18645v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video prediction requires models to capture complex spatiotemporal dependencies and to retain relevant information over long sequences. PredRNN and its successor PredRNN-V2 address this challenge with spatiotemporal Long Short-Term Memory (ST-LSTM) units that maintain two separate memory states, at the cost of a relatively complex recurrent building block. In this work, we investigate whether the simpler gating of Gated Recurrent Units (GRUs) can be transferred to spatiotemporal predictive learning. To this end, we develop a spatiotemporal GRU (ST-GRU) unit as a GRU-based alternative to the ST-LSTM. The ST-GRU replaces the ST-LSTM's three gates per memory state with two GRU-style gates (update and reset), and merges the temporal memory state into the hidden state, which reduces the number of parameters by about 5%. We incorporate the ST-GRU into PredRNN-V2 and evaluate the resulting model on the Moving MNIST video prediction benchmark, following the original experimental protocol. Averaged over ten predicted frames, the ST-GRU-based PredRNN-V2 achieves an MSE of 55.1, an SSIM of 0.883, an LPIPS of 0.065 and a PSNR of 19.53dB. It does not surpass the original ST-LSTM-based PredRNN-V2 on MSE, SSIM or PSNR, but it achieves a lower LPIPS and performs on par with the earlier ST-LSTM-based PredRNN, while using a simpler recurrent cell. These results suggest that ST-GRUs are a promising building block for spatiotemporal predictive learning and motivate further work on their design and integration.

</details>

## 8. MajorTOM:Elliot Pretrain, A Multi-Sensor, Multi-Temporal Pretraining Dataset for Earth Observation

**中文标题**: MajorTOM:Elliot Pretrain, A Multi-Sensor, Multi-Temporal Pretraining Dataset for Earth Observation

**作者**: Carmen Oliver Huidobro, Mikolaj Czerkawski, Kevin Monsalvez-Pozo, Cesar Aybar, Gustau Camps-Valls et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-13

**arXiv ID**: [2610.18624v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18624v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18624v1)

**项目主页**: https://source.coop/major-tom/elliot-pretrain.

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Pretraining generalist Earth observation foundation models requires data that combine several sensors, varied temporal sampling and, increasingly, text, yet open datasets rarely provide all three in one consistent, easily accessible form. We introduce MajorTOM:Elliot Pretrain, a collection of 279,166 tiles of 10 km drawn from the Major-TOM grid by clustering AlphaEarth embeddings. Each tile stacks Sentinel-2 L1C, Landsat-8/9, Sentinel-1 RTC, Copernicus DEM and ESA WorldCover on a shared 1056x1056-pixel, 10 m grid, complemented by cloud masks, OpenStreetMap entities and ERA5 weather. The tiles are partitioned into three temporal regimes, Monotemporal (250,000 tiles, one acquisition), Monthly (12,500 tiles, twelve acquisitions spread over the annual cycle) and Burst (16,666 tiles, six acquisitions within 30 days), and are released as 6.3 TB of Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs indexed by TACO v3 Parquet metadata, a standard that allows cloud streaming, efficient data access, and filtering before any pixel is read. A companion library derives sensor-tagged fact sheets, captions and 16 families of vision-language tasks from these layers at load time. We test our design choices in short pretraining runs of OlmoEarth-Base. First, a 25% subset of Major-TOM Core-S2L2A curated with AlphaEarth embeddings matches or exceeds the full corpus on 8 of 9 benchmarks. Second, on Elliot-Pretrain, adding Landsat-8/9 improves or matches Sentinel-2 only pretraining on every benchmark. Third, using DEM and WorldCover as prediction targets during pretraining further improves 8 of 9 benchmarks, while giving the same maps to the encoder as inputs degrades 5 of 9. Fourth, Sentinel-1 helps on 7 of 9 benchmarks, particularly on benchmarks with radar inputs. Fifth, rule-based captions match a small language model at no cost. The dataset is openly available at https://source.coop/major-tom/elliot-pretrain.

</details>

## 9. VideoJev: System One Model for Video Understanding and Interaction

**中文标题**: VideoJev: System One Model for Video Understanding and Interaction

**作者**: Yuhan Zhu, Xiangyu Zeng, Qingyi Si, Chenxu Yang, Yifei Li et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-13

**arXiv ID**: [2610.18615v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18615v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18615v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Visual agents must turn video observations into timely decisions to track progress and choose what to do next. Building on the emerging System One paradigm, we introduce VideoJev, a family of models that cast video understanding and interaction as structured decision tasks. We adapt pretrained multimodal large language models (MLLMs) by replacing autoregressive answer decoding with a shared head that scores temporal intervals and textual options against video evidence. These scores yield event spans or answer choices, unifying temporal grounding and multiple-choice video question answering. This combines the semantic flexibility of MLLMs with efficient decisions that agents can use directly. As an interaction unfolds, new goals and intermediate results prompt further queries about the same visual history. VideoJev therefore reuses visual computation through Cached inference for queries as they arrive and Joint inference for multiple queries in a single forward pass. Across three temporal grounding benchmarks, VideoJev-4B and VideoJev-35B-A3B improve mean mIoU over their backbones by 6.7 and 10.8 points, respectively, with measured inference speedups of $2.8$-$3.1\times$. On four video QA benchmarks, both achieve performance comparable to their backbones in Thinking mode, with measured inference speedups exceeding $100\times$. These results offer a unified, reusable decision interface for composing structured visual judgments into complex, open-ended workflows.

</details>

<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

World Action Models (WAMs), often built upon pretrained video-generation world models, have emerged as a promising framework for general-purpose and open-ended robot control. However, their real-world deployment often suffers from discontinuous and jerky execution. Existing methods typically address this issue by either improving inference efficiency or enhancing action continuity across prediction chunks, without jointly addressing both challenges. To bridge this gap, we propose StreamingWAM, a streaming-diffusion framework for WAM-based robot control. StreamingWAM combines a noise ladder for chunk-wise action prediction, a prefix rollout module for cross-chunk continuity, and dense video-history memory for temporally consistent conditioning. Experiments on LIBERO, LIBERO-Plus, RoboTwin 2.0, and real-world robotic tasks demonstrate that StreamingWAM improves action continuity across static and dynamic settings, achieving competitive performance while reducing steady-state replanning latency to 72.9 ms in simulation and 57.3 ms on the real robot. The method also produces smoother action transitions, while the streaming staircase reduces sensitivity to the execution horizon K.

## 1. Refrain: Learning Multi-Shot Consistency from Inconsistent References for A$^2$O Video Generation

**中文标题**: Refrain: Learning Multi-Shot Consistency from Inconsistent References for A$^2$O Video Generation

**作者**: Hongru Yan, Yue Wu, Jian-Wei Zhang, Liangbin Xie, Mingzhe Zheng et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-20

**arXiv ID**: [2611.27806v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27806v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27806v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

A comic-book character, a photorealistic cityscape, an oil-painted coffee cup -- such stylistically diverse references provide visual guidance across shots and frames, yet generating a coherent multi-shot video that unifies their visual styles while preserving subject identities and scene content remains challenging. Existing methods maintain cross-shot continuity through additional attention control or memory processing. However, they struggle to resolve disparate per-shot styles or handle reference-target duration mismatches within a single framework. We introduce Refrain, a unified model that learns cross-shot consistency through a self-supervised reconstruction task. Reference clips from the same original video are deliberately perturbed with distinct visual edits, and the model recovers the unedited source footage under a shared style prompt. The resulting pipeline yields DurMix-300K, which pairs independently edited references of varying durations with their original videos. Operating on the A$^2$O principle (Any Reference, Any Duration, One Timeline), Refrain maps image and video references onto a shared temporal canvas without auxiliary conditioning modules. For evaluation, experiments show that standard CLIP or DINO metrics frequently overlook stylistic clashes or misinterpret intended narrative cuts as inconsistency. We propose Shot-Debiased Consistency (SDC), which uses an extensive real-video bank to establish a baseline for style variation associated with content changes within shots. By discounting this baseline divergence, SDC evaluates cross-shot style shifts against real-world distributions, where smaller residual differences indicate higher stylistic continuity. Extensive experiments demonstrate that Refrain achieves state-of-the-art performance in cross-shot consistency, while excelling at shot timing, reference adherence, and text alignment.

</details>

## 2. Feasibility of Multifrequency Neural Network-Based Wave Inversion in Ultrasound Time-Harmonic Elastography

**中文标题**: Feasibility of Multifrequency Neural Network-Based Wave Inversion in Ultrasound Time-Harmonic Elastography

**作者**: Héloïse Bustin, Tom Meyer, Paul Spiesecke, Jakob Jordan, Anja Hennemuth et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-20

**arXiv ID**: [2611.27792v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27792v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27792v1)

**类别**: eess.IV, cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Ultrasound time-harmonic elastography (THE) is a non-invasive imaging technique that measures soft-tissue stiffness. Relying on the same principle of time-harmonic shear wave excitation as established magnetic resonance elastography (MRE), THE offers full field-of-view stiffness mapping at short acquisition times and sub-second temporal resolution across organs. However, reconstruction of shear wave speed maps from acquired tissue wavefields remains challenging due to speckle noise and sparse spatial coverage of the lateral component.   Deep learning methods have shown promising results for image reconstruction in transient-based ultrasound elastography modalities but remain unexplored in THE. In MRE, MF-ElastoNet, a 2D multifrequency neural network-based wave reconstruction method trained on synthetic physics-based wave data, has shown high generalizability across dynamic ranges and resolutions.   We investigated the potential of a multifrequency neural network-based wave inversion for THE, by performing a first feasibility analysis. To this end, we adapted the processing pipeline of MF-ElastoNet from MRE to THE. We tested our approach on THE acquired phantom data from the algorithms for mapping elastic modulus Challenge, as well as on in vivo liver THE in 4 healthy volunteers and 14 patients with focal liver lesions, including benign focal nodular hyperplasia, and malignant hepatocellular carcinoma and adenocarcinoma. MF-ElastoNet was successfully adapted to THE with in vivo liver testing. Compared with k-MDEV, the state-of-the-art wave inversion method for THE, Bland-Altman analysis showed a mean bias of -0.02 m/s (95% limits of agreement: -0.36 to 0.33 m/s).

</details>

## 3. MoHA: Model-Conditioned Harness Adaptation for Long-Video Agents

**中文标题**: MoHA: Model-Conditioned Harness Adaptation for Long-Video Agents

**作者**: Jianghan Chao, Wenhui Tan, Hang Su, Qiuyu Tian, Tanxiao Li et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-20

**arXiv ID**: [2611.27646v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27646v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27646v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/roverx12345/MoHA.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Long-video understanding increasingly relies on agentic systems that actively acquire evidence rather than process fixed video representations. Their execution harnesses must coordinate language-level planning with video-level observation and evidence acquisition, yet are typically fixed across model stacks. We therefore introduce MoHA, a framework for model-conditioned harness adaptation that adapts a common initial execution harness to the specific planner and observer models it hosts. MoHA treats the harness itself as a model-conditioned optimization object: under the same task distribution and initialization, different planner-observer stacks can benefit from different executable harnesses. Starting from a common initial harness, MoHA analyzes failed calibration traces to diagnose model-harness mismatches and adapts both planner-side reasoning support, such as overview, memory, and verification, and observer-side capability assignments, including OCR and ASR specialists. After structural adaptation, MoHA separately calibrates observer execution configurations for the hosted model stack. Across three frozen planner stacks, MoHA improves Video-MME Full accuracy by 6.2-27.1 percentage points and Video-Holmes accuracy by 3.1-20.1 points over the fixed initial harness. Cross-model transfer further shows that the adapted harnesses generalize only partially to other models, highlighting the harness as a model-conditioned optimization layer for long-video understanding. Code is available at https://github.com/roverx12345/MoHA.

</details>

## 4. Beyond Resolution: Inference-Time Parameterized Temporal Consistency in Generative Regional Climate Downscaling

**中文标题**: Beyond Resolution: Inference-Time Parameterized Temporal Consistency in Generative Regional Climate Downscaling

**作者**: Luca Glawion, Julius Polz, Harald Kunstmann, Benjamin Fersch, Patrick Ludwig et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-20

**arXiv ID**: [2611.27641v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27641v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27641v1)

**类别**: physics.ao-ph, cs.CV, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

GCM-to-RCM downscaling emulation is dominated by generative diffusion models, but most existing work evaluates skill only per timestep, treating each field as an independent snapshot. This leaves open whether generated sequences are temporally coherent or merely independently plausible, a gap that matters increasingly for sub-daily resolutions since downstream impacts depend on accumulation and persistence, not just on isolated states. This study asks whether autoregressive conditioning can deliver coherent rollouts without error accumulation and ensemble-spread collapse, and whether a single model can expose the resulting coherence-diversity trade-off as a controllable inference choice rather than a fixed design commitment. We introduce a noise-conditioned teacher-forcing training scheme conditioning a diffusion U-Net downscaling model on a noised previous high-resolution state, so the trained model interpolates continuously between autoregressive (AR) and per-timestep independent (PTI) sampling via one noise parameter. We apply the scheme to an hourly, European-domain historical simulation downscaling AWI-CM1 (1$^\circ$) with ICON (0.11$^\circ$, regridded to 0.25$^\circ$) for precipitation and 2-meter temperature. The noise parameter traces a smooth interpolation between ICON's autocorrelation and PTI generation. AR rollouts of up to six months show no progressive degradation, with ensemble spread contracting only slightly within the first 24 hours. In contrast, the same architecture trained on noise-free previous states diverges. For precipitation PTI underestimates maxima by up to 2x in daily aggregations, while AR tracks ICON's maxima closely. For temperature, differences between modes are negligible. These results show that in diffusion downscaling temporal consistency can be achieved architecture-agnostically and offered as a controllable, not fixed, inference choice.

</details>

## 5. GameScrub: Learning HUD Removal from Real Gameplay with Recovered Targets

**中文标题**: GameScrub: Learning HUD Removal from Real Gameplay with Recovered Targets

**作者**: Mohammad Areeb Qazi, Yiyan Hu, Yanan Wang, Xuezhi Liang, Xin Li et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-20

**arXiv ID**: [2611.27547v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27547v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27547v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Gameplay videos are a rich source of experience for learning virtual-world dynamics, yet their frames contain heads-up displays (HUDs) that follow interface rules rather than scene dynamics. Removing HUDs would make this footage directly usable for learning world dynamics. However, training a removal model requires paired clips with and without HUDs, whereas real recordings contain only HUD-overlaid footage. Existing work synthesizes pairs by compositing HUD graphics onto HUD-free footage, requiring a separately curated clean corpus and producing HUDs that only approximate real ones. Models trained on such pairs leave 40 to 50% of real HUD elements untouched. We instead recover pseudo-clean targets directly from real recordings by inpainting HUD regions with a pretrained video diffusion model. Preventing HUD traces from leaking into recovered targets is challenging because latent video autoencoders mix information across mask boundaries. Our training-free recovery pipeline, GameScrub-Data, pre-fills HUD regions with flow-guided background estimates before encoding, excludes HUDs from scene conditioning, and confines restoration to masked regions through pixel-space compositing. The resulting pairs train GameScrub, a video diffusion removal model that handles diverse HUDs without inference-time masks or clean reference images. On the in-the-wild Game2World benchmark, GameScrub achieves an artifact-adjusted removal score of 71.95, compared with 57.86 for the strongest reference-free GameCleaner configuration, and removes 90.06% of annotated HUD elements versus 59.07%. These results demonstrate that real recordings with recovered targets can support effective HUD removal, advancing HUD-free gameplay data for world models and controllable video generation.

</details>

## 6. Beyond a Single Human Demonstration: Learning from Scored Trajectory Alternatives for End-to-End Driving

**中文标题**: Beyond a Single Human Demonstration: Learning from Scored Trajectory Alternatives for End-to-End Driving

**作者**: Xinyao Rao, Zecheng Tang, Liuxuan Sun, Zheyuan Lai, Minjun Yu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-20

**arXiv ID**: [2611.27515v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27515v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27515v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Human driving logs record only one trajectory per scene, leaving other feasible driving choices without explicit trajectory targets or quality labels. This limits supervision for end-to-end planners that generate and refine multiple candidate trajectories. Meanwhile, conditioning refinement on a large vision-language model adds substantial online inference cost. We propose DriveSTA, which extends autoregressive proposal generation and proposal-conditioned residual flow refinement with scored trajectory alternatives. For each training scene, we generate alternatives offline through smooth deformations of the recorded trajectory and evaluate their simulated execution against logged traffic. Diverse, high-quality alternatives supervise distinct proposals and define residual targets for quality-guided denoising, while candidates spanning the full score range train a scorer to assess driving quality. A shared joint-embedding predictive architecture (JEPA), adapted through masked video-feature prediction, encodes observed frames once and provides temporal context for both refinement and scoring. On NAVSIM v1 Navtest, DriveSTA ranks first among the compared planners trained on NAVSIM data alone. With expanded training data, DriveSTA-Scale achieves state-of-the-art results among the compared methods on Navtest and closed-loop HUGSIM, while reducing mean planner forward-pass latency by 73% relative to ChainFlow-VLA in matched evaluations.

</details>

## 7. Self-Supervised Continuous-Time Depth Learning and Ego-Motion Estimation from Asynchronous Events

**中文标题**: Self-Supervised Continuous-Time Depth Learning and Ego-Motion Estimation from Asynchronous Events

**作者**: Sadiq O. Macaulay, Dipon Kumar Ghosh, Hadfield Simon

**发布日期**: 2026-11-20

**arXiv ID**: [2611.27509v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27509v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27509v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.RO

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Event cameras register brightness changes pixel by pixel with microsecond precision, whereas the dense depth that could supervise them is costly to acquire and exists only at isolated instants. Condensing the stream into frames eases learning, but at the expense of the very timing that sets event sensing apart. We propose a self-supervised approach in which each event is assigned its own depth in continuous time, and the camera motion is recovered from the same observations. Supervision arises from geometry: lifted into 3D by its predicted depth and displaced by the camera motion at the instant it fired, an event should coincide with others produced by the same edge, so any misalignment exposes an error in depth or motion. Differentiable rendering then turns these per-event estimates into dense depth maps at target times within each window, faithful to the events behind them. On MVSEC, the approach outperforms prior event-only self-supervised methods in most sequences and depth ranges, with the widest margins at night, and recovers camera rotation more accurately than an earlier unsupervised event-based method. Ablations isolate the roles of local spatiotemporal context, the temporal backbone, and the alignment objective. These results position motion-compensated event alignment as a principled source of supervision for continuous-time depth and ego-motion estimation.

</details>

## 8. Solar-Conditioned Flow Matching for Multitemporal Aerial Visible-to-Infrared Image Translation

**中文标题**: Solar-Conditioned Flow Matching for Multitemporal Aerial Visible-to-Infrared Image Translation

**作者**: Wenwen Fang, Bingwei Hui, Xin Chen, Zhongzhen Sun, Zhenfeng Shao

**发布日期**: 2026-11-20

**arXiv ID**: [2611.27501v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27501v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27501v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Aerial visible-to-infrared (V2IR) image translation provides scalable thermal data across the full diurnal cycle. However, existing methods predominantly learn a static mapping within narrow acquisition windows. As the thermal state evolves throughout the day, a single visible image may correspond to multiple plausible IR appearances, making V2IR translation an inherently multitemporal task. This letter proposes solar-conditioned latent flow matching (Solar-Flow), which upgrades aerial V2IR translation from static mapping to multitemporal generation. First, we design three computable physical factors, namely the solar elevation, the thermal phase clock (TPC), and the flight altitude. The TPC counts the elapsed heating and cooling hours on a continuous 24-hour axis anchored at sunrise, so the thermal state stays well defined even at hours without training samples. Second, we inject the condition into every Transformer block of a pretrained flow matching generator through adaptive layer normalization. This design preserves translation fidelity and enables multitemporal generation. Finally, we construct a paired aerial V2IR dataset covering 14 hour groups. Solar-Flow achieves multitemporal generation on this dataset, where a single visible image is translated into plausible IR images at multiple times of day, even at hours that were never seen in training.

</details>

## 9. Ego2Ego: Generative Visual Perspective Taking Across Collaborators

**中文标题**: Ego2Ego: Generative Visual Perspective Taking Across Collaborators

**作者**: Chuhan Jiao, John Gideon, Anshul Gupta, Guy Rosman, Andreas Bulling

**发布日期**: 2026-11-20

**arXiv ID**: [2611.27459v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27459v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27459v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.RO

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Visual perspective taking, the ability to infer what another person sees from their position, is fundamental to human collaboration. However, prior works reduced perspective taking to state estimation, geometric reconstruction, or discrete question answering, whereas existing cross-view video generation methods either inferred the target view from the input video alone or required an external camera that observes the target person. We introduce generative visual perspective taking: Given an ego person's egocentric video, a scene point cloud, and a collaborator's camera trajectory, the task is to generate the continuous egocentric video from the collaborator's perspective. This task is challenging given extreme viewpoint shifts, dynamic occlusions, and sparse visual overlap. We address these challenges with Ego2Ego -- a set of backbone-agnostic geometric modules that provide a pretrained video diffusion model with the 3D scene geometry shared by both interactants. Ego2Ego renders dense, temporally consistent scene coordinate maps into every view as a shared spatial reference, aligns cross-view tokens in 3D ray space through relative geometric attention, and recovers regions outside the ego person's current view from the ego person's visual memory. Experiments on the CoMind dataset across three video diffusion backbones show that Ego2Ego consistently improves visual quality, Level-2 scene layout accuracy, and collaborator motion, outperforming state-of-the-art cross-view video generation baselines on every backbone. By generating the collaborator's egocentric video more accurately, Ego2Ego brings visual perspective taking to human-robot collaboration, where it can help infer what a human collaborator sees and does, and can turn single-person recordings into synchronised multi-perspective videos for robot learning.

</details>

We present Ego4Gen (Egocentric 4D Future Generation), a world model for 4D future generation in robotic manipu lation. ## 21. SMSD: A Semantic Misinformation Spreader Detection Approach

**中文标题**: SMSD: A Semantic Misinformation Spreader Detection Approach

**作者**: Zeeshan Bin Siddique, Michael Bewong, Md Geaur Rahman, Sajal Halder, Selasi Kwashie

**发布日期**: 2026-11-20

**arXiv ID**: [2611.27661v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27661v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27661v1)

**类别**: cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Misinformation spreaders on social media significantly amplify false narratives, making early detection critical to mitigate their spread. Existing approaches often overlook evolving user behavior, introduce semantic noise by ignoring user stance, and fail to handle newly emerging users effectively. To address these limitations, we propose a \underline{S}emantic \underline{M}isinformation \underline{S}preader \underline{D}etection approach (SMSD) that models misinformation propagation as a temporal, user-centric, and behavior-driven learning problem. SMSD adopts a multimodal architecture that incorporates three categories of features: user-level, network-level, and conversation propagation. A stance-aware labeling strategy is also introduced to differentiate supportive, opposing, questioning, and neutral users towards misinformation claims. Additionally, a monthly quantile analysis is introduced to simulate realistic, evolving user participation. Initial experimental results on the FibVid COVID-19 benchmark dataset demonstrate that SMSD improves recall by 45.0\%, F1 by 49.5\%, and MCC by 74.6\% under stance-aware labeling and by 17.7\%, 7.4\%, and 11.4\%, respectively, under threshold-based labeling. It consistently outperforms all baselines in both settings.

## 22. Efficient VLA Reinforcement Learning via Subchunk Rollouts in Latent World Models

**中文标题**: Efficient VLA Reinforcement Learning via Subchunk Rollouts in Latent World Models

**作者**: Nan Tang, Han-Tao Zhang, Jing-Cheng Pang, Hao-Nan Wang, Hao-Xiang Ren et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-20

**arXiv ID**: [2611.27645v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27645v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27645v1)

**类别**: cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Model-based reinforcement learning (MBRL) can improve pretrained vision-language-action (VLA) policies through imagined experiences. However, existing VLA-MBRL methods primarily rely on large-scale image or video generation world models, incurring large computational overhead. Latent world models offer a more efficient alternative but face two challenges: compounding errors during recursive prediction and incompatibility with VLAs' visual input interfaces. In this work, we propose LS-MBPO, a lightweight residual MBRL framework that leverages latent world models to improve upon pretrained VLAs. LS-MBPO introduces two key techniques: (1) Hierarchical Forcing, which combines dense causal supervision with autoregressive feedback to mitigate compounding prediction errors; and (2) Latent Subchunk Rollouts, which leverage action chunks from a frozen VLA policy and a latent residual policy for successive subchunk refinement, bridging the gap between latent imagination and VLA policy execution. We further introduce a Progress-Conditioned Branch-Start Curriculum that progressively shifts rollout starting points from later to earlier task stages, enabling effective policy learning from short imagined rollouts. Extensive experiments on LIBERO, RoboTwin, and a real-world ALOHA robot show that LS-MBPO achieves superior task performance and training efficiency compared to existing methods.

</details>

## 1. UniAction: Unified Action Conditioning for World Models

**中文标题**: UniAction: Unified Action Conditioning for World Models

**作者**: Bowen Xue, Zizhang Li, Hong-Xing Yu, Jiajun Wu

**发布日期**: 2026-10-23

**arXiv ID**: [2610.32977v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32977v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32977v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Actions that condition world models are highly heterogeneous, and thus existing action-conditioned world models are typically built for individual action types, fragmenting already limited action-annotated data across separate models. We introduce UniAction, a unified action-conditioning framework that represents any action as a sparse set of action elements, each with a discrete semantic identity and a continuous spatiotemporal grounding coordinate. By mapping these coordinates directly into the pretrained video model's native positional space, UniAction allows heterogeneous actions to condition a shared world model within the same spatiotemporal structure. We further introduce cardinality-calibrated attention to address the bias from differing action representation granularities. We evaluate UniAction on four representative action forms: force, camera control, hand pose, and robot pose. UniAction outperforms conditioning alternatives in action following, and a single jointly trained model surpasses the action-specific experts, showing that heterogeneous action data benefit from unified model and joint training.

</details>

## 2. CompMVR: Compression-Aware Multi-View Restoration Using Diffusion Models for Geometrically Consistent 3D Reconstruction

**中文标题**: CompMVR: Compression-Aware Multi-View Restoration Using Diffusion Models for Geometrically Consistent 3D Reconstruction

**作者**: Dong-hwi Kim, Chaewon Moon, Hojun Song, Dongbeom Kim, Junyeong Jang et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-23

**arXiv ID**: [2610.32800v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32800v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32800v1)

**项目主页**: https://mchaewon.github.io/CompMVR-project-page/.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Real-world 3D reconstruction pipelines often rely on multi-view images degraded by lossy compression. While compression artifacts have been extensively studied in single-image or video restoration, their impact on multi-view geometry remains largely overlooked. In this work, we identify compression-induced cross-view geometric inconsistency as a critical failure mode, where view-dependent artifacts disrupt feature matching, dense correspondence, camera parameter estimation, and downstream 3D reconstruction tasks such as novel view synthesis. To address this problem, we propose CompMVR, a compression-aware multi-view diffusion restoration framework that jointly refines compressed views while preserving cross-view consistency, and construct compressed datasets for systematic evaluation. Unlike conventional restoration methods that optimize each image independently for 2D perceptual quality, CompMVR leverages learned compression priors and cross-view correspondence to recover visual details while improving geometric reliability across views. Experiments across diverse datasets, codecs, and compression levels demonstrate gains in restoration quality and downstream 3D tasks, including novel-view synthesis, camera pose estimation, and view matching. These results establish compressed multi-view restoration as a distinct problem and highlight its importance for 3D reconstruction under lossy compression. Code and data for this paper are available at https://mchaewon.github.io/CompMVR-project-page/.

</details>

## 3. Video-MemComp: Taylor-Guided Sensitivity-Aware Memory Management for Streaming Video Understanding

**中文标题**: Video-MemComp: Taylor-Guided Sensitivity-Aware Memory Management for Streaming Video Understanding

**作者**: Yonghui Niu, Yuhao Ji, Chang Nie, Jiao Zhang, Yilong Li et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-23

**arXiv ID**: [2610.32784v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32784v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32784v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

The rapid growth of video data has created an urgent need for efficient video understanding systems that can handle long and continuous visual inputs, posing significant challenges for Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) due to severe memory bottlenecks. Moreover, existing compression methods often overlook the explosive accumulation of approximation errors. To address this issue, we propose Video-MemComp, a training-free and sensitivity-aware context management framework for memory-constrained streaming video understanding. Our method theoretically decomposes compression error into orthogonal local approximation error and layer-wise structural sensitivity via a first-order Taylor expansion. To minimize local approximation error, we perform token aggregation in a pre-RoPE safe zone with an attention-conserving logit bias, mitigating geometric distortions caused by RoPE rotations. To manage layer-wise structural sensitivity, we dynamically allocate the KV cache budget based on offline gradient priors and online context entropy, prioritizing high-sensitivity layers. Video-MemComp outperforms or achieves performance comparable to full-cache and state-of-the-art compression methods on benchmarks including MLVU, VideoMME, and EgoSchema. Notably, it improves the overall accuracy of Qwen2.5-VL-3B from 62.3% to 64.1% while reducing the KV cache to an equivalent of 6,000 tokens per layer, saving over 90% of KV memory.

</details>

## 4. VERA-Hand: A Verified Multidimensional Benchmark for 2D Hand Pose Estimation in Third-Person Dynamic Gesture Videos

**中文标题**: VERA-Hand: A Verified Multidimensional Benchmark for 2D Hand Pose Estimation in Third-Person Dynamic Gesture Videos

**作者**: Gibran Benitez-Garcia, Hiroki Takahashi

**发布日期**: 2026-10-23

**arXiv ID**: [2610.32517v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32517v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32517v1)

**项目主页**: https://gibranbenitez.github.io/VERA_Hand/

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Hand landmarks serve as input for gesture recognition, sign language understanding, and touchless human-computer interaction. Yet hand pose estimators are typically evaluated with frame-wise localization metrics such as PCK or MPJPE, which are computed over matched predictions and overlook missed or spurious hands, handedness errors, and temporal instability. To address these limitations, we present VERA-Hand, a benchmark that evaluates estimators using manually verified, frame-level 2D annotations for 10,690 hands across four complementary dimensions (detection, localization, identification, and temporal stability), as well as a joint Pose-F1 metric that requires both correct detection and accurate localization. Evaluating six estimators spanning hand-specific, whole-body, reconstruction-based, and foundation-model paradigms, we find that no estimator dominates: rankings reorder sharply across dimensions. VERA-Hand also exposes failures that localization-only and downstream evaluations may not reveal, including cross-hand overlap in over 85% of frames for a recent foundation model. A post-hoc cleanup case study demonstrates how the benchmark can validate corrective interventions, while stratified analyses show that robustness varies across motion-blur classes and hand regions. We release the verified annotations, protocol, and code at https://gibranbenitez.github.io/VERA_Hand/

</details>

## 5. Distortion-Aware Spatiotemporal Learning for Real-Time Wide-Angle Video Rectification

**中文标题**: Distortion-Aware Spatiotemporal Learning for Real-Time Wide-Angle Video Rectification

**作者**: Lang Nie, Yihan Yang, Wenbo Nie, Hongguang Zhu, Yang Wei et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-23

**arXiv ID**: [2610.32500v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32500v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32500v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

While wide-angle lenses provide an expanded field of view (FoV), they exhibit severe nonlinear distortion near image boundaries, unnaturally stretching people in these regions. Existing methods mostly focus on facial regions, require high computational costs, and lack temporal modeling, resulting in incomplete full-body rectification, impractical real-time deployment, and noticeable inter-frame shakes. To tackle these issues, we propose \textbf{FastVC}, an efficient full-body video rectification framework driven by distortion-aware spatiotemporal learning. Grounded in lens physics, FastVC introduces a distortion-aware thin-plate spline (DA-TPS) module that nonlinearly remaps control points into a peripheral-dense layout, enabling more accurate geometric fitting without additional computational cost. We further develop a dual-level spatiotemporal learning scheme for causal and stable video rectification. At the model level, neighborhood spatiotemporal attention (NST) aggregates local context and selectively fuses historical features with low overhead. At the optimization level, sparse spatiotemporal supervision applies spatial alignment only to anchor frames, decoupling frame-wise accuracy from temporal stability. Besides, we contribute the first benchmark dataset for wide-angle full-body video correction. Extensive experiments show that FastVC achieves state-of-the-art rectification fidelity and visual stability at 57.0 FPS.

</details>

## 6. Efficient Zero-Shot Video Anomaly Detection via Signal-Aware Adaptive Fusion (SAAF)

**中文标题**: Efficient Zero-Shot Video Anomaly Detection via Signal-Aware Adaptive Fusion (SAAF)

**作者**: Khaled Waleed Dawoud, Muhammad Haris Khan

**发布日期**: 2026-10-23

**arXiv ID**: [2610.32486v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32486v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32486v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/kdawoud91/SAAF

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Deploying Vision-Language Models for video anomaly detection confronts a fundamental constraint. Their semantic reasoning demands computational budgets incompatible with the scale of real-world video streams. We present Signal-Aware Adaptive Fusion (SAAF), a framework that achieves efficient zero-shot anomaly detection through three key innovations. First, we introduce a dual-signal adaptive sampler operating in semantic embedding space: a delta signal captures sudden events via frame-to-frame cosine distance, while a drift signal detects static and gradual anomalies through deviation from rolling temporal context. Their OR-gated combination with adaptive EMA-based thresholding provides comprehensive anomaly coverage without training data. Second, we propose a threshold-gated cascaded architecture where lightweight frame-level scoring precedes expensive video-level temporal analysis, invoking it only when confidence warrants additional context. Third, we design a signal-aware weighted fusion mechanism that dynamically adapts score combination based on which signal triggered frame selection. Across four benchmarks, SAAF delivers competitive accuracy at substantially lower cost. Among training-free zero-shot methods, SAAF sets a new state of the art on XD-Violence (92.07%), MSAD (86.70%), and ShanghaiTech (80.54%) AUC-ROC, with XD-Violence and MSAD also surpassing supervised baselines. SAAF sustains 44.95, 48.61, and 41.06 FPS on XD-Violence, UCF-Crime, and MSAD, a 2.43$\times$, 2.63$\times$, and 2.22$\times$ throughput improvement over the prior best zero-shot method. Code: https://github.com/kdawoud91/SAAF

</details>

## 7. OPTARI: An open-source software framework for clinical translation of optoacoustic imaging

**中文标题**: OPTARI: An open-source software framework for clinical translation of optoacoustic imaging

**作者**: Moritz Schillinger, Maximilian Bader, Adrian Buehler, Felix Wachter, Ferdinand Knieling et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-23

**arXiv ID**: [2610.32468v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32468v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32468v1)

**类别**: cs.SE, cs.CV, physics.med-ph

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Combined optoacoustic (OptA) and ultrasound imaging is a promising modality across a wide range of pathologies. Its use in clinical research, however, is limited by a lack of analysis software accessible to users without programming expertise. Existing tools are either script-based engineering frameworks or proprietary vendor applications. We present OPTARI, an open-source graphical software application to facilitate large-scale clinical optoacoustic-ultrasound (OPUS) studies. OPTARI builds on the napari image viewer and the PATATO toolkit for OptA data processing. It supports native vendor and open HDF5 data formats, aligns with the IPASC standard, and is distributed as a standalone executable. Features include region-of-interest annotation, reconstruction, chromophore unmixing, deep-learning-based tissue segmentation, and temporal and spectral analysis modules. OPTARI offers a combination of features for clinical study analysis that none of the compared tools provide. The automated tissue segmentation produced results comparable to expert labeling for muscle tissue, and batch processing reduced analysis time from minutes to under one second per scan. The tool is used in ongoing clinical OPUS studies. OPTARI connects engineering frameworks with medical research and provides an open-source basis for reproducible and standardized analysis in clinical OptA imaging studies.

</details>

## 8. Tempo-Zero: Recursive Self-Play in Executable Worlds for Video Reasoning

**中文标题**: Tempo-Zero: Recursive Self-Play in Executable Worlds for Video Reasoning

**作者**: Jingmin Zhu, Yanyan Zou, Xiuyu Li, James Bailey, Dinh Phung et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-23

**arXiv ID**: [2610.32433v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32433v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32433v1)

**项目主页**: https://alchemist-jo.github.io/tempo-zero/

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video reasoning requires understanding how events unfold over time, yet videos with accurate temporal annotations are expensive to collect. Self-play offers an alternative by letting a model generate and solve its own tasks without manual annotation. However, existing video self-play methods train on fixed sets of recorded videos, so they cannot alter the underlying event dynamics and rely on noisy pseudo-labels derived from the model's own outputs, such as majority voting, that risk reinforcing shared model errors. To overcome these limitations, we introduce Tempo-Zero, a self-improvement framework that closes a loop of executable environments, execution-verified self-play, and environment evolution, where executing a program renders a video with the ground-truth option and interval of every question. A single policy plays both Questioner and Solver, proposing questions and answering them with timestamped evidence lines, and is trained on execution-grounded rewards instead of model consensus. The Solver's performance steers a curriculum over tasks and difficulty levels, and the policy selects rule changes that enrich the event dynamics of later rounds. Despite being trained solely on simple programs, without supervised fine-tuning or external video data which prior methods typically require, Tempo-Zero outperforms the base models and recent self-play baselines on average across twelve real-world video benchmarks at different model scales, with particularly strong gains on temporal grounding tasks. Project page: https://alchemist-jo.github.io/tempo-zero/

</details>

## 9. UniWorld: A Unified World Model for Real-Time Visual World Simulation

**中文标题**: UniWorld: A Unified World Model for Real-Time Visual World Simulation

**作者**: Xin Zhang, Yabo Chen, Shuaiwei Wang, Zixuan Duan, Haibin Huang et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-23

**arXiv ID**: [2610.32381v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32381v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32381v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Continuous visual world simulation requires coupling evolving scene geometry with temporally persistent appearance. We present UniWorld, a unified framework that integrates depth trajectories and reference appearance within a shared video generation model. The central design maintains visual and geometric histories on a common timeline. Noise-aligned overlapping continuation places generated history at the prescribed sampling noise level and commits only newly generated latents. Rolling depth latent state preserves the corresponding geometric controls, incrementally reads newly available geometry, and keeps the conditioning fixed within each sampling window. Together, these mechanisms ensure exact preservation of latent history while bounding the active depth state by the window size. We evaluate continuous generation, paired history interventions, and long-horizon behavior across diverse scene categories. The same generator supports estimated, native, and on-demand simulator depth, while cached and incremental execution paths produce bitwise-identical outputs. Appearance changes and geometric control branches operate through the same interface without modifying committed history. The measured steady-state DiT execution path achieves real-time generation throughput, demonstrating the efficiency of UniWorld for continuous visual world simulation.

## 10. Past as State, Present as Attention: Persistent-State Blockwise Flow Matching for Long-Horizon Co-Speech Motion Generation

**中文标题**: Past as State, Present as Attention: Persistent-State Blockwise Flow Matching for Long-Horizon Co-Speech Motion Generation

**作者**: Yi Liu, Xiangyue Zhang, Jianfang Li, Jia Ma, Yisheng He et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-23

**arXiv ID**: [2610.32373v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32373v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32373v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.GR, cs.MM

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Co-speech motion generation remains challenging over long horizons due to the need for temporal coherence and consistent style. Existing methods typically generate long motions segment by segment, either conditioning on a short seed of recent frames, which gradually drifts, or retaining ever-longer explicit histories, which are costly to maintain and hard to learn from limited data. We trace these limitations to a key asymmetry between past and present: past motion carries slowly varying style that should be compactly summarized, whereas the current segment exhibits fast-changing local dynamics that require fine-grained modeling. This asymmetry motivates a simple Past-As-State, Present-as-Attention principle for long-horizon co-speech motion generation. Following this principle, we propose PASPA, which summarizes past history with an inter-block persistent state and models the current segment with intra-block bidirectional self-attention. We instantiate PASPA within a blockwise flow matching framework, with multi-block supervision for effective training over variable histories, prefill-decode state caching for efficient rollout, and hybrid classifier-free guidance for enhanced history and speech conditioning. Extensive public-benchmark experiments show that PASPA achieves state-of-the-art FGD performance and preserves motion style across long-sequence generation.

</details>

<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Stochastic Push-Pull achieves linear speedup with respect to the network size $n$ on static strongly connected digraphs, but whether this speedup persists on time-varying networks remains unclear. In this work, we construct a quadratic decentralized problem on period-two networks showing that stochastic Push-Pull may fail to attain linear speedup over time-varying digraphs. However, periodic networks can have $n_{\mathrm{eff}}=o(n)$, demonstrating the loss of linear speedup under temporal variation. Numerical experiments illustrate effective-count scaling and the separation between static and periodic networks, consistent with our theoretical findings.

## 26. When to Trust Memory: Retrieval-Guided Probabilistic Spatiotemporal Forecasting under Distribution Shift

<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Spatiotemporal forecasting plays an important role in real-world applications, where probabilistic approaches provide uncertainty estimates for decision-making. Real-world systems are inherently non-stationary, leading to distribution shifts between training and test data. However, existing probabilistic methods mainly focus on modeling data distributions under standard settings, while distribution shift methods often handle such shifts in a coarse manner, failing to characterize shift degree and adapt prediction strategy accordingly. As a result, they often produce miscalibrated and unreliable forecasts. To address this issue, we propose REMIND, a retrieval-guided probabilistic framework for spatiotemporal forecasting under distribution shift. REMIND leverages retrieval to estimate how well current observation are supported by historical patterns, enabling adaptive prediction under different shift regimes. A shift-adaptive gate, guided by retrieval similarity and memory dispersion, balances the two branches according to shift degree. We further design a hierarchical Gaussian mixture to capture heterogeneous uncertainty over the dual branch predictions, and introduce a memory perturbation training scheme to improve generalization. Experiments on real-world datasets with distribution shifts demonstrate that REMIND consistently outperforms state-of-the-art methods in both forecasting accuracy and probabilistic prediction quality.

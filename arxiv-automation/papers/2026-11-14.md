## 1. Back to Pixels: Lifting Latent Video Diffusion into Pixel Space

**中文标题**: Back to Pixels: Lifting Latent Video Diffusion into Pixel Space

**作者**: Yanke Song, Shuo Chen, Chris Rockwell, Guande He, Nan Liu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.17336v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17336v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17336v1)

**项目主页**: https://chenshuo20.github.io/PixVid/

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Frontier video generation models work in a VAE latent space that compresses video in both space and time. This compression made training tractable, but now limits high-resolution, controllable, and interactive generation -- doubling resolution quadruples token count, conditions apply to groups of frames rather than individual ones, and each response must wait for the rest of its multi-frame block. We present PixVid, a simple yet effective method that lifts a pretrained latent video model to directly generate every frame in pixel space. PixVid keeps the diffusion transformer and replaces the VAE decoder with a convolutional head that renders its outputs into pixels. We find that predicting clean video rather than velocity, together with a larger noise shift, is key to quality. Finetuned from Wan2.1-14B, PixVid is competitive with the latent baseline within $\sim 1.3$M training clips. Working framewise in pixels, it generates at $2\times$ higher resolution with the same per-frame token count, keeps depth-conditioned video editing aligned under fast motion, and responds to each action at the next frame. Project page: https://chenshuo20.github.io/PixVid/

</details>

## 2. K2V: Generative Keyframing For Physically Plausible Video Generation

**中文标题**: K2V: Generative Keyframing For Physically Plausible Video Generation

**作者**: Felix O'Mahony, Daniel Olmeda Reino, Roberto Cipolla, Ayush Tewari

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.17324v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17324v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17324v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video diffusion models are able to generate samples with high-quality appearance and textural qualities. However, content produced by these methods is known to exhibit significant physical errors. This is because, in a classical video generation setting, all physical reasoning must take place simultaneously and exclusively within the diffusion process.We propose to resolve these issues by redistributing the task of physical reasoning onto a language model-backed keyframe generation module, which allows for explicit reasoning in language, and fine-grained control over the appearance of the video at important moments in the video. We then condition a video generation model with these keyframes, reducing the role of the diffusion model to the simpler task of interpolating between physically-correct intermediate states, rather than inferring an entire sequence with diffusion. Results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing approaches, achieving state-of-the-art performance on the Physics-IQ Verified benchmark and PhyGenBench.

</details>

## 3. Spatial Foundry: A Multimodal Dataset for Spatial Intelligence

**中文标题**: Spatial Foundry: A Multimodal Dataset for Spatial Intelligence

**作者**: Ethan Lin, Yang Song

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.17318v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17318v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17318v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Spatial intelligence requires connecting visual observations to the geometry, objects, and persistent state of an environment. Images alone leave these relationships implicit, while disconnected annotations make it difficult to study appearance, camera motion, and scene changes under a common reference. We introduce Spatial Foundry, a collection of 5,280 furnished residential 3D scene configurations with editable assets and aligned visual, geometric, and temporal data. Its modalities include floor plans, perspective and panoramic RGB, metric depth, point clouds, meshes, instance-level 3D bounding boxes, and camera trajectories paired with video. Shared scene coordinates link observations across views and time; editable source scenes enable additional camera paths and controlled object changes. We describe the data representation and construction workflow, and discuss applications in floor-plan-conditioned 3D generation, geometric reconstruction, instance-level perception, camera-conditioned video generation, shared world modeling, and scene editing. Controlled camera and scene variations support spatial perception and world-model research. The public project page provides videos and an interactive colored point-cloud preview.

## 4. Consistent Video-to-Video Translation via Explicit Correspondences

**中文标题**: Consistent Video-to-Video Translation via Explicit Correspondences

**作者**: Gaurav Parmar, Zhengqi Li, Richard Zhang, Jun-Yan Zhu, Srinivasa Narasimhan et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.17298v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17298v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17298v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.GR, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Interactive video-to-video applications require real-time generation while maintaining long-range temporal consistency. However, recent methods achieve speed by restricting attention to a fixed-length causal window. This limits long-range consistency whenever relevant history falls outside the context window. To address this limitation, we introduce vid2vid-long, a correspondence-based approach grounded in a simple observation. Video-to-video translation provides an explicit cross-frame reference: the regions that match in the input video should match in the output video too. Concretely, we compute patch-level correspondences in the input, guiding retrieval of previously generated outputs in the corresponding regions, and condition the model on these patches through correspondence cross-attention. Our experiments demonstrate that vid2vid-long consistently improves long-range temporal consistency across recent video models while preserving generation quality and real-time performance.

## 5. SpotDiff: A Plug-and-Play Framework for Zero-Shot Change Detection in Remote Sensing Imagery

**中文标题**: SpotDiff: A Plug-and-Play Framework for Zero-Shot Change Detection in Remote Sensing Imagery

**作者**: Anit Upadhyaya, Ata Akbari Asanjan, Chaitanya Jalluri, Subash Yeggina, Jeremy Kravitz

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.17172v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17172v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17172v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Remote sensing change detection usually relies on large collections of temporally aligned, pixel-level annotations, which limits its scalability across regions, imaging platforms and application domains. Recent zero-shot approaches reduce the need for supervision, but they often localize fine-grained changes poorly because their features are coarse or inconsistent across time. We present SpotDiff, a training-free, plug-and-play framework for zero-shot change detection built by composing complementary vision foundation models. SpotDiff uses DINOv3 for semantically robust features, AnyUp to reconstruct them at pixel resolution, and SAM 3 for text-guided region-of-interest proposals, which an inference-time multi-scale strategy (SWIMS) makes reliable on small objects. Change is then measured by comparing the reconstructed features of the two dates inside the proposed regions, with no learnable components, fine-tuning or change labels. To our knowledge, SpotDiff is the first change detection method to use a universal feature upsampler, which alone adds 13.8 F1 over bilinear upsampling of the same features. On LEVIR-CD, SpotDiff reaches 82.46 F1, 2.06 points above the strongest prior training-free method, and on the multi-class SECOND benchmark it improves building change detection by 10.3 IoU and the six-class average by 2.2 IoU. Qualitative results on PlanetScope (3 m) and Sentinel-2 (10 m) imagery show that the same pipeline transfers across resolutions without modification. Our results indicate that composing modern foundation models is an effective route to scalable, label-free change detection.

</details>

## 6. HiVE-VQA: Hierarchical Interaction via Volumetric Embedding for Video Quality Assessment

**中文标题**: HiVE-VQA: Hierarchical Interaction via Volumetric Embedding for Video Quality Assessment

**作者**: Robbie Hamilton, Fan Zhang, David Bull

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.17146v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17146v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17146v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/RobHam99/HiVE-VQA.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

The proliferation of user-generated video content has made accurate and robust no-reference video quality assessment (NR-VQA) a highly important challenge. Existing research increasingly relies on architecturally complex multi-branch pipelines or computationally intensive fine-tuning of single-branch backbones. In this paper, we investigate whether a single frozen video foundation model can serve as an effective NR-VQA backbone, and propose HiVE-VQA, the first framework (to our knowledge) to apply a JEPA-based video encoder to this task. HiVE-VQA extracts token grids from multiple depths of a pre-trained V-JEPA 2.1 encoder and stacks them into a hierarchical spatio-temporal volume, which a compact 3D convolutional head fuses across its layer, spatial, and temporal axes, combining low-level distortion cues from shallow layers with the semantic and temporal context captured at greater depths. The encoder remains frozen throughout, so only the head is trained: 5.2M parameters (5.7\% of the total), with no auxiliary multi-modal models and an inference cost that is invariant to video resolution. Trained solely on LSVQ and evaluated without any fine-tuning, HiVE-VQA outperforms 17 state-of-the-art (SOTA) methods in SROCC and PLCC on both intra-dataset (LSVQ-test, LSVQ-1080p) and cross-dataset (KoNViD-1k, LIVE-VQC) benchmarks. These results indicate that hierarchical, depth-wise fusion of features from a single frozen video foundation model provides a more effective and efficient basis for NR-VQA than task-specific multi-network fusion. Code and model checkpoints are available at https://github.com/RobHam99/HiVE-VQA.

## 7. STeP-C: Real-Time Neural Compression for Distributed Fiber-Optic Sensing at Scale

**中文标题**: STeP-C: Real-Time Neural Compression for Distributed Fiber-Optic Sensing at Scale

**作者**: Tingfeng Li, Ming-Fang Huang, Shaobo Han, Yue Tian

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.17126v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17126v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17126v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Distributed fiber-optic sensing (DFOS) technologies such as Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) generate continuous, high-resolution spatio-temporal data at rates exceeding hundreds of megabytes per second, creating severe challenges for real-time storage and transmission. While neural compression methods can achieve strong rate-distortion performance on sensing data, prior approaches either require per-segment retraining incompatible with streaming, or have only been validated offline. None have been demonstrated end-to-end on a continuously streaming DAS acquisition system at scale. We present STeP-C (Spatio-Temporal Predictive Compression), a neural compression framework that decouples spatial encoding, temporal prediction, and entropy-optimized residual coding, trained end-to-end with a rate-distortion objective motivated by conditional entropy reduction. Critically, we identify entropy coding as the dominant bottleneck for streaming deployment. We address it through GPU-side symbol computation, cached probability tables, and parallel GIL-free arithmetic coding, together with FP16 inference and batched entropy coding, enabling a full real-time pipeline built around a GPU ring buffer. We validate this pipeline on a live DAS testbed, achieving a 98.85% file size reduction at 31.04 dB full-band PSNR and an average throughput of 535 MB/s, exceeding the 324 MB/s requirement for 1 kHz, 81 km DAS streams. We further show that STeP-C substantially outperforms conventional codecs at practical streaming bitrates, can be applied to unseen channel ranges within the same acquisition system without retraining, and largely maintains downstream detector consistency, with 96.62% high-confidence class agreement and 100% Jaccard similarity on detected event types.

</details>

## 8. Spatiotemporal learning improves quantification of pore-space evolution and fluid displacement

**中文标题**: Spatiotemporal learning improves quantification of pore-space evolution and fluid displacement

**作者**: Zhuangzhuang Ma, Rukuan Chai, Qianqian Ma, Yanghua Wang, Branko Bijeljic et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.17111v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17111v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17111v1)

**类别**: physics.flu-dyn, cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Pore-space evolution and fluid displacement influence how rocks transmit and store fluids, affecting geological carbon storage, underground hydrogen storage and groundwater contaminant transport. A single time-resolved micro-CT experiment may produce tens to hundreds of three-dimensional images, and segmentation is required to quantify physical properties and run image-based simulations. Traditional methods such as thresholding perform poorly on noisy, low-contrast images and need manual adjustment per image; 3D deep learning networks segment each image without temporal information, so their errors vary between images and could be mistaken for physical change. Here we introduce a transformer-based 4D segmentation framework combining spatial and temporal information. It uses sequential images to resolve ambiguous phase assignments and separate physical change from segmentation error. The method is tested on images of mineral dissolution: it measures pore-volume growth and rock boundaries more accurately than an equivalent 3D model that ignores temporal information. It also captures the increase in permeability verified against reference segmentations and flow simulations. The advantage transfers to another dissolution sample without retraining. On two other datasets of fluid displacement, it reduces spurious changes in predicted rock and porosity fluctuations and accurately identifies filling events. Spatiotemporal segmentation turns sequences of hundreds of three-dimensional images into more reliable quantitative measurements of pore-space evolution and fluid displacement without per-image parameter adjustment to support the analysis of time-resolved CT imaging.

</details>

## 9. SALT: State-Aligned Low-Rank Transfer for Single-Temporal Cross-Dataset Structural Localization in Remote Sensing

**中文标题**: SALT: State-Aligned Low-Rank Transfer for Single-Temporal Cross-Dataset Structural Localization in Remote Sensing

**作者**: Aggelos Psiris, Georgios Angelidis, Giorgos Sfikas, Maria Vakalopoulou, Thomas Lagkas et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.17077v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17077v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17077v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/gthpapadopoulos/SALT

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Change detection in remote sensing conventionally relies on spatially aligned bi-temporal imagery, which may be unavailable or unreliable in practical scenarios. Recent single-temporal approaches address this limitation by learning structural evidence, such as building regions, from a labeled source dataset and transferring it to unseen target imagery using only the current image. However, adaptation to the source dataset can over-specialize pretrained features and reduce their cross-dataset generalization, a risk that this paper argues is higher when the feature adaptation is parameterized independently of the states used for prediction. To address this issue, the current study introduces SALT, a State-Aligned Low-Rank Transfer framework. SALT keeps a DINOv3 backbone frozen and learns negative and positive structural states that both score each patch and, through State-Aligned Low-Rank Adaptation (SALA), span the rank-2 subspace in which patch features are corrected. Directional State Alignment supervises these corrections to raise the evidence of positive patches and lower that of negative ones, while Geometry Preservation limits changes to the pairwise relations among pretrained patch features. Experiments on LEVIR-CD and the WHU aerial building dataset show that, at the pixel level and in both transfer directions, SALT outperforms four baselines reproduced under a common source-only protocol, improving over the strongest reproduced baselines by 9.45 and 5.79 points in pixel-level AP and by 27.69 and 14.25 points in IoU (LEVIR-CD to WHU and WHU to LEVIR-CD, respectively), with only 0.068M trainable parameters and 40.2 ms per image. In order to facilitate reproducibility and further research, the source code of SALT is available at https://github.com/gthpapadopoulos/SALT

## 10. TokenStream: Multi-Subject Personalized Video Generation with Token-wise LoRA and Guided Attention

**中文标题**: TokenStream: Multi-Subject Personalized Video Generation with Token-wise LoRA and Guided Attention

**作者**: Hung Nguyen, Quang Qui-Vinh Nguyen, Khoi Nguyen

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.16897v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16897v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.16897v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

We introduce TokenStream, an optimization-based framework that composes independently learned subject identities in text-to-video generation without a reference-video training dataset or joint optimization of each new subject combination. Learning from a single reference image makes subject appearance difficult to separate from static-image bias, while merging independently trained adapters can mix identities. Token-wise LoRA (ToRA) restricts low-rank updates to the cross-attention value projections of subject-associated text tokens, providing a separate adaptation path for each subject. A T2V-guided stream copies queries and keys from the frozen base model during early denoising to reuse its layout and motion priors while retaining personalized values. We also introduce Multi-DB, a 30-subject, 122-prompt benchmark with appearance-free prompts and varied interactions. On Multi-DB at 1.3B, TokenStream improves identity preservation from 0.422 for DreamBooth to 0.467 and textual alignment from 0.652 to 0.688.

**作者**: Shujie Duan, Jia Hong Puah, Haocheng Wu, Yifei Mao, Hongting Zhou et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.16514v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16514v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.16514v1)

**类别**: cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Time series forecasting aims to predict the future evolution of complex systems by learning their underlying dynamics from past observations. Despite recent advances in deep learning, Transformers in particular still struggle to capture the temporal evolution of system dynamics. Existing approaches have been largely driven by architectural innovation, with comparatively little attention paid to training strategies that explicitly exploit the temporal structure governing system dynamics. In this work, we introduce DeltaTSF, a dynamics-aware forecasting framework based on discrete derivative representations of time series. Specifically, DeltaTSF jointly learns the series and their discrete temporal derivatives across both input and forecast horizons within a unified, model-agnostic framework. By coupling these representations over time, the model is encouraged to maintain temporal consistency, a property we show to induce a form of discrete Taylor consistency during training. This formulation enables the model to better reflect the underlying system dynamics, resulting in more stable and physically coherent forecasts. Notably, DeltaTSF does not require explicit derivative computation and introduces no additional computational overhead at inference time. Extensive experiments across diverse benchmarks and backbone architectures demonstrate that DeltaTSF consistently improves forecasting performance and yields more dynamically coherent predictions than strong SOTA methods. These results highlight the effectiveness of enforcing dynamic coherence through derivative representations as a model-agnostic paradigm that operates alongside and complements architectural design. Source code will be made publicly available upon acceptance.

## 42. Does Deep Knowledge Tracing Learn Semantic Relations Between Knowledge Components?

**中文标题**: Does Deep Knowledge Tracing Learn Semantic Relations Between Knowledge Components?

**作者**: Kia Karbasi, Mahdi Saiedi, Kevin Hong, Gregory Pottie

**发布日期**: 2026-11-12

**arXiv ID**: [2611.16323v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16323v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.16323v1)

**类别**: cs.LG, cs.CL

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Deep Knowledge Tracing (DKT) is a knowledge tracing (KT) model that updates a shared latent state from sequences of interactions, each pairing a knowledge component (KC) with a response. An interaction with one KC can therefore affect predictions for other KCs. We ask: Are the KC relations learned by DKT semantic? We construct text-derived semantic KC representations using nine pipelines to test whether the conclusions hold across alternative ways of constructing those representations.   The first test compares DKT-derived KC relation graphs with semantic relation structures induced by pairwise cosine similarities among the KC representations. Across the evaluated datasets, embedding models, and pipelines, the two structures align only weakly and inconsistently. The second test introduces semantic information into DKT; the pretrained text-derived representations do not consistently outperform the standard DKT baseline.   In the reduced-data experiments, semantic initialization yields a higher test area under the ROC curve (AUC) than the baseline at eight of nine nested sample sizes for ASSISTments 2017. KC-supervised fine-tuned BERT produces small positive AUC differences in some matched comparisons, but the study does not establish their stability across runs. These effects remain limited and inconsistent. The findings suggest that behavioral, temporal, structural, or psychometric signals may also shape DKT's learned relations. DKT-derived relations are not primarily semantic: the tested text-derived semantic structures do not strongly recover the DKT-derived relation structure.

</details>

We also describe TemporalBertSpam, an architecture that encodes each sender's message history with a causal Transformer, and outline the same-split evaluation needed to measure its contribution.

## 46. TRACE: Trajectory Synchronization for Training-Free Resolution Extension in Video Generation

**作者**: Libo Zhu, Fan Li, Zhixin Wang, Binglu Wang, Weisheng Dong et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-11

**arXiv ID**: [2611.15983v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15983v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15983v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/libozhu03/TRACE.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

More text-to-video models are available, but many remain limited to low resolutions. Training them for higher resolutions is costly, while existing training-free methods still struggle with fine detail, scene structure, and efficiency. We propose TRACE, a training-free method that synchronizes low- and high-resolution generation trajectories. During early denoising, TRACE aligns predicted scene content at corresponding noise levels. It computes high-resolution updates from low-resolution predictions and the current high-resolution state, without high-resolution model inference. The high-resolution trajectory retains fine-grained variation for generating new details. Local window attention then refines fine details in a shared high-resolution latent state. TRACE checks suspected duplicate subjects against the corresponding low-resolution layout and corrects selected regions and their solver history while preserving detail elsewhere. Experiments across all kinds of video generation backbones and resolutions show improved perceptual quality and text-video alignment over FreeSwim, a state-of-the-art training-free method, with 2.0-2.9x end-to-end speedups. Our code and models will be released at https://github.com/libozhu03/TRACE.

First, multimodal understanding separates the agents, especially in understanding space and camera motion. Second, all agents seem to already master basic camera work and staging, and fail at challenging requests. Third, agents inspect their own renders but fail to correct their errors. Agents thus need better visual understanding of space and camera, explicit checks for challenging requests, and item-by-item verification of renders against the brief.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-11

**arXiv ID**: [2611.15640v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15640v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Streaming video understanding aims to continuously process incoming visual observations, making the retention and retrieval of relevant information increasingly important as the video history grows. Although existing benchmarks include memory-related questions, question-answering accuracy alone does not reliably measure memory capabilities. Incorrect predictions may arise from failures in visual understanding rather than forgetting, whereas correct predictions may rely on linguistic priors or recent observations without requiring historical memory. To address these ambiguities, we introduce MemInStream, a benchmark dedicated to evaluating memory capabilities in streaming video understanding. We develop an evidence-controlled validation protocol that associates each question with temporally localized evidence intervals. The protocol validates evidence solvability and memory necessity by retaining questions that models can answer from the annotated evidence but cannot resolve using recent observations, post-evidence observations, or textual input alone. This helps disentangle memory limitations from understanding failures while reducing memory-independent shortcuts. MemInStream provides a systematic evaluation of memory capabilities through four major categories, covering Episodic Retrieval, State Tracking \& Updating, Multi-Evidence Integration, and Contextual Memory across diverse video scenarios. Overall, \textsc{MemInStream} provides a comprehensive and reliable benchmark for systematically evaluating memory capabilities in streaming video understanding.

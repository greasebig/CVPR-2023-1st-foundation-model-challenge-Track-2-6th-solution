## 1. Video-Coder: Reinforcing Agentic Coding for Video Generation

**中文标题**: Video-Coder: Reinforcing Agentic Coding for Video Generation

**作者**: Chaoyang Wang, Sicheng Gao, Junan Hu, Kunyang Li, Dongyang Chen et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-06

**arXiv ID**: [2611.09894v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09894v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09894v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Recent years have witnessed remarkable progress in video generation in terms of visual quality and temporal consistency. However, generating videos with precise spatial structure, coherent motion dynamics, and controllable scene composition remains challenging. In this paper, we introduce Video-Coder, the first attempt to train a code-driven video generation agent, which autonomously writes and executes code to construct a dynamic 3D scene (Draft Video) that provides structure and motion signals to downstream video generators. To support this paradigm, we design a dedicated data pipeline and curate two high-quality datasets, Video-Coder-SFT-6k and Video-Coder-RL-2k, which contain diverse prompts and ground-truth reference videos. We further develop the Video- Coder Harness, a unified interactive environment integrating code execution, 3D scene rendering, and execution feedback for agent training and inference. We then train Video-Coder with SFT fol- lowed by harness-native agentic reinforcement learning with dual reward feedback, which combines execution-based and video-based rewards. Additionally, we design an annealing training strategy that gradually reduces the execution reward over time, allowing the model to rely more on the video reward in the later training stages. Experiments show that Video-Coder achieves significant gains, improving Minimax-H3 by approximately 7 points on PhyGenBench and 24 points on VBench-2.0. We hope this work can serve as an open foundation for coding agents in video generation, and we fully open-source our data, models, and code

</details>

## 2. FlowMoP: Stochastic Multi-Person Motion Prediction

**中文标题**: FlowMoP: Stochastic Multi-Person Motion Prediction

**作者**: Aadya Agrawal, Ho Kei Cheng, Alexander Schwing

**发布日期**: 2026-11-06

**arXiv ID**: [2611.09882v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09882v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09882v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Predicting the future motion of multiple people is inherently stochastic: the same observed history admits many plausible continuations depending on intent, coordination, and social context. Yet the dominant paradigm in multi-person 3D motion prediction remains deterministic, with existing methods producing a fixed number of outputs per agent. In contrast, we present a conditional flow matching framework that jointly generates diverse future motions for multiple agents, modeling both global trajectories and root-relative poses. A B-spline reparameterization compresses the generative state space while enforcing temporal smoothness, and a dual-stream transformer conditions each agent's predictions on observed neighbor motion for interaction-aware forecasting. Our method achieves state-of-the-art performance on CMU-Mocap (UMPM) and 3DPW, outperforming deterministic baselines on joint position error, pose error, and final displacement error despite producing a full predictive distribution. We additionally establish the first motion prediction baseline on WorldPose, a large-scale professional soccer dataset, where social conditioning demonstrably concentrates predictive uncertainty along interaction-constrained directions.

## 3. AR-Edit: Training-Free Autoregressive Video Editing without Inversion

**中文标题**: AR-Edit: Training-Free Autoregressive Video Editing without Inversion

**作者**: Hovhannes Margaryan, Quentin Bammey, Christian Sandor, Vicky Kalogeiton

**发布日期**: 2026-11-06

**arXiv ID**: [2611.09795v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09795v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09795v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Editing a video stream in real time, without access to future frames, is essential for interactive applications. Existing training-free video editing methods, however, assume full offline access to the entire sequence, while streaming approaches either rely on additional training or are limited to specific editing techniques. We show that this limitation can be removed by analyzing the self-attention dynamics of DMD-distilled autoregressive (AR) video diffusion models. We uncover a key asymmetry: queries and keys remain stable and aligned with their source counterparts across denoising timesteps, while values diverge and encode the evolving content. Inherited from the non-distilled bidirectional teacher, this asymmetry is strongest at high noise, where few-step streaming models operate. The resulting mechanism is simple: a single forward pass suffices to capture source identity in the value features, which can be reused throughout generation. Starting from pure Gaussian noise and injecting only these source values reconstructs the input with fidelity matching or surpassing inversion-based methods, effectively eliminating inversion. Based on this insight, we introduce AR-Edit, a training-free, inversion-free method that caches source values once and selectively injects them during denoising. Across multiple AR backbones, AR-Edit enables real-time streaming editing with superior source preservation and tonal fidelity at minimal overhead.

</details>

## 4. Direct Regression and Geometric Recovery for Event-based Spacecraft Pose Estimation

**中文标题**: Direct Regression and Geometric Recovery for Event-based Spacecraft Pose Estimation

**作者**: Pruthvi Nandan Janga, Kashyap Hegde Kota, Bharatesh Chakravarthi

**发布日期**: 2026-11-06

**arXiv ID**: [2611.09717v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09717v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09717v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Event cameras offer high temporal resolution and dynamic range for uncooperative spacecraft pose estimation, but models trained on synthetic event streams must generalize to the noise, background activity, and contrast variation of physical sensors. We present a comparative systems study of two independently developed approaches to synthetic-to-real, event-based 6-DoF spacecraft pose estimation. The first is a modular pipeline that combines learned keypoint detection and unlabeled target-domain adaptation with geometry-based pose recovery and temporal smoothing. The second directly regresses translation and rotation from global and cropped event representations using an apparent-scale cue, physics-motivated synthetic corruptions, and feature-consistency regularization. We evaluate both systems under the same SPARK 2026 hidden-test protocol on the real-event split of SPADES, with predictions for all test frames. The results reveal a component-wise reversal concealed by the aggregate challenge score: direct regression reduces orientation error by 22.7% (1.1767 to 0.9101 rad) and obtains a 13.4% lower composite score, whereas the modular pipeline reduces relative translation error by 78.7% (0.1335 to 0.0285). Archived configurations and a qualitative failure case identify plausible explanations for these complementary error profiles, without isolating their causes. These findings motivate a testable hybrid design that combines direct orientation estimation with geometry-constrained translation recovery.

</details>

## 5. Video2Asset: Online Construction of Persistent 3D Street-Scene Assets

**中文标题**: Video2Asset: Online Construction of Persistent 3D Street-Scene Assets

**作者**: Zihan Zheng, Chunlei Cai, Zhenlong Wu, Linjun Li, Yichen Gong et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-06

**arXiv ID**: [2611.09661v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09661v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09661v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Online 3D street-scene asset construction turns multi-view video into a continually updated scene of independently editable objects. The central challenge is to accumulate partial, intermittent observations under motion and occlusion without fragmenting identities or mixing evidence across objects. To address this challenge, we propose Video2Asset, an online framework for constructing persistent 3D assets from multi-view video. Using occupancy as an intermediate representation, we construct identity-aware observations with Historical Carrier Guidance for instance decoding and an identity encoder fusing instance-aligned 3D features with multi-view appearance. Accumulated-Evidence Association compares current observations with identity, geometric, and appearance evidence accumulated within asset records to determine identity inheritance and update world-coordinate states. We instantiate meshes from an asset library using these states and combine them with an occupancy-derived static background to support scene visualization, mesh replacement, object deletion, and trajectory editing. Experiments on several datasets demonstrate improvements in temporal identity consistency and asset quality across multiple occupancy models, including a 15.4% reduction in asset fragmentation on nuScenes with SGE.

## 6. Video Stitching from Multiple Moving Cameras

**中文标题**: Video Stitching from Multiple Moving Cameras

**作者**: Shira Ifergane, Roy Amoyal, Shahar Benishay, Oren Freifeld

**发布日期**: 2026-11-06

**arXiv ID**: [2611.09570v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09570v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09570v1)

**项目主页**: https://bgu-cs-vil.github.io/MViS-project/

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

We address geometric video alignment for the same scene captured by multiple freely moving cameras. Existing methods, which are based on image stitching or video stabilization, are typically limited to two cameras or must assume the restrictive setting of a fixed linear ordering of the cameras. We propose MViS, a lightweight test-time optimization framework for joint multi-view alignment with temporal consistency. MViS uses a spatial-transformer-inspired net to jointly process frames from all views within a short temporal window and predict per-view, per-time homographies. Optimization proceeds in two stages: geometry-guided alignment followed by appearance-based refinement, with both stages enforcing multi-view and temporal consistency across the sequence. The method requires no scene-specific hyperparameter tuning, handles arbitrary and time-varying view-intersection graphs, and remains robust under rapid camera motion. On a standard two-view video benchmark, MViS matches or surpasses the state of the art. To facilitate evaluation in more complex scenarios, we introduce an annotated dataset with more than two freely moving cameras and establish the first benchmark for this setting. Project page: https://bgu-cs-vil.github.io/MViS-project/ .

## 7. ExQuery: Explicit Structured Query Priors for Temporal 3D Object Detection

**中文标题**: ExQuery: Explicit Structured Query Priors for Temporal 3D Object Detection

**作者**: Honglin Sun, Yifei Zhang, Junyang Zhang, Kenji Hashimoto

**发布日期**: 2026-11-06

**arXiv ID**: [2611.09527v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09527v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09527v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Sparse query-based 3D detectors commonly encode heterogeneous semantic, geometric, and motion information into unified latent queries, including when explicit 3D anchors are used. We introduce ExQuery, an explicit structured query framework for temporal 3D object detection. Each decoded query is organized into a shared semantic context slice and physical-state-specific slices for center, dimensions, orientation, and velocity. This structure is preserved from prior encoding to branch-specific regression and is reused to reconstruct temporal queries: predicted physical states are transformed by ego motion, classification scores form soft semantic priors, and the reconstructed query is fused with the stored latent instance feature through gated affine modulation. Experiments on the nuScenes benchmark show that our method achieves competitive detection performance, and ablation studies validate the effectiveness of our proposed modules.

## 8. V-STORM: A Structure-Aware Grounded Chain of Thought Dataset for Faithful and Fine-Grained Spatio-Temporal Video Reasoning

**中文标题**: V-STORM: A Structure-Aware Grounded Chain of Thought Dataset for Faithful and Fine-Grained Spatio-Temporal Video Reasoning

**作者**: Dian Jiao, Yuqian Yuan, Jiaqi Zhu, Mingjian Gao, Siwen Tan et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-06

**arXiv ID**: [2611.09511v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09511v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09511v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

While MLLMs can often identify what happens in a video, they still struggle with fine-grained spatio-temporal reasoning, which requires precise grounding when and where the supporting evidence appears and composing such evidence into a coherent reasoning chain. This challenge is exacerbated by the lack of scalable data construction and evaluation pipelines that provide granular, verifiable supervision linking reasoning steps to explicit spatio-temporal evidence.To address this gap, we present V-STORM, a structure-aware framework that transforms videos into Spatio-Temporal Scene Graphs (STSGs) and derives Grounded Chain of Thought (GCoT) from validated evidence paths, making each reasoning step traceable to structured and verifiable spatio-temporal evidence.Under this framework, we construct a 260K-sample training set and a 3K-sample human-verified benchmark, both featuring GCoT annotations that link reasoning to explicit spatio-temporal evidence.Experiments reveal a clear separation between evidence localization and evidence composition: current MLLMs can often identify relevant spatio-temporal cues, yet still fail to integrate them into correct multi-step reasoning, exposing a bottleneck that answer-only evaluation largely obscures. Moreover, fine-tuning with V-STORM substantially improves both answer accuracy and grounding faithfulness, validating its effectiveness as supervision for faithful and evidence-grounded video reasoning. Our dataset, benchmark, and source code will be publicly released.

## 9. FusionCLIP: Multimodal Contrastive Pretraining for Fusion Plasma Diagnostics

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Visible-light videos and multivariate diagnostic signals provide complementary observations of tokamak discharges. However, existing fusion pretraining methods, primarily learn representations by reconstructing or predicting observations, leaving the correspondence between these modalities underexplored. Representation learning is further challenged by the fact that plasma-related regions occupy only part of each video frame and diagnostic variables have substantially different sampling rates. We propose FusionCLIP, a multimodal contrastive pretraining framework. Spatiotemporal Stability Selection retains informative visual tokens, while a Temporal Query Encoder integrates asynchronous diagnostic signals using their observation timestamps. Contrastive learning then aligns the two modality representations. FusionCLIP achieves an aggregate score of 89.07\%, exceeding the best general-purpose pretraining configuration and an in-domain contrastive pretraining configuration without Spatiotemporal Stability Selection by 6.27 and 2.12 percentage points, respectively. Ablations and attention visualizations further support the contributions of its key components.

</details>

## 10. HMMF-Net: A Lightweight Hierarchical Multi-Frame Multimodal Fusion Network for Accurate Fish Feeding Intensity Assessment

**中文标题**: HMMF-Net: A Lightweight Hierarchical Multi-Frame Multimodal Fusion Network for Accurate Fish Feeding Intensity Assessment

**作者**: Phi-Hung Hoang, Van-Manh Tran, Nam-Thuan Trinh, Thi-Thu-Hong Phan

**发布日期**: 2026-11-06

**arXiv ID**: [2611.09224v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09224v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09224v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Automated fish feeding intensity assessment (FFIA) is important for precision aquaculture and feed cost optimization. However, unimodal methods are vulnerable to acoustic noise and visual disturbances, while existing multimodal frameworks often rely on computationally intensive 3D convolutions or Transformers, limiting their use on edge devices. To address these limitations, we propose HMMF-Net, a lightweight multimodal architecture that combines early multi-frame visual encoding and multi-axis acoustic modeling through a hierarchical bidirectional messenger cross-modal fusion (BMCF) mechanism. The visual branch captures temporal variations in feeding behavior by stacking temporally distant frames as a multi-channel input to an adapted 2D EfficientNetB0 backbone. The acoustic branch employs adaptive multi-axis residual units (AMARU) to extract temporal, frequency, and local time-frequency representations from log-mel spectrograms. BMCF progressively exchanges information between the two modalities at intermediate representation levels through compact messenger tokens, reducing the computational overhead of dense cross-modal attention. Evaluated on the AV-FFIA dataset using five-fold cross-validation, HMMF-Net achieves 96.77% accuracy and 96.88% F1-score on the held-out test folds, with 3.987M parameters and 1.002 GFLOPs. Compared with the best-performing visual configuration (95.49% accuracy), HMMF-Net reduces the classification error by 28.38%. Deployment on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano using TensorRT with FP32 precision maintains comparable accuracy, with an inference time of 11.54 ms per 2-second sample and a model size of 18.81 MB. These results demonstrate that HMMF-Net combines effective multimodal representation learning with computational efficiency, enabling accurate and practical FFIA on resource-constrained edge platforms.

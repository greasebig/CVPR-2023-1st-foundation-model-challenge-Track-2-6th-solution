## 1. Images Talk Back: Guiding MMDiTs by Suppressing Image-to-Text Feedback

**中文标题**: Images Talk Back: Guiding MMDiTs by Suppressing Image-to-Text Feedback

**作者**: Yucheng Yang, Xiaoxiao Sun, Shusheng Yang, Jiakang Chen, Serena Yeung-Levy

**发布日期**: 2026-10-09

**arXiv ID**: [2610.13941v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13941v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13941v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/yyc1215lll-creator/SFG.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Multimodal diffusion transformers (MMDiTs) allow the evolving image to update internal text representations through joint attention. These representations then guide subsequent image updates, raising a question: does this feedback help correct deviations from the prompt, or preserve them? We find that image-to-text (I2T) feedback can preserve the objects and attributes already represented in the evolving image, including errors. Transplanting I2T messages can rescue failures or disrupt successes. At matched intervention strengths, suppressing the influence of I2T feedback improves generation, whereas opposite controls degrade it. These findings motivate Semantic Feedback Guidance (SFG), which suppresses the influence of I2T feedback by guiding away from an auxiliary branch that strengthens this feedback and weakens text-to-image conditioning. The in-place variant, iSFG, directly suppresses I2T feedback and strengthens text-to-image conditioning, requiring no extra model evaluations. Temporal interventions motivate running the SFG branch during the first 20% of image sampling steps, requiring about $1.2\times$ the base NFE without classifier-free guidance (CFG). Experiments across three image-generation backbones show gains in alignment and preference-model scores. On FLUX, adding SFG to CFG raises GenEval from $0.6661$ to $0.7479$; on SD3.5M, iSFG adds $+1.33$ HPSv2.1 over CFG at zero extra NFE. The control also transfers to text-to-video and image-to-video generation, improving VBench scores. Code is available at https://github.com/yyc1215lll-creator/SFG.

</details>

## 2. Chronos Policy: Learning Robot Policies on Physical Time

**中文标题**: Chronos Policy: Learning Robot Policies on Physical Time

**作者**: Changhao Wang, Jaden Clark, Shuran Song

**发布日期**: 2026-10-09

**arXiv ID**: [2610.13929v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13929v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13929v1)

**类别**: cs.RO, cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Robot policies often convert sensing and control into fixed-rate observation-action sequences, leaving physical time implicit in sequence position. This assumption breaks when observation rates or delays vary, or when inference latency changes. We introduce Chronos Policy, which makes physical time explicit by conditioning on observation capture times and desired action execution times. We study two ways to incorporate time into a diffusion transformer: adding timestamp embeddings to token features, or encoding physical time in positional encoding through time-aware rotary positional encodings (time-aware RoPE). We find that time-aware RoPE is more robust, allowing attention to depend directly on physical time while keeping token content unchanged. During training, we temporally randomize which observations are sampled and when the action chunk begins, exposing the policy to a wider range of sensing and execution schedules that may occur during deployments. This also enables learning from asynchronous, multi-rate data without forcing all measurements onto a fixed temporal grid. Across simulation and real-world experiments, Chronos Policy improves performance on dynamic tasks, where observation-to-action timing is critical, and on multisensory tasks, where the relative timing between observations matters. On standard quasi-static tasks, it preserves nominal performance while improving robustness to changes in sensing and inference timing.

</details>

## 3. Make It Flow: Animating Water in 3D Gaussian Splatting Scenes

**中文标题**: Make It Flow: Animating Water in 3D Gaussian Splatting Scenes

**作者**: Kjell Keune, Florian Hahlbohm, Jannis Möller, Susana Castillo, Martin Eisemann et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-09

**arXiv ID**: [2610.13860v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13860v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13860v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.GR

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Explicit 3D scene representations such as 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) have recently enabled high-quality, real-time novel view synthesis. However, these methods assume static scenes, limiting realism in the presence of dynamic phenomena ubiquitous in real-world environments. While coherent motion (e.g., humans or vehicles) can be captured and reconstructed, high-frequency effects such as flowing water remain intractable under casual capture conditions, where dense multi-view, high-speed acquisition is not feasible. We present a method to animate water in static 3DGS scenes, enabling visually plausible motion without requiring dynamic capture or physical simulation. Our approach estimates 2D optical flow from reference images or model renderings and lifts it into a consistent 3D Eulerian motion field. We propose a cinemagraph-inspired algorithm that applies this motion field to deform Gaussian primitives, producing temporally seamless looping animations. The resulting representation retains real-time rendering performance, significantly enhances perceptual realism, and preserves free-viewpoint navigation within the scene. We evaluate our method on a newly captured dataset of in-the-wild waterfall scenes and demonstrate clear improvements over prior work in dynamic Gaussian splatting and 3D cinemagraph generation in visual quality and computational efficiency.

</details>

## 4. LapMerge: Differentiable Exact-Budget Token Merging in Linear Time

**中文标题**: LapMerge: Differentiable Exact-Budget Token Merging in Linear Time

**作者**: Magdalena Trędowicz, Aleksandra Nowak, Tomasz Steifer, Łukasz Struski, Szymon Płotka et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-09

**arXiv ID**: [2610.13858v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13858v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13858v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Modern vision and language models process ever longer token sequences, and reducing them is the main lever on downstream cost. Token compression, however, faces a fundamental trade-off: selecting tokens discards information, while existing merging methods preserve it only through pairwise token-to-token matching or $MN$ token-to-output interactions. We introduce LapMerge, a differentiable merging operator that resolves this trade-off for ordered sequences: a learned mass distribution partitions the sequence into exactly $N$ equal-mass segments under a Laplace-kernel density, and prefix/suffix scans aggregate each segment, so both the forward and the backward pass run in $\mathcal{O}(M+N)$ time. This combination - an exact output budget, end-to-end differentiability, and linear cost - is precisely what previous reducers trade off against one another. The budget can be changed without changing operator parameters, and the learned mass doubles as a token-level map of where the budget is allocated. Across vision and vision-language tasks, LapMerge matches or improves on strong token reducers while being up to two orders of magnitude faster at large sequence lengths; it is particularly effective when token order reflects spatial or temporal locality, and works both as a drop-in reduction module and as a trainable architectural component.

</details>

## 5. Revisiting the Task of Building Damage Assessment by Satellite Imaging

**中文标题**: Revisiting the Task of Building Damage Assessment by Satellite Imaging

**作者**: Anthony Cioppa, Virgile Blua, Sébastien Piérard, Pietro Ceccato, Marc Van Droogenbroeck

**发布日期**: 2026-10-09

**arXiv ID**: [2610.13857v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13857v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13857v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Natural disasters such as earthquakes, tropical storms, and floods cause extensive damage to buildings, and rapid, reliable damage maps are essential to guide emergency response. Automating this task, referred to as building damage assessment (BDA), from satellite imagery, has become an active research topic at the intersection of remote sensing, computer vision, and machine learning, particularly since supervised deep learning methods were shown to outperform earlier handcrafted approaches. Yet, despite a decade of intense activity and the release of several large-scale datasets, the field lacks a shared foundation. This chapter revisits the task of BDA from first principles, organized around three pillars: data, evaluation, and methods. We first clarify the candidate task for- mulations and argue for a sensor-agnostic, instance-based definition that tolerates missing pre-event imagery. We then review existing datasets along the axes of sensing modality, temporal configuration, and geographic coverage. On the evaluation side, we show that prevailing practices, largely based on a single aggregate score, are theoretically fragile, and we lay the foundations of a more informative, ranking-based, multi-score protocol. We further review methods ranging from task-specific deep networks to recent Earth observation foundation models, noticing that generalization to unseen events and regions remains an open difficulty. Finally, we discuss how these elements can inform the organization of a BDA challenge, including cross-validation, challenge, and private data-sharing setups designed to build trust between developers and users.

## 6. Detection of extremely rare dead cells in fluorescence microscopy images

**中文标题**: Detection of extremely rare dead cells in fluorescence microscopy images

**作者**: Prateek Verma, Benjamin Butter, Jian Zhang, Xintao Wu

**发布日期**: 2026-10-09

**arXiv ID**: [2610.13780v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13780v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13780v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Dead cell detection in fluorescence microscopy is essential for phenotypic screening and drug efficacy assessment. However, automated identification remains challenging due to signal variability, optical artifacts, and colocalization ambiguities across multiple channels. Markers that define nuclear boundaries do not neatly coincide spatially or temporally with apoptotic markers. Furthermore, marker accumulation kinetics vary across individual cells and apoptosis stages, making universal thresholds unreliable. We present a systematic comparison of several computational approaches for detecting apoptotic cells in nucleus-stained specimens: (1) heuristic methods using intensity thresholds, distributions, correlations, and domain guidance; (2) classification using numerical features (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost and LightGBM) and image features (ResNet-18 and EfficientNet-B0); and (3) anomaly detection, also using numerical features (Isolation Forest, one-class SVM and PCA reconstruction) and image features (convolutional autoencoders and Deep SVDD). Using a dataset of 258 images containing 65,527 cells/nuclei across multiple scenes with 243 manually annotated ground truth dead cells, we evaluate each method on metrics such as PR-AUC, Precision, Recall, F1-score, and macro-F1. Our results indicate that classification using image features (deep learning) and numerical features (traditional) achieved the best performance, with ResNet-18 and XGBoost significantly outperforming manually designed heuristics and anomaly detection methods. Our findings demonstrate that such biomedical problems remain highly challenging to solve even with the state-of-the-art computational and learning techniques. We aim to provide an effective decision-support tool to researchers while tackling such problems either fully-automatically or in a human-in-the-loop setting as an aide to domain scientists.

</details>

## 7. READ: Reading the Selective Scan for Faithful Video Attribution

**中文标题**: READ: Reading the Selective Scan for Faithful Video Attribution

**作者**: Magdalena Trędowicz, Piotr Kubaty, Arkadiusz Lewicki, Jacek Tabor, Łukasz Struski

**发布日期**: 2026-10-09

**arXiv ID**: [2610.13776v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13776v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13776v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Selective state-space models are an efficient approach to long-sequence video modeling. However, their recurrent computation makes it difficult to determine which video tokens drive a prediction. Existing attribution methods often require additional backward passes or repeated model evaluations, making them substantially more expensive than the forward inference they aim to explain. In this work, we introduce READ, a parameter-free attribution method for selective state-space models that unrolls the recurrence at the position read by the classifier. READ traces the contribution accumulated in the recurrent state back to the video tokens that contribute to the final prediction. By anchoring attribution at the classifier's readout, it produces a single spatiotemporal relevance map from which frame- and region-level relevance can be obtained directly. Despite requiring only one forward pass, READ achieves competitive faithfulness and localisation compared with substantially more expensive attribution methods. Experimental results show that READ avoids the systematic relevance biases of existing methods while recovering meaningful temporal and class-specific structure across datasets and backbones. More broadly, our results show that the recurrence underlying selective state-space models is not only an efficient mechanism for inference, but also a direct source of interpretable evidence for their predictions.

</details>

## 8. Neurons++: A Unified Visual-Stream Framework for Image and Video Reconstruction from fMRI with Neural-to-Visual Interpretability

**中文标题**: Neurons++: A Unified Visual-Stream Framework for Image and Video Reconstruction from fMRI with Neural-to-Visual Interpretability

**作者**: Haonan Wang, Yuchen Wang, Jingyu Lu, Qixiang Zhang, Lehan Wang et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-09

**arXiv ID**: [2610.13763v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13763v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13763v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/xmed-lab/NeuronsPlusPlus.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Decoding visual experience from fMRI is important for understanding human perception and developing brain-computer interfaces. Recent generative decoding methods can reconstruct plausible images and videos, but visual plausibility alone does not ensure that the output is faithfully grounded in brain activity. In this work, we propose Neurons++, a visual-stream fMRI reconstruction framework for both static image and dynamic video reconstruction. Compared with our conference version Neurons, Neurons++ extends the paradigm from video-only reconstruction to visual-stream reconstruction for both images and videos, and replaces parallel task conditioning with serial cortex-inspired decoding. The model first uses image-fMRI pretraining to obtain a stable spatial-semantic initialization, and then performs subject-specific fine-tuning for image and video reconstruction separately. The serial design organizes fMRI-derived representations into explicit spatial, semantic, language-level, and temporal cues. Extensive experiments show that Neurons++ achieves state-of-the-art video reconstruction and competitive image reconstruction across standard benchmarks. Beyond reconstruction fidelity, neural-to-visual analyses indicate that Neurons++ exhibits qualitative directional controllability under neural perturbation, that its fMRI encoder read-out is sensitive to stimulus-relevant cortical features, and that it supports ROI-guided semantic modulation. Code and model weights are available at: https://github.com/xmed-lab/NeuronsPlusPlus.

</details>

## 9. KVeil: First-Place Solution to ECCV 2026 LifeGenIP Challenge on Unlearnable Videos

**中文标题**: KVeil: First-Place Solution to ECCV 2026 LifeGenIP Challenge on Unlearnable Videos

**作者**: Boheng Li, Yueming Xing, Yiming Li, Jie Zhang, Han Qiu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-09

**arXiv ID**: [2610.13733v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13733v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13733v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/AntigoneRandy/KVeil.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

The rapid development of personalized video generation has raised serious concerns over identity privacy, as a few facial videos of a person now suffice to reproduce their identity in synthetic videos. This report presents the first-place solutions to both phases of the ECCV 2026 LifeGenIP Challenge on Unlearnable Videos, which aims to protect facial videos from unauthorized customization under a limited and imperceptible perturbation budget. For Phase 1, we introduce Reference Capacity Collapse, a pair of complementary losses that degrade the information carried by the reference condition while keeping subsequent video representations dependent on it, together with a Motion-Guided EOT mechanism that improves robustness across unknown inference conditions. Phase 2 further considers tuning-based customization and unseen generative models. To address both threats within a single perturbation, we design a two-layer framework that protects the facial and background regions separately, constructing structure-aligned perturbations from a synthetic target identity in the former and strengthening the white-box disruption of Phase 1 in the latter. Our method achieves nearly perfect protection under white-box reference-based customization, and remains effective under other customization paradigms, various models, and temporal attacks. Both solutions are built upon our recent work LETHE. We hope our solutions can inspire future work on protecting facial videos against unauthorized customization. Our code is available at https://github.com/AntigoneRandy/KVeil.

## 10. PhysForge: Material-Controllable 3D Reconstruction with Separable Components

**中文标题**: PhysForge: Material-Controllable 3D Reconstruction with Separable Components

**作者**: Yuchen Wang, Yanqi Bao, Zheng Gu, Zhengqing Chen, Jieqi Shi et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-09

**arXiv ID**: [2610.13681v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13681v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13681v1)

**项目主页**: https://masterwanna.github.io/PhysForge.html

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Recent advances in physical 3D generation have enabled visually faithful assets with simulatable mechanical behavior. However, existing methods provide limited control over material behavior beyond appearance-based inference and struggle to estimate physical properties from complex interactions between objects and semantic parts. Therefore, we present PhysForge, a Video Diffusion Model (VDM)-based framework for addressing these challenges, comprising two complementary modules for material-controllable physical fitting and componentlevel interaction modeling. The first, PhysFit, leverages material-conditioned VDM dynamics as references for physical-property fitting, introducing windowed fitting to focus on material-revealing interactions and Hallucinated Forcing to disentangle nonphysical motion hallucinated by the VDM from material responses. The second, PhysComp decomposes complex interactions into individual objects and semantic components, enabling their physical parameters to be optimized independently and flexibly composed for joint simulation. Reinforcement learning (RL) under partial observability typically re- lies on recurrent networks to encode temporal information, with the recurrent module trained jointly with the policy or value function. This raises a natural question: is learning the recurrent network actually necessary? In this paper, we investigate whether a frozen recurrent network can provide sufficient memory for solving par- tially observable Markov decision processes (POMDPs). Motivated by the theoretical foundations of Reservoir Computing (RC) and Generalized Reservoir Computing (GRC), we study a simple archi- tecture that combines a fixed recurrent network with a trainable nonlinear output module. We evaluate this approach across tasks with varying memory demands, observation modalities, and action spaces. Surprisingly, frozen recurrent networks support recall over hundreds of steps and achieve competitive control performance across diverse POMDPs, while using less than half the number of trainable parameters required by their fully trainable counterparts. These results suggest that learning the recurrent memory module is not always necessary for solving POMDPs. Frozen recurrent networks therefore provide a simple, parameter-efficient, and sur- prisingly strong baseline for partially observable RL. Our source code is available at https://github.com/Aphlios/FRN-POMDP

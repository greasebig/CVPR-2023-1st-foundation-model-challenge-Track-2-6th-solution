## 1. Reframe: Observation-Aligned Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Visual Reasoning

**中文标题**: Reframe: Observation-Aligned Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Visual Reasoning

**作者**: Haozhi Yuan, Handong Li, Yifan Zhang, Qianshan Wei

**发布日期**: 2026-11-09

**arXiv ID**: [2611.12886v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12886v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12886v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Agentic reinforcement learning (RL) and multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) offer complementary routes to post-training. For visual agents, however, tool use presents two related challenges. Models may overlook useful tool-returned observations, while poorly grounded actions can limit the evidence available for subsequent distillation. We introduce Reframe, a post-training framework that treats the observation state, rather than only the output distribution, as an object of alignment. First, Ground-Drop-GRPO (GD-GRPO) trains spatial and temporal specialists with evidence-aware rewards and stochastic removal of the original visual input, encouraging them to acquire grounded evidence and use the returned observations. Second, Observation-Gated-MOPD (OG-MOPD) replaces deficient tool actions with canonical ones mid-rollout and resumes distillation from the repaired observation. The two stages complement each other, as learning to rely on tool-returned observations can help the policy benefit from observation repair. Neither stage requires a task-specific SFT cold start. Extensive experiments over 14 image and video benchmarks demonstrate that Reframe outperforms both Mix-RL and vanilla MOPD. Ablation results show that GD-GRPO increases observation dependence and tool gain compared with outcome-based GRPO.

</details>

## 2. Grasping the Future: Joint 3D Hand-Object Forecasting From Internet Videos

**中文标题**: Grasping the Future: Joint 3D Hand-Object Forecasting From Internet Videos

**作者**: Rustin Soraki, Matthew Wallingford, Ryan Aby, Brandon Chee, Jason Ren et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-09

**arXiv ID**: [2611.12879v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12879v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12879v1)

**项目主页**: https://grasping-the-future.github.io.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

To interact with an object, we need to anticipate both how it will move and how our hands should move with it. Human videos capture these relationships across everyday activities, offering a natural source for learning to predict future interactions. Learning from them requires recovering motion from completed interactions and using it to anticipate what could happen next. We present $\textit{Grasping-the-Future}$, a unified generative model of joint 3D hand-object motion, including wrist motion, finger articulation, and object position and orientation. Through masked spatiotemporal completion and flow matching, we train a single model for several prediction tasks: forecasting future interactions, predicting hand motion given object motion or vice versa, generating both trajectories toward a desired final object pose, and completing partially observed interactions. We explicitly model hand-object relations and incorporate contact constraints to help preserve grasp relationships during motion. To obtain 3D supervision from internet videos, we develop a scalable pipeline that combines pretrained perception models with joint geometric optimization to reconstruct completed interactions. We combine these recovered trajectories with annotated interaction datasets as training data for all tasks. We demonstrate strong performance across all tasks, outperforming released forecasters on datasets with 3D annotations. Moreover, we show geometric and physical plausibility of the predicted trajectories by retargeting them to a robotic embodiment. Videos are available at https://grasping-the-future.github.io.

</details>

## 3. Complementarity-Driven Dual-Teacher Hierarchical Alignment for Zero-Shot EEG-to-Image Retrieval

**中文标题**: Complementarity-Driven Dual-Teacher Hierarchical Alignment for Zero-Shot EEG-to-Image Retrieval

**作者**: Lukun Wu, Jie Li, Ziqi Ren, Ruiyang Xia, Yan Gao et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-09

**arXiv ID**: [2611.12798v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12798v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12798v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Decoding visual information from EEG is central to brain--computer interfaces and brain-inspired intelligence, and contrastive alignment with pretrained vision foundation models (VFMs) has become the dominant paradigm. However, existing methods align EEG solely with CLIP-family VFMs, leaving hierarchical visual information in neural responses under-exploited. To address this gap, we formulate a mechanism-paradigm correspondence view linking hierarchical visual processing (from appearance to semantics) to the complementary biases of cross-paradigm VFMs, with image-only models (e.g., DINO) and image--text models (e.g., CLIP) capturing distinct facets of this hierarchy. Guided by this view, we propose the Dual-Teacher Hierarchical Alignment (DTHA) framework. Concretely, a shared EEG encoder is supervised by frozen multi-blur CLIP and shallow DINOv2 teachers via dual-branch contrastive alignment, so each branch fully supervises the visual cues it emphasizes. A fused-retrieval objective learns adaptive weights to combine their complementary visual representations for zero-shot EEG-to-image retrieval. For the shared EEG encoder, we introduce the Attentive Modulated Scalp-Pattern Bank Encoder (AMSPBE), combining temporo-spatial attention with a learnable scalp-pattern bank for accurate, interpretable visual feature extraction, while modeling regional temporal dynamics and filter-bank topographies. On THINGS-EEG zero-shot retrieval, DTHA achieves 92.4% Top-1 accuracy, exceeding prior state of the art by 12.4 percentage points. Spatial, spectral, and representational-similarity analyses, together with THINGS-MEG evaluation, validate DTHA's effectiveness, biological plausibility, and cross-modality generalization. This complementarity-driven perspective shows that supervision from cross-paradigm VFMs enables more complete extraction of hierarchical visual information from brain activity.

</details>

## 4. Plant Orbiter: 3D Plant Reconstruction and Generation with Video Diffusion Orbit Cameras and 3D Gaussian Splatting

**中文标题**: Plant Orbiter: 3D Plant Reconstruction and Generation with Video Diffusion Orbit Cameras and 3D Gaussian Splatting

**作者**: Zane K. J. Hartley, Yong En Kok, Lewis A. G. Stuart, Andrew P. French, Michael P. Pound

**发布日期**: 2026-11-09

**arXiv ID**: [2611.12739v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12739v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12739v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Modern digital phenotyping pipelines require the precise measurement of a range of 3D morphological plant traits. The accurate, high throughput acquisition of these traits helps breeders and researchers accelerate the development of new varieties, techniques and products. Recent advances in 3D technologies have enabled a rapid transition from 2D to 3D pipelines, but high throughput acquisition of digital 3D twins of plants remains a significant challenge due to complex morphology, thin structures and occlusions. In this paper we present Plant Orbiter, a new approach to generating 3D plants that leverages recent advances in video diffusion to produce high quality 3D Gaussian Splats of plants from a single image. We show that our method more accurately recreates plant morphology than other state-of-the-art methods, achieving the highest 3D F-score against all baselines, while being more practical for high throughput pipelines due to easier alignment and reduced cost of camera placement. Combined with a 2D diffusion model, we show that our method also achieves state-of-the-art results in the generation of 3D plant models from a text prompt.

</details>

## 5. MARS: Multiscale Appearance-Residual Streams for Video Forgery Detection

**中文标题**: MARS: Multiscale Appearance-Residual Streams for Video Forgery Detection

**作者**: Ahmed Y. Radwan, Shaina Raza

**发布日期**: 2026-11-09

**arXiv ID**: [2611.12508v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12508v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12508v1)

**项目主页**: https://vectorinstitute.github.io/mars/

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/VectorInstitute/mars

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Generalizing deepfake detection across facial manipulation and fully generated video remains challenging due to forgery traces varying with the manipulation method and scene content. Explicit spatial and temporal feature differences offer a way to capture this variation, but also respond to natural texture and motion, motivating their joint interpretation with appearance features. We investigate whether combining multiscale feature differences with adapted appearance representations improves detection across both forgery categories. We introduce Multiscale Appearance-Residual Streams (MARS), a two-path video detector. The residual path applies fixed spatial and temporal difference-of-Gaussians operators to frozen intermediate features and models the resulting differences alongside appearance context across frames. The semantic path adapts the encoder's normalization parameters for frame classification, and a clip-dependent gate combines their predictions. Under a shared protocol across seven datasets, MARS achieves the highest average AUROC of 91.43\%, outperforming seven compared detectors. When trained on facial manipulations alone, MARS achieves 93.99\% AUROC on GenVideo and 75.50\% on DF26, demonstrating transfer to generated videos without generated-video training data. MARS also provides signed patch and frame contributions, whose faithfulness we evaluate through controlled spatial and temporal removal against random controls. MARS is publicly available for research: Project page: https://vectorinstitute.github.io/mars/ Github: https://github.com/VectorInstitute/mars

</details>

## 6. V3DSAM: Semantic-Geometric Loop Verification and Geometry-Anchored Persistent Instance Mapping for Feed-Forward Dense SLAM

**中文标题**: V3DSAM: Semantic-Geometric Loop Verification and Geometry-Anchored Persistent Instance Mapping for Feed-Forward Dense SLAM

**作者**: Maolin Ye, Dongqing Zhao, Linyang Li, Shuai Xue, Yuan Liu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-09

**arXiv ID**: [2611.12452v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12452v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12452v1)

**类别**: cs.RO, cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

To address unreliable cross-view association in long-sequence feed-forward dense SLAM, this letter presents V3DSAM, an open-vocabulary semantic dense SLAM system that establishes physical correspondences at both the scene and object levels. The system combines feed-forward geometric estimates from VGGT-SLAM 2.0 with open-vocabulary semantic observations from SAM3. We introduce semantic-geometric dual-scale loop verification, which identifies false loop closures using pixel-level bidirectional reprojection and object-level 3D spatial correspondences, without accessing backbone-internal features. We further propose geometry-anchored persistent instance mapping, which defines physical instances through class-constrained 3D connectivity in an optimized global coordinate system and assigns global IDs without inter-frame identity propagation. The proposed verifier rejects all four false loop candidates in our TUM RGB-D evaluation, while long-sequence ScanNet experiments show improved instance identity consistency. Thus, V3DSAM reduces dependence on internal backbone features and temporal ID propagation for robust long-term cross-view association.

</details>

## 7. LongSCOP: Semantically Consistent Long Video Outpainting

**中文标题**: LongSCOP: Semantically Consistent Long Video Outpainting

**作者**: Yu Tang, Zhiyuan You, Zhixiao Wang, Fanghua Yu, Qingyu Zhang et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-09

**arXiv ID**: [2611.12445v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12445v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12445v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video out-painting is an important problem, yet existing research focuses primarily on short clips, leaving long video out-painting largely underexplored. Extending video out-painting to long videos poses substantial challenges. A natural solution is iterative out-painting, i.e., generating videos chunk by chunk, but this introduces two major issues: generation instability or even failure caused by inter-chunk inconsistencies and error accumulation, and semantic drift over long temporal horizons. In this paper, we propose LongSCOP, short for Semantically Consistent long Video Out-Painting. First, we introduce a training recipe with three components for smooth and stable long-horizon generation: self-forcing training, latent calibration, and future references. Beyond generation stability, we recognize that the rich temporal context of long videos provides valuable references for maintaining semantic consistency. We therefore develop a VLM-based agent that automatically selects the most informative reference frames from the entire video to maintain consistent subjects, scenes, and backgrounds throughout long-horizon generation. We also construct a high-quality training dataset of 62K video clips. To facilitate research on this underexplored problem, we introduce LVO-Bench, a benchmark curated by human experts to assess long-horizon stability and semantic consistency. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our method outperforms existing approaches in both generation stability and semantic coherence.

</details>

## 8. MoSTE: Mixture of Spatio-Temporal Experts for Deepfake Detection in Videos

**中文标题**: MoSTE: Mixture of Spatio-Temporal Experts for Deepfake Detection in Videos

**作者**: Charbel Yahchouchi, Nabyl Quignon, Noemi Roggero, Laurent Saroul, Antitza Dantcheva

**发布日期**: 2026-11-09

**arXiv ID**: [2611.12443v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12443v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12443v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/charbelyah/MoSTE

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Generalizable deepfake video detection remains challenging because forgery artifacts vary substantially across manipulation types and often combine diverse spatial and temporal inconsistencies. Existing parameter-efficient adaptation methods typically rely on a single shared representation, rendering them prone to manipulation-specific bias and limiting transfer to unseen forgeries. To address this challenge, we propose Mixture Of Spatio-Temporal Experts (MoSTE), an adaptation framework that enables conditional specialization of forensic representations. Instead of forcing one adapter to model all forgery patterns, MoSTE decomposes adaptation into parallel spatial and temporal expert pools and dynamically routes tokens to the most relevant experts through an unsupervised token-level routing mechanism. This allows experts to naturally specialize in complementary forgery cues without requiring explicit manipulation labels or multi-stage training, while effectively transferring representations from CLIP to the video domain. Extensive experiments across cross-dataset and cross-manipulation benchmarks demonstrate that MoSTE sets a new state of the art in average generalization while preserving strong performance across diverse manipulation families. Additional analysis of expert routing and attention behavior reveals the emergence of expert diversity, suggesting that MoSTE learns complementary spatio-temporal forensic features. Code is available at https://github.com/charbelyah/MoSTE

</details>

## 9. PhysOPSD: Code as Privilege for Physically Plausible Video Generation

**中文标题**: PhysOPSD: Code as Privilege for Physically Plausible Video Generation

**作者**: Harold Haodong Chen, Rui Peng, Lingteng Qiu, Kejie Qiu, Chao Xu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-09

**arXiv ID**: [2611.12411v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12411v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12411v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

While modern video generative models achieve remarkable visual fidelity, they remain noticeably brittle when generating physically plausible dynamics and complex interactions. Targeted post-training is crucial, yet supervised fine-tuning suffers from offline state mismatch, and reinforcement learning is hindered by sparse, ill-defined physical reward functions. On-Policy (Self-)Distillation (OPSD) reconciles on-policy rollouts with dense corrective feedback, shifting the core challenge to designing effective privileged context. However, conventional modalities fall short: text prompts are inherently declarative, whereas real reference videos heavily entangle physical laws with visual appearance without parametric control. To this end, we propose PhysOPSD, introducing executable code as privileged physical context for video generation. Unlike static visual observations, executable code instantiates an editable physical world with intrinsic abstraction and controllability. Powered by PhysOPSD-Engine, we translate real-world physical scenarios into verifiable simulation programs. PhysOPSD then incorporates (i) dual-level physical privilege to bridge the information gap via an intermediate bottleneck, alongside (ii) controlled physical variations under shared rollout noise to expose causal dynamic shifts. Extensive experiments demonstrate that PhysOPSD substantially improves physical plausibility across diverse backbones, outperforming SFT and RL baselines with only $3$K samples, while preserving general video generation quality.

</details>

## 10. PTC-IoU: CPU-Friendly Detection Quality Estimation for Edge Video Analytics

**中文标题**: PTC-IoU: CPU-Friendly Detection Quality Estimation for Edge Video Analytics

**作者**: Wei Geng, Hyerin Kim, Chang Lu, Zhenghao Lu, Joerg Ott

**发布日期**: 2026-11-09

**arXiv ID**: [2611.12405v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12405v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12405v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.PF

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

An edge video analytics system must estimate the quality of local detections since there are no ground-truth labels available during runtime. Detectors usually provide confidence scores for their results, but these scores can not reveal every missed object or misplaced bounding box. So a quality estimator is necessary to provide a decision signal indicating which frame has potential detection failures that need to be escalated to a more powerful model in the cloud. Neural network based estimators add workload to the GPU already running detection, while CPU capacity may remain available. We present Predictive Temporal Consistency IoU (PTC-IoU), which moves this workload to CPU by compacting detection boxes histories to support deterministic result scoring. Comparing to neural counterparts, experiments on representative edge hardware, Nvidia Jetson Orin Nano, show that our method reduced per-frame estimator latency from 10.3 ms for neural counterparts to 2.2 ms (4.7x faster), increased pipelined throughput by 13.6%, and cuts estimator energy consumption by up to 95%, while maintaining competitive final detection quality verified on multiple datasets.

</details>

One-step temporal-difference (TD) learning bootstraps after every transition, so small per-step errors accumulate along the horizon, and early states can be badly misvalued even when the TD loss is small. Multi-step TD shortens this chain by following the dataset trajectory for several steps; however, since the behavior policy is often suboptimal, every followed action adds a bias, and no fixed number of steps suits a whole trajectory. HQL extends a target through a dataset action only when the critic values it at least as highly as the best-of-N actions sampled from the BC policy, and otherwise falls back to a one-step target. We show that this rule is a principled off-policy correction that needs no action densities, which makes HQL compatible with expressive flow and diffusion policies. Experiments show that HQL avoids the failures of both one-step and multi-step TD on a combination-lock task across horizons, and outperforms prior horizon-reduction methods on long-horizon OGBench and OCBench tasks.

## 91. MoE-DAgger: Transition-Aware Router-Entropy Gating for Selective Human Correction in Phase-Structured Manipulation

<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Selective data acquisition in Dataset Aggregation (DAgger) on robots requires deciding which learner rollouts to correct and when to intervene.

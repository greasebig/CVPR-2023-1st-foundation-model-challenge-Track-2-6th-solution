## 1. Do You Miss the Nights We Spent Crafting Figures? Rethinking Teaser and Framework Figures in the Age of Generative AI

**中文标题**: Do You Miss the Nights We Spent Crafting Figures? Rethinking Teaser and Framework Figures in the Age of Generative AI

**作者**: Xiaoqi Zhao, Youwei Pang, Kailai Zhou

**发布日期**: 2026-11-05

**arXiv ID**: [2611.08486v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08486v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.08486v1)

**项目主页**: https://ntu-ai4x.github.io/Rethinking_Figures/index_en.html.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Scientific figures give research ideas a visual form through choices of abstraction, composition, and emphasis. Generative AI offers new ways to realize these choices, raising questions about explanatory clarity and purposeful visual expression. We examine these questions through a visual retrospective of 371 motivation and framework figures from 200 papers. The corpus comprises a quota-based main sample of 160 papers spanning four temporal periods and a separate purposive style-contrast sample of 40 papers. A single multimodal judge evaluates six dimensions of communication and design, with three repeated judgments per figure. We attempt source-constrained revisions of all 371 figures, of which 369 pass the recorded fidelity checks. Original-figure ratings show no uniform decline across periods. Revisions increase mean clarity ratings by 0.428 points and decrease aesthetic coherence ratings by 0.111 points on a five-point scale. Gains in the six-dimension average are larger for main-sample motivation figures than for frameworks. Case analyses show that added explanation can clarify comparisons while leaving typographic hierarchy and visual economy unresolved. These model-based findings support researcher-led design, with AI assisting exploration and authors retaining responsibility for explanatory purpose, composition, and scientific relationships. The accompanying atlas and evaluation records are available at https://ntu-ai4x.github.io/Rethinking_Figures/index_en.html.

## 2. EgoGeneralist: Unifying Egocentric Action-Video Tasks through Video-Native Modeling

**中文标题**: EgoGeneralist: Unifying Egocentric Action-Video Tasks through Video-Native Modeling

**作者**: Yanlin Jin, Bowen Yang, Cheng Zhang, Yifan Yang, Zhe Li et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-05

**arXiv ID**: [2611.08477v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08477v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.08477v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Modern video models possess strong visual priors, yet do not natively represent human action in metric 3D space. We present EgoGeneralist, which adapts pretrained video generators to unify action-conditioned egocentric video generation, pose estimation, and joint action-video generation. Our role-adaptive action renderer provides video-native representations for multidirectional action-video learning, making metric motion usable as both a spatial control signal and a recoverable generation target. Cross-stream action-video coupling establishes close correspondence between metric motion and visual dynamics through lightweight adaptation while preserving the pretrained visual prior. Together, these designs establish a generalist framework spanning five action-video tasks. Despite adaptation only on controlled laboratory recordings, EgoGeneralist generalizes to diverse unseen scenes and interactions with effective action control and visual quality, bridging pretrained visual knowledge and metric 3D human motion.

## 3. ManimCode: Learning Visual Self-Correction for Educational Animation

**中文标题**: ManimCode: Learning Visual Self-Correction for Educational Animation

**作者**: Zhengyang Liang, Lizi Liao

**发布日期**: 2026-11-05

**arXiv ID**: [2611.08294v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08294v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.08294v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Manim supports precise, programmatic video generation, but a successfully rendered educational animation can still have visual defects, missing explanations, or incorrect content. Rendering does not directly identify the code changes needed to correct these visual failures, so feedback-driven revision is needed. Existing agentic frameworks use iterative rendering and revision but typically rely on large models, often proprietary. Existing Manim datasets primarily contain final programs and often lack quality verification and the trajectories of feedback and revisions needed to learn this refinement process. We introduce ManimData and ManimCode, a data and training framework for feedback-driven educational animation generation. We construct ManimData through a render-guided pipeline that iteratively generates, executes, reviews, and revises Manim programs with retrieved API documentation. Its 24,034 render-verified, quality-filtered records preserve intermediate programs, execution errors, visual critiques, and corrective revisions. We use role-conditioned supervised fine-tuning on these trajectories to train ManimCode, a single 4B model, to write, review, and revise animation programs. To improve correction of errors that differ from those in the collected trajectories, we then train the Reviser with reinforcement learning on self-generated states, using rendering outcomes and reference-free quality feedback. ManimCode achieves 96.6% and 97.0% render success on ManimData-test and ManimBench, compared with 52.6% and 82.0% for the untrained 4B model under the same inference setup, while also improving video quality scores from 2.48 to 2.94 and from 3.00 to 3.44. Integrating ManimCode into two long-form educational video generation systems increases completed videos from 19/117 to 107/117 and from 53/239 to 239/239, alongside improvements in educational effectiveness and visual quality.

</details>

## 4. FreeOcc: Decoupling Ego-Motion for Efficient 4D Occupancy Forecasting via Continuous Flow Matching

**中文标题**: FreeOcc: Decoupling Ego-Motion for Efficient 4D Occupancy Forecasting via Continuous Flow Matching

**作者**: Zeping Zhang, Zhuoya Zhao, Samy Metari, Robert Laganière

**发布日期**: 2026-11-05

**arXiv ID**: [2611.08128v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08128v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.08128v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Current 4D occupancy world models use heavy cross-attention or deep recurrence to disentangle ego-motion from scene dynamics. This requires significant computational capacity. However, this effort is largely redundant since the ego-vehicle's intended future motion is provided by the downstream motion planner. We present FreeOcc, a lightweight continuous-time world model that introduces explicit $SE(2)$ geometric decoupling into a conditional flow-matching framework. By mathematically factoring out self-motion, we create a purely dynamic feature space. This space supports attention-free temporal fusion and allows physics-constrained ODEs to operate strictly on residual agent kinematics. To maximize deployment efficiency, FreeOcc introduces an uncertainty-aware blending mechanism. This routes well-observed voxels to a single-pass decoder, reserving the ODE solver exclusively for ambiguous, high-uncertainty regions. Requiring only 28.3M parameters-roughly 40% of comparable models-our optimal ensemble mode (FreeOcc-E) runs at 27 FPS. On nuScenes, FreeOcc achieves state-of-the-art forecasting performance with a 0.47 m Near Field Chamfer Distance (NFCD) and 32.1% mIoU. Furthermore, the model transfers zero-shot to SemanticKITTI. It also proves highly robust, retaining 92.8% of its ideal mIoU under significant sensor packet loss and timestamp jitter.

</details>

## 5. Towards Practical 20 kbps Generative Video Compression via Adaptive Tokenization and Entropy Modeling

**中文标题**: Towards Practical 20 kbps Generative Video Compression via Adaptive Tokenization and Entropy Modeling

**作者**: Jiancheng Zhao, Jixiang Luo, Haibin Huang, Chi Zhang, Xuelong Li

**发布日期**: 2026-11-05

**arXiv ID**: [2611.08099v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08099v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.08099v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video compression at ultra-low bitrates remains challenging due to the loss of perceptually important details under limited bit budgets. Recent generative video compression methods leverage pretrained video priors to improve perceptual quality and temporal coherence, but many follow a compress-then-generate paradigm, where generative priors are primarily exploited during reconstruction. Moreover, representations learned for generation and reconstruction are not inherently optimized for entropy coding.   In this work, we propose \method, which adapts pretrained video representations into compression-oriented representations while preserving their reconstruction capability. Specifically, we adapt the pretrained causal video VAE of MiniMax-H3 into a discrete video tokenizer and develop a small autoregressive entropy model that captures local and global token dependencies, with grouped prediction for parallel decoding. To further improve coding efficiency, we jointly optimize the tokenizer and entropy model using a differentiable rate surrogate, allowing rate gradients to adapt token assignments toward lower coding costs while preserving reconstruction quality. Experiments on UVG, MCL-JCV, and HEVC Class B demonstrate superior perceptual rate--distortion performance, achieving BD-LPIPS savings of 82.2\%--87.5\% and BD-DISTS savings of 78.3\%--85.0\% relative to GLC-Video.

## 6. Spatiotemporal Dynamics-Aware Industrial Anomaly Detection and Reasoning

**中文标题**: Spatiotemporal Dynamics-Aware Industrial Anomaly Detection and Reasoning

**作者**: S-Wei Chen, Siangyu Yang, Yi-Chen Jan, Chun-Yi Lee, Shao-Yuan Lo

**发布日期**: 2026-11-05

**arXiv ID**: [2611.08080v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08080v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.08080v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Industrial Anomaly Detection (IAD) is a long-established problem that aims to detect defects in industrial products during manufacturing for quality control. Most existing studies focus on static visual patterns as the basis for detection. However, in real manufacturing environments, many anomalies are dynamics-based, such as irregular movements that cannot be perceived from static cues alone. We observe that even recent Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM)-based approaches fall short in identifying such dynamic anomalies due to limited temporal modeling capability. To address this, we introduce MUSTARD: MUltimodal SpatioTemporal Anomaly Reasoning and Detection, a framework that equips MLLMs with fine-grained spatiotemporal dynamics awareness for detecting and reasoning about dynamics-based defects. MUSTARD introduces optical-flow-guided keyframe selection and augmented modalities to capture critical motion irregularities through local motion patterns and global dynamic evolution. Its normal pattern-based reasoning pipeline requires only a few normal reference samples as the detection basis, avoiding any model updates. This enables efficient adaptation across various products and provides high decision interpretability. MUSTARD is the first MLLM-based framework for dynamics-based IAD. Experiments demonstrate its superior performance in detecting spatiotemporal anomalies and providing plausible explanations. The code will be made publicly available.

</details>

**项目主页**: https://fudan-generative-vision.github.io/WAM-Sim.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Autoregressive driving world models systematically suffer from structural drift, identity drift, and quality decay over long rollout horizons due to transient conditioning signals that lack persistent state. We present WAM-Sim, a driving world model anchored by a persistent hierarchical memory and optimized via online flow-matching RL. WAM-Sim structures persistent context across three temporal and semantic scales: pose-aligned structural tokens encoding persistent road topology, read-only scene tokens supplying geolocated background appearance, and write-once instance tokens preserving individual traffic participant identities across occlusions and chunk boundaries. To restore visual sharpness and physical plausibility degraded by multi-scale conditioning, we introduce an online post-training framework that optimizes self-generated rollouts via flow-matching RL guided by hierarchy-aligned visual rewards and a vision-language model physical judge. The invariant memory empirically confines policy optimization within the anchored context, helping suppress semantic collapse and reward hacking, while online RL systematically enforces physical commonsense and fine-grained visual fidelity. On the nuScenes benchmark, WAM-Sim achieves a superior generation fidelity (FID=3.47, FVD=25.72) and strong downstream perception performance (24.16 mAP, 71.23 road mIoU). On minute-scale rollouts of self-collected data, WAM-Sim substantially mitigates structural and identity drift, preserving high scene similarity and subject identity coherence across extended horizons. Project page: https://fudan-generative-vision.github.io/WAM-Sim.

## 8. EDGAR: Efficient Diffusion-Driven Gaussian Avatar Refinement via Synthetic Failure

**中文标题**: EDGAR: Efficient Diffusion-Driven Gaussian Avatar Refinement via Synthetic Failure

**作者**: Kwan Yun, Minhyeok Bang, Colette Suhjung Koo, Junyong Noh

**发布日期**: 2026-11-05

**arXiv ID**: [2611.07909v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07909v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07909v1)

**项目主页**: https://kwanyun.github.io/edgar/

**类别**: cs.GR, cs.CV, cs.LG, cs.MM

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Recent advances in Gaussian splatting have enabled efficient and photorealistic head avatar reconstruction, yet monocular video-based or image-based methods still suffer from under-constrained geometry and texture, leading to artifacts such as blurring, stretching, and temporal instability. We present EDGAR (Efficient Diffusion-Driven Gaussian Avatar Refinement), a conditional diffusion framework designed to enhance the quality of Gaussian avatars by correcting artifacts while preserving identity fidelity. To train EDGAR, we construct EDGAR-SET, a hybrid triplet dataset composed of real-captured and synthetic-rendered samples, which simulates realistic Gaussian avatar failure cases by pairing degraded renderings with ground-truth and reference images. EDGAR refines degraded frames through a fusion block that injects identity features extracted by the self-supervised Sapiens model into a one-step diffusion backbone, enabling effective artifact removal without sacrificing efficiency. Our EDGAR model incorporates a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture to address diverse degradation types and severity levels. The refined multi-view renderings are subsequently used as pseudo-targets to optimize a single underlying Gaussian avatar, so that the final avatar can be rendered without applying EDGAR to every output frame. Experiments on three different Gaussian avatar reconstruction methods, each evaluated under three different settings, show improvements in both perceptual and reconstruction measures over the raw outputs and an SDEdit-style refinement baseline. In addition, cross-view evaluation demonstrates improved calibrated consistency, while tests under large yaw and open-mouth expressions verify robustness under extreme settings. These results collectively indicate a useful operating regime for recoverable appearance artifacts. The code is available at https://kwanyun.github.io/edgar/

## 9. XM4D: 4D Reconstruction through Certifiable Geometric Optimization

**中文标题**: XM4D: 4D Reconstruction through Certifiable Geometric Optimization

**作者**: Haoyu Han, Jack Benarroch Jedlicki, Jiahui Lei, Heng Yang

**发布日期**: 2026-11-05

**arXiv ID**: [2611.07902v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07902v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07902v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.RO, math.OC

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Accurate 4D reconstruction remains challenging: jointly estimating camera motion, scene geometry, and non-rigid deformation entails highly nonconvex optimization, making global optimality difficult to guarantee. We present XM4D, a framework for accurate and efficient 4D reconstruction built upon certifiably globally optimal geometric optimization. By separating the estimation of camera motion from non-rigid deformation, we formulate both stages as structured quadratically constrained quadratic programs (QCQP) that admit semidefinite programming (SDP) relaxations and verifiable global optimality certificates. For camera motion estimation, we lift static point tracks into 3D using foundational depth prediction models and formulate a scaled bundle adjustment problem that jointly estimates camera poses, depth scales, and static landmarks. By analytically eliminating translations and landmarks, we reduce the problem to a QCQP over scaled rotations. For non-rigid reconstruction, we represent dynamic motion using an embedded deformation graph (EDG) and show that its tracking, rigidity, and temporal regularization objectives similarly reduce to a rotation-only QCQP. Both problems admit SDP relaxations that we solve efficiently with a GPU-accelerated Burer-Monteiro solver based on Riemannian optimization. The recovered geometry further initializes dynamic Gaussian splatting for photometric refinement. Extensive experiments across seven benchmarks demonstrate accurate camera motion estimation on both static and dynamic scenes, efficient optimization that scales to thousands of frames, and state-of-the-art novel-view rendering quality.

</details>

## 10. PVDepth: Panoramic Video Depth Estimation via Geometry-Aware Spatiotemporal Adaptation

**中文标题**: PVDepth: Panoramic Video Depth Estimation via Geometry-Aware Spatiotemporal Adaptation

**作者**: Chuanxin Song, Peixi Peng

**发布日期**: 2026-11-05

**arXiv ID**: [2611.07873v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07873v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07873v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Panoramic video depth estimation is pivotal for applications such as Virtual Reality and World Models. However, advancements in this field are impeded by two primary obstacles: the scarcity of large-scale training data and the unique spatiotemporal challenges of Equirectangular Projection (ERP), which hinder the direct transfer of perspective models. In this paper, we first present PanoCARLA, a large-scale synthetic RGB-D panoramic video dataset, featuring natural motion trajectories and drone-like roaming perspectives. ## 31. Ordering Multivariate Time Series by Volatility Memory: A Temporal Convolutional Approach to Volatility Heredity

**中文标题**: Ordering Multivariate Time Series by Volatility Memory: A Temporal Convolutional Approach to Volatility Heredity

**作者**: Kaoutar El Azaar, Mohamed Hamlich, Moncef Garouani, Franck Ravat, Asmae Chakir et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-05

**arXiv ID**: [2611.07746v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07746v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07746v1)

**类别**: cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Volatility dynamics can carry information about temporal relationships that is not apparent in the conditional mean. Extracting this information is difficult because the persistence of each variable can obscure its dependence on other variables. This paper presents QS-TCN, a method for ordering multivariate time series that combines statistical estimates of volatility memory with temporal convolutional learning. Persistence estimates obtained by quasi-maximum likelihood and temporal modes extracted by the Matrix Pencil method define structured filters within the network. Together with learned filters, they represent the history of the target and support comparisons of directional evidence between variables. These comparisons are combined into a global order of precedence, which does not identify a causal graph. The method is evaluated on synthetic systems with linear and nonlinear volatility interactions and on the Finance and fMRI benchmarks, whose reference relations are known. Across the four synthetic volatility regimes studied, QS-TCN reaches a higher mean ordering quality than an external lead--lag ordering and its squared-input adaptation, although the gain over the adaptation is uncertain in two regimes. Applications to currency, gold and cryptocurrency markets are reported descriptively.

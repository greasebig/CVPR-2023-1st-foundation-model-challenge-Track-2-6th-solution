## 1. FastFrames: Video Generation at Interactive Latency

**中文标题**: FastFrames: Video Generation at Interactive Latency

**作者**: Gene Chou, Guandao Yang, Fujun Luan, Lars Mescheder, Wei Dong et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-17

**arXiv ID**: [2611.23226v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23226v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23226v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Autoregressive video diffusion models generate frames based on new observations and inputs, making them a promising medium for interactive visual experiences. However, existing models are orders of magnitude too slow for fluid interaction. This paper introduces FastFrames, a family of fast autoregressive video diffusion models that achieves interactive latency without compromising quality. We profile the inference pipeline of state-of-the-art models and find that operations often treated as fixed implementation details, such as VAE decoding and KV cache updates, in fact account for over half of end-to-end inference latency. We challenge the assumption that these components are indispensable and replace them with lightweight alternatives. Our 1.3B variant runs at 36 ms per frame, or 28 responses per second (RPS), on a single B200 GPU while matching the generation quality of a state-of-the-art model that is nearly 8x slower. Our 14B variant runs at 45 ms per frame, or 22 RPS, on an 8x B200 GPU node and yields significantly better visual quality and realism. We show that FastFrames enables real-time interaction in applications such as camera-conditioned and physics-conditioned video generation.

</details>

## 2. VIVID: Beyond Static Multi-Image Reasoning with Localized Visual Differences and Textual Entity Dynamics in Video MLLMs

**中文标题**: VIVID: Beyond Static Multi-Image Reasoning with Localized Visual Differences and Textual Entity Dynamics in Video MLLMs

**作者**: Chengqian Ma, Yuanze Zeng, Hongyu Wang, Mingfeng Lin, YiCheng Xiao et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-17

**arXiv ID**: [2611.23153v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23153v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23153v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video question answering under tight frame budgets requires preserving localized motion and event order, yet current video multimodal large language models (MLLMs) reduce comprehension to static multi-image reasoning by discarding unselected frames and encoding keyframes independently: (1) Spatially, whole-frame pooling dilutes small moving targets into static backgrounds; (2) Temporally, dropping intermediate frames or averaging tokens erases inter-frame motion, confusing direction, speed, and order. We propose VIVID (Visual Inter-frame Variations and Individual Dynamics), which moves beyond static snapshots like human vision: keyframes capture core states while inter-frame Patch Diffs preserve neighboring changes within $K_f$ keyframes. Autonomous Delta-Driven Keyframe Planning (ADKP) selects anchors from a $3\text{ fps}$ pool and feeds surrounding patch differences into Dual-Road Change Memory (DRCM), forming Dynamic Tokens at zero extra anchor tokens alongside a camera-stabilized Active Motion Region of Interest (ROI) and chronological Entity State Chains. Adding $0.22\%$ trainable parameters to a frozen $27\text{B}$ backbone, VIVID-Small ($50$ examples, $\sim 1.5$ GPU hours) beats all eleven baselines in overall accuracy across $8,083$ questions and trails only VIVID-Large ($3,600$ examples), which leads on MotionBench, Video-MME, and TempCompass.

</details>

## 3. Event-Based Dynamic Lip Region Estimation During Speech

**中文标题**: Event-Based Dynamic Lip Region Estimation During Speech

**作者**: Hamze Hammami, Omar Eldardeer, Francesco Rea

**发布日期**: 2026-11-17

**arXiv ID**: [2611.23111v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23111v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23111v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Event cameras offer high temporal resolution and low power consumption, making them promising for speech-related applications. These include automatic lip reading, audiovisual speech analysis, and human-robot interaction, particularly in challenging environments characterized by low illumination or high dynamic range. Event-based lip reading, speaker recognition, and voice activity detection, however, mostly operate on a cropped region of interest (ROI), by hand or by the dataset, and no event-based method supplies that ROI as a spatial region; existing event-based face analysis gives the mouth as landmarks, which presents a mismatch between application and localization. The present work predicts a dense heatmap over the lip region from the full event "frame". Events are encoded as a polarity-separated voxel grid and processed at every location by a two-pathway continuous-time recurrent network (CT-RNN) with learned, log-space bounded time constants acting on the real elapsed time of each bin: a stateless fast pathway and a stateful slow pathway, utilised for refining and anchoring the prediction. The model reaches 69.5% on lips on four unseen speakers and switches target only 2.1 times per second, against 8.9 and 11.0 for convolutional baselines with   more parameters. Adding a spatial encoder improves precision but fails on a new face scale. On a real event sensor, trained only on simulated events, the proposed model reaches 58.0% on lips against 43.2% for the CNN, with fewer than half its target switches.

</details>

## 4. Transfer Once, Ask Locally: Compact Audio-Visual Memory for On-Device Question Answering

**中文标题**: Transfer Once, Ask Locally: Compact Audio-Visual Memory for On-Device Question Answering

**作者**: Qitong Wang, Jihui Jin, Divya Kothandaraman, Vishnu Raj, Christopher Rasmussen

**发布日期**: 2026-11-17

**arXiv ID**: [2611.23085v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23085v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23085v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Audio-visual question answering on lightweight user devices requires balancing speed, memory usage, privacy, and communication. One approach is to run audio-visual encoders on a server and a lightweight language decoder on the user device, keeping questions local while offloading media encoding. However, transferring and processing the full multimodal representation remains costly. We propose a training-free, query-agnostic token pruning framework that constructs a compact, reusable memory before transmission. We define video spatio-temporal blocks and aligned audio windows as players and estimate their importance through Monte Carlo Shapley valuation. We use an embedding coverage objective that combines query-independent utility with cross-modal redundancy calibration and rewards unimodal diversity through marginal coverage gains. We retain the original token embeddings of high-scoring players under a limited token budget and transfer the resulting memory once for reuse across subsequent local questions. Experiments on Video-MME, Daily-Omni, and Video-Holmes using video-SALMONN 2+ (3B) and video-SALMONN 2 Pro (4B) show that our method achieves the best or tied-best QA accuracy among the compared compression methods across all evaluated settings. On Daily-Omni with video-SALMONN 2+ (3B) at 80\% pruning, our method improves accuracy by 12.86--14.62 percentage points over these methods while achieving the lowest device-side LLM prefill and decoding latency, with up to $1.92\times$ decoding speedup at comparable embedding memory usage.

</details>

## 5. UnrealEvent: Synthetic Dataset Generation and Annotation for Event-Based Vision

**中文标题**: UnrealEvent: Synthetic Dataset Generation and Annotation for Event-Based Vision

**作者**: Yentel Schoovaerts, Zijie Ning, Patrick Vandewalle

**发布日期**: 2026-11-17

**arXiv ID**: [2611.23078v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23078v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23078v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Event-based cameras provide high temporal resolution, low power consumption, and a high dynamic range, but progress in event-based object detection remains constrained by the scarcity of annotated training data. To solve this issue, this paper introduces the tool UnrealEvent, a synthetic dataset-generation pipeline implemented in Unreal Engine 5.5. The pipeline combines a V2E-inspired event-frame generator with EasySynth semantic-rendering passes, allowing rendered level sequences to be converted into paired event representations and YOLO-format bounding-box annotations without manual labeling. Events are generated from per-pixel log-luminance differences between consecutive frames, with contrast thresholds sampled independently from a normal distribution to emulate the fabrication-induced threshold mismatch of real sensors. Using this pipeline, we provide a synthetic dataset and evaluate YOLOv8 detectors under synthetic, zero-shot real, and real fine-tuning training on eTraM. The results show strong in-domain synthetic performance but a substantial synthetic-to-real gap, especially for pedestrians. Fine-tuning on a small real subset improves real-domain performance, while the remaining gap highlights the importance of asset diversity and sensor calibration.

</details>

## 6. FrameRouter: Frame Budget Routing for Long-Video Understanding on Video-MME-v2 and Beyond

**中文标题**: FrameRouter: Frame Budget Routing for Long-Video Understanding on Video-MME-v2 and Beyond

**作者**: Bingjun Luo, Jialin Guo, Siqi Li

**发布日期**: 2026-11-17

**arXiv ID**: [2611.23055v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23055v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23055v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/bingjunluo/FrameRouter.

**类别**: cs.AI, cs.CV, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Long-video question answering benchmarks like Video-MME-v2 typically evaluate systems under a uniform frame budget for every question, despite stark variation in the evidence each query actually demands. We analyze this mismatch on Video-MME-v2 and find that different question subsets prefer different frozen frame-budget policies, while uniformly increasing the frame count does not reliably improve all metrics. These findings motivate fixed-mean frame-budget routing, a controlled inference setting in which each question may receive a different frame budget while the average budget matches a uniform reference. We introduce FrameRouter, a training-free test-time framework that combines an evidence-demand router, a budget-constrained allocation rule, and a frozen bank of frame-sampling policies. By controlling how much visual evidence each question receives while leaving the underlying samplers unchanged, FrameRouter is orthogonal to query-aware frame selection. Across Video-MME-v2 and several long-video understanding benchmarks, FrameRouter improves long-video question answering by using the same average visual budget more selectively across questions. Code is available at https://github.com/bingjunluo/FrameRouter.

</details>

## 7. When a Larger Guidance Scale Repairs Text-to-Video Failures and When It Does Not

**中文标题**: When a Larger Guidance Scale Repairs Text-to-Video Failures and When It Does Not

**作者**: Taewon Kang, Matthias Zwicker

**发布日期**: 2026-11-17

**arXiv ID**: [2611.23025v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23025v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23025v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

When a text-to-video diffusion model ignores part of a prompt, the usual first fix is to raise the classifier-free guidance scale. This paper is a measurement study of when that fix works. On Mochi, the same increase repairs failures on rare compositions ("a snowy beach") beyond what generating again does, does not repair negations ("a highway with no vehicles"), where it fixes fewer failures than generating again does, and changes nothing for events that must happen late in a clip ("a sandcastle that a wave washes flat"). Which of these happens can be known in advance, from the prompt and one inexpensive score. The score, which we call demand, measures how strongly the denoiser reacts to the part of the prompt that the failed video leaves out, and it is read from the first quarter of denoising, before any video exists. Our main finding is a condition: raising the scale is effective only when the prompt contains no negation and no event tied to a specific time, and when demand is sufficiently high, with recovery clearly higher above a fitted threshold than below it. Published corrections for the three failure types, used as fixed probes, behave as this account predicts. A short decision rule follows: before generating again, a user can tell whether the most common fix is worth trying, and what to use when it is not.

</details>

## 8. The Rhythm of Generated Video: Latent Chunking Leaves Periodic Traces in Compressed Bitstreams

**中文标题**: The Rhythm of Generated Video: Latent Chunking Leaves Periodic Traces in Compressed Bitstreams

**作者**: Chen Ye Gan, Bryan Zhen Zhang, Gene Wen, Yuxing han

**发布日期**: 2026-11-17

**arXiv ID**: [2611.22996v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22996v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22996v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Most AI-generated video detectors analyze decoded pixels and overlook how videos are produced. We show that causal video autoencoders leave an architecture-specific rhythm in compressed bitstreams: their temporal stride concentrates unpredictable content at latent-chunk boundaries, so rate-distortion optimized encoders allocate bits periodically. Without decoding pixels, we observe four-frame pulses in CogVideoX and Wan2.2 and eight-frame pulses in LTX-Video, which are absent from content-matched camera footage. Passing camera footage through a generator's autoencoder alone induces the pulse, supporting its proposed origin. A training-free peak-ratio statistic separates generated from camera video (AUC 0.84-0.94) and persists across tested quantization settings and H.264/H.265 encoding. Frame-size traces also identify four commercial generators, with 89.2%, 84.1%, and 83.0% accuracy under H.264, H.265, and AV1, beating the pixel-based UnivFD baseline (72.7-73.6%). Our results directly link generative architecture to measurable coding demand.

</details>

## 9. Training-Free Building Change Detection with Example-Induced Discriminants

**作者**: Zhi Lu, Bin Hong, Zhenming Peng

**发布日期**: 2026-11-17

**arXiv ID**: [2611.22964v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22964v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22964v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/lzcn/lind

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Building change detection (BCD) relies on dense annotations that are costly to collect across regions. A few annotated examples offer a more economical way to specify the target, but change maps provide incomplete building supervision: they label only changed buildings and leave the building-containing date unspecified. Our key observation is that these annotations nevertheless reveal features that recognize buildings in other images, including unchanged ones. We present Linear INduced Discriminants (LIND), a training-free framework that turns regional feature contrasts into a reusable linear building classifier. Using a single frozen vision transformer, it unifies building recognition and temporal comparison in the same feature space. This approach requires neither text prompts nor an auxiliary segmentation model. Only one parameter is tuned on a single validation dataset and then kept fixed across datasets and shot settings. Extensive experiments demonstrate strong detection accuracy, cross-dataset transfer, and efficient inference, with extensions to other semantic classes and broader land-cover changes. Change annotations thus provide a compact visual specification for detection in new scenes. Code: https://github.com/lzcn/lind

</details>

## 10. Code World Model 2.0: Towards Infinite Controllable Video Generation

**中文标题**: Code World Model 2.0: Towards Infinite Controllable Video Generation

**作者**: Ruoyu Wang, Hao Zhong, Yanming Yang, Jiahao Tian, Lin Liu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-17

**arXiv ID**: [2611.22925v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22925v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22925v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Visual proxies decouple explicit world-state simulation from photorealistic video synthesis, offering a controllable interface for both offline clip creation and real-time interactive streaming. Yet a practical renderer must address two challenges. Upstream proxies vary from coarse layouts to fine articulated geometry depending on the interaction, and streaming inference must operate in a few causal steps without accumulating drift across self-generated history. We present Invoker-Render, pairing a bidirectional creation model with a causal four-step streaming model under a shared conditioning interface over structural proxies, an appearance reference, and language. Trained on an aligned approximately 200-hour multi-granularity corpus, Invoker-Render honors fine geometric constraints when supplied while synthesizing plausible local motion under coarse proxies, enabling runtime selection of input detail. To bridge bidirectional creation and real-time streaming without compounding step compression and rollout drift, we introduce Progressive Forcing (ProForcing). ProForcing first trains a causal predictor and distills it into a four-step renderer using histories derived from training videos. Training then shifts to continuous self-generated rollouts. A pipelined serving stack supports efficient streaming inference. Experiments show versatile control across proxy families, granularities, and camera paths, consistent gains over recent streaming baselines, and stable 30-minute racing rollouts that preserve scene and subject appearance.

</details>

Cached tokens have their keys and values computed once and reused, and kept tokens are processed as usual. ## 100. Streaming across viewing conditions - The effect of viewing distance and display luminance on video quality

**发布日期**: 2026-11-15

<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

The visibility of streamed video distortions is affected by the user's display and viewing conditions, such as their display's luminance or the viewing distance. Distortions become less visible at lower luminance levels or farther viewing distances. This allowed us to identify a selection of existing quality metrics that can be effectively adapted to account for the viewing conditions.

## 102. DiffWAM: Learning World Action Model from Consecutive Visual Difference

**中文标题**: DiffWAM: Learning World Action Model from Consecutive Visual Difference

**作者**: Xinheng Zhang, Haoyu Xiong, Chao Jing, Xiaohan Zhang, Linshan Li et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-15

**arXiv ID**: [2611.19904v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19904v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.19904v1)

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

World Action Models (WAMs) have emerged as an important paradigm in robot learning, jointly predicting future scene states and robot actions from current observations and instructions. However, future states entangle task-related changes with large amounts of task-irrelevant content, such as static backgrounds, which weakens the model's ability to capture changes relevant to task execution. To address this, we propose Difference World Action Model (DiffWAM), a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) framework that enhances world representations by jointly learning future-state prediction and consecutive visual difference prediction. We define consecutive visual differences as differences between the latent representations of consecutive observations, providing an explicit learning signal for temporal variation. The framework comprises a World Expert for future frame prediction, an Action Expert for action generation, and a Diff Expert for predicting these latent differences along the trajectory. A structured unidirectional attention mask conditions the Diff, World, and Action Experts solely on the current-frame representation while blocking attention between experts. This design allows both the World and Diff Experts to be removed during inference, introducing no additional inference overhead. DiffWAM achieves state-of-the-art performance on the LIBERO and RoboTwin 2.0 benchmarks and substantially improves over FastWAM on real-world manipulation tasks. Specifically, it attains average success rates of 98.8%, 92.65%, and 88.3%, outperforming the state-of-the-art baseline (i.e., FastWAM) by 1.75, 1.55, and 15.0 percentage points, respectively.

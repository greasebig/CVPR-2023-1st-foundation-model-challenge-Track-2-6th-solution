## 1. Gaspard: Expressive unified speech and gesture synthesis with continuous autoregressive flow-matching and decoupled classifier-free guidance

**中文标题**: Gaspard: Expressive unified speech and gesture synthesis with continuous autoregressive flow-matching and decoupled classifier-free guidance

**作者**: Téo Guichoux, Théodor Lemerle, Catherine Pelachaud, Nicolas Obin

**发布日期**: 2026-11-18

**arXiv ID**: [2611.24862v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24862v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24862v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Human communication involves highly coordinated multimodal signals such as speech and co-speech gestures. Existing approaches for generating both modalities commonly follow a cascaded pipeline, where speech is generated first and subsequently used to drive gesture generation; speech generation is therefore independent of gestures. We introduce Gaspard, a unified speech--gesture generation model based on continuous autoregressive generation with next-step flow-matching predictions, and trained on Seamless Interaction, a large-scale multimodal dataset. The heterogeneous temporal properties of speech and gesture motivate modality-specific prediction heads and decoupled classifier-free guidance over the two generation streams. This guidance rebalances the influence of speech and gesture at inference time, improving expressivity while mitigating dynamics collapse in continuous autoregressive gesture generation. Objective and perceptual evaluations show that Gaspard is a strong gesture generation model against unimodal baselines and that it produces highly synchronous speech and gestures compared to a matched cascaded system.

</details>

## 2. A$^3$Bench: Compositional Reasoning over Aggressive Interactions in Videos

**中文标题**: A$^3$Bench: Compositional Reasoning over Aggressive Interactions in Videos

**作者**: Austin C Baker, Raiyaan Abdullah, Daniel Zeev Yoffe, Shruti Vyas, Yogesh S Rawat

**发布日期**: 2026-11-18

**arXiv ID**: [2611.24762v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24762v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24762v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/austincbaker/A3Bench.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Recent Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have achieved strong performance on general video understanding benchmarks, yet their ability to reason about participant roles in aggressive interactions remains largely unexplored. Existing violence and crime datasets primarily evaluate whether aggression occurs, without testing whether models can identify who performed the action, who received it, and how participants are related within the scene. To address this gap, we introduce A$^3$Bench, a benchmark to study aggressive action and association in videos. The benchmark evaluates whether models can jointly recognize aggressive actions, distinguish aggressors from victims and bystanders, and correctly bind actions to participants under challenging distractor settings. We benchmark 17 multimodal models and find that they struggle substantially on fine-grained aggressive-scene understanding. Performance degrades consistently as reasoning complexity increases, with models frequently confusing aggressors with victims or bystanders, revealing a systematic failure in role-action association rather than coarse aggression recognition alone. We further observe that increasing model scale or generic reasoning models experience limited improvement. Finally, we explore a simple role-graph prompting strategy that encourages structured reasoning over participant interactions, leading to improvements in compositional reasoning. Our findings highlight that reliable aggressive-scene understanding remains a major challenge for current MLLMs and motivate future research on role-aware video reasoning beyond coarse violence detection. Code and dataset: https://github.com/austincbaker/A3Bench.

</details>

## 3. HandWorldBench: A Unified Benchmark for Hand-Centric World Models

**中文标题**: HandWorldBench: A Unified Benchmark for Hand-Centric World Models

**作者**: Zhaolu Wang, Jiacheng Xu, Lihong Wang, Shuqing Wang, Jianjie Fang et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-18

**arXiv ID**: [2611.24731v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24731v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24731v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Recent advances in world models are opening new possibilities for fine-grained embodied tasks, with hand-object interaction offering a particularly demanding setting. Modeling these interactions involves capturing how hand actions change objects and how the resulting states persist over time. Yet systematic evaluation tailored to these hand-centric capabilities remains underdeveloped. To address this gap, we introduce HandWorldBench, a unified benchmark that systematically evaluates hand-centric world models across human hands, simulated dexterous hands, and real dexterous hands along six dimensions: visual quality, hand fidelity, appearance consistency, instruction following, physical consistency, and temporal coherence. To construct the benchmark, we develop a data curation pipeline that extracts hand-object interaction clips and screens them for quality, yielding 20,500 high-quality egocentric clips from over 600 hours of footage. We then manually select 500 clips covering seven task families for evaluation. Benchmarking eleven models under a unified protocol reveals that stronger hand-pose control does not consistently translate into coherent object-state evolution and persistence or robust cross-embodiment performance, with several specialized hand-control models underperforming general-purpose text/image-to-video generators on dexterous-hand tasks.

</details>

## 4. H3Remover: Reinforcing Video Object Removal without Paired Data

**作者**: Kangjie Chen, Xiangyu Li, Jinhao Deng, Dongbin Zhang, Chaoda Zheng et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-18

**arXiv ID**: [2611.24633v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24633v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24633v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Pretrained video editors possess rich removal and completion priors, yet may retain targets or alter unrelated content. We present H3Remover, which reinforces MiniMax-H3 Base Ref2VA for video object removal without paired object-free training targets. To discourage superficial edits such as recoloring, a target-removal reward favors calibrated depth changes consistent with revealing farther content. To limit unintended scene changes, a background-geometry reward encourages preservation of relative depth ordering. To preserve background appearance without retaining unwanted shadows or reflections, an appearance reward constrains RGB changes in source-derived protected regions while exempting targets and estimated effects. Frozen HPSv3 complements these constraints with perceptual-quality feedback for synthesized content. Evaluation regions remain fixed across candidates, and DiffusionART optimizes LoRA adapters with the backbone frozen. Experiments show substantial reconstruction gains over original H3, with qualitative comparisons demonstrating more effective object removal and better preservation of surrounding scene content.

## 5. SCRiD: Spatial and Contextual References for Generalizable AI-Generated Video Detection

**中文标题**: SCRiD: Spatial and Contextual References for Generalizable AI-Generated Video Detection

**作者**: Hao Tan, Zekai Zuo, Boyang Chen, Jinghui Qin

**发布日期**: 2026-11-18

**arXiv ID**: [2611.24524v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24524v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24524v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Generalizable AI-generated video detection remains challenging because natural motion and content variations can resemble synthesis artifacts, making isolated video discrepancies unreliable indicators of authenticity. To address this challenge, we propose Spatial and Contextual Reference-informed Detection (SCRiD), a framework that interprets video variations through complementary spatial and contextual references. Specifically, we introduce Matched-Coordinate Residual Response (MCR) to characterize temporal residual associations under motion and coordinate references with matched subpixel sampling conditions. We further develop Real-Conditioned Score Adaptation (RCSA), which learns a content-conditioned denoising reference from authentic videos and probes its responses across multiple noise scales and quality views to guide feature adaptation. To integrate these complementary sources of evidence, we design Reversible Evidence Exchange (REE) to enable information exchange through reversible symplectic updates. We train SCRiD using synthetic videos from only 4 generators and evaluate it across 88 generator or task subsets spanning 6 benchmarks. Our method achieves average ACC and AP of 72.72% and 74.23%, respectively, outperforming the strongest baseline by 3.24 and 0.81 percentage points.Ablation and robustness experiments further validate the effectiveness of the proposed components and the stability of SCRiD under common post-processing operations.

</details>

## 6. Comparing Each Generation with Independent Siblings Gives Video World Model Post-Training a Proper Objective for Outcome Frequencies

**中文标题**: Comparing Each Generation with Independent Siblings Gives Video World Model Post-Training a Proper Objective for Outcome Frequencies

**作者**: Xin Li, Yiwen Chen

**发布日期**: 2026-11-18

**arXiv ID**: [2611.24512v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24512v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24512v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video world models are used as simulators, so when a first frame leaves several physical outcomes open, repeated generations should produce each outcome as often as it occurs in the data. Physics post-training scores every generation against the single recorded future of its history, which makes single videos more faithful, but the expected score is linear in the model distribution and is maximized by always producing the most likely outcome. The log-likelihood of the recorded outcome is maximized by the data distribution, and its gradient weights agreement with the record by the inverse of how often the model produces the generated outcome, a frequency that other generations of the same history with independent noise, its siblings, can estimate. To train with this weight from a finite group, we propose SibPO, which performs Sibling-paired Policy Optimization: siblings estimate a truncated inverse-frequency weight for agreement with the record, and a second term subtracts the same weight times the agreement with the siblings, which makes the expected objective a strictly proper divergence over outcome cells at every group size. On PisaBench, SibPO lowers the KS distance to simulated landing times by 9.1-10.2% relative to the strongest method outside its reward family on three of four video models without degrading single videos, and it lowers the error of estimated robot policy success rates by 13%.

</details>

## 7. Every Query Routes Itself: Accelerating Video Diffusion Transformers with Per-Query Routed Sparse Attention

**中文标题**: Every Query Routes Itself: Accelerating Video Diffusion Transformers with Per-Query Routed Sparse Attention

**作者**: Zhen Huang, Shuwei Li, Ruizhe Yao, Xinrui Chen, Danyi Liu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-18

**arXiv ID**: [2611.24479v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24479v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24479v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Self-attention over long token sequences dominates the inference cost of video diffusion transformers. Clustering-based sparse attention clusters both queries and keys and lets all queries in a cluster share one selection of key clusters. We show that this sharing limits accuracy: with key clusters and density fixed, attention grows more accurate as the query clusters are refined, and is most accurate when each query selects on its own, which needs no query clustering at all. We therefore propose Per-Query Routed Sparse Attention (PQRS), a training-free method that clusters only keys, lets each query select its own key clusters, and compensates for the unselected ones with their centroids. Per-query selections leave no query tile with a shared key list, so PQRS traverses key clusters instead of queries, buckets queries with identical selections into dense tasks, and merges their partial results in one pass under a fixed softmax offset carried from the previous denoising step. On four text-to-video and image-to-video models from the Wan and HunyuanVideo families, PQRS is the fastest of all the sparse attention methods we evaluate and the closest to the dense output, reaching $28.9$ dB PSNR on Wan2.1-T2V and $35.4$ dB on HunyuanVideo at $1.66$--$2.10\times$ end-to-end speedup.

</details>

## 8. Anchoring with Visual Evidence: Explicit Semantic Anchors for Joint Change Detection and Captioning

**中文标题**: Anchoring with Visual Evidence: Explicit Semantic Anchors for Joint Change Detection and Captioning

**作者**: Kai Zheng, Hang-Cheng Dong, Puyi Fang, Junjia Pan, Zhenkai Wu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-18

**arXiv ID**: [2611.24462v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24462v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24462v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Joint change detection and captioning seeks to localize changes and describe them from bi-temporal remote sensing images. A key challenge is translating dense visual evidence into the semantic conditions that guide description generation. We propose Explicit Semantic Anchors (ESA), a supervised interface that exposes entity presence, temporal appearance variation, and spatial extent. A prediction-guided readout combines visual features and predicted change probabilities, and three factor heads estimate the corresponding anchor activations. These activations guide generation through two complementary routes. The context route maps the three factor activations to three separate memory tokens, one per factor, and appends them to the dense visual memory, while the lexicon route produces an additive vocabulary bias at each decoding step. Dense visual memory remains available for spatial detail and information beyond the explicit factors. Anchor targets are derived from existing images and change annotations, requiring no additional anchor-level language annotation. At inference, all conditions are computed from the input image pair and the model's own predictions. Experiments on LEVIR-MCI and WHU-CDC show strong joint detection and captioning performance, supporting the use of supervised visual attributes as explicit conditions for change description generation.

</details>

## 9. Probing Physics in Motion Diffusion Models Against Their Own Estimate

**中文标题**: Probing Physics in Motion Diffusion Models Against Their Own Estimate

**作者**: Saeed Ghorbani

**发布日期**: 2026-11-18

**arXiv ID**: [2611.24423v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24423v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24423v1)

**类别**: cs.LG, cs.CV, cs.GR

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Recent studies report that video diffusion models "know more than they show": physical properties decode better from their activations than from their inputs or outputs. But a diffusion model computes its clean-sample estimate from those same activations, so such a comparison measures how easily a readout extracts the information. Whether the model hides anything is a separate question. We set out to find hidden physical knowledge in kinematic text-to-motion diffusion models, where the physics of every intermediate estimate can be computed exactly, and recorded every layer along the sampling trajectories of MDM (two checkpoints) and MotionDiffuse. Against the usual baselines the layers do look ahead of the model's own estimate on balance and flight. With every readout tuned the same way, the estimate closes the gap: for balance and flight, adding a layer gains at most 0.01, a size that a positive control shows we would detect. The fall rate of jumps is the one clear exception. Early in denoising, MDM's layers add about 0.08 in $R^2$ (up to 0.11 in a refit) that the estimate does not yet show, and the gain fades as sampling proceeds; on MotionDiffuse it is small and not robust. The estimate also flags floating samples without training, but aborting them saves under a tenth of the compute. In a video model, plausibility that linear probes miss in Wan 2.1's clean latent becomes readable after one fixed nonlinearity.

</details>

## 10. PhysResponse: Probing the Physics of Video Generation with Controlled Interventions

**中文标题**: PhysResponse: Probing the Physics of Video Generation with Controlled Interventions

**作者**: Yuhan Li, Shuran Zhang, Haixin Tian, Baonuo Rao, Weipeng Li et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-18

**arXiv ID**: [2611.24419v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24419v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24419v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video generation models are increasingly viewed as world simulators, which requires not only plausible motion but dynamics that respond correctly to specified physical parameters. Existing models, datasets and benchmarks, however, largely target physical plausibility, leaving this physical parameter responsiveness insufficiently explored. We introduce PhysResponse, a benchmark that measures it at two levels: scene-conditioned simulator inversion recovers the effective gravity, friction and initial speed of generated motion, and event evaluation tests outcomes near and across critical transitions. Evaluating ten image-to-video models, we find that even the lowest parameter errors exceed those of physically correct videos by over an order of magnitude, that models rarely reproduce both outcomes of a transition pair, and that the two levels expose complementary failures that visual metrics such as PSNR miss. To supply the missing training signal, we construct 72,000 simulated videos of 20 multi-object interaction types with controlled parameters; fine-tuning on them lowers parameter errors, even on held-out interaction types, and transfers to real videos and an external physics benchmark. Continued training with a numerical encoder further lowers all three errors on held-out types. PhysResponse provides a foundation for world simulators that follow physical conditions, not just appearances.

</details>

## 11. Semantic Guidance for Joint Infrared-Visible Video Fusion and Frame Interpolation

**中文标题**: Semantic Guidance for Joint Infrared-Visible Video Fusion and Frame Interpolation

**作者**: Yushen Xu, Xiaosong Li, Huaming Wu, Dongwei Ren

**发布日期**: 2026-11-18

**arXiv ID**: [2611.24277v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24277v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24277v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/XylonXu01/SAFI.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Infrared and visible video fusion at high frame rates requires recovering missing observations when camera rates differ or fall below the desired output rate. Cascading interpolation and fusion can accumulate errors and cause brightness fluctuations. Motion and occlusion further complicate content selection, particularly when missing visible frames also remove direct semantic guidance. We propose SAFI, which uses semantics at the target time to guide joint interpolation and fusion. When the visible frame rate exceeds the infrared rate, available visible frames provide semantic context directly. At times without visible observations, motion guides local matching, and propagation recovers semantics from neighboring frames. Semantic context complements aligned image features at the target time, while matching confidence helps select reliable infrared and visible information. Reconstruction and fusion share source selection within each scale, allowing supervision on missing frames to guide fusion.

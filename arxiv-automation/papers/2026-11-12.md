## 1. Measuring Robustness and Efficiency in a Connectome-Constrained Fly Visual System Model: Linking an Adversarial and a Perceptual Illusion

**中文标题**: Measuring Robustness and Efficiency in a Connectome-Constrained Fly Visual System Model: Linking an Adversarial and a Perceptual Illusion

**作者**: Alexandre Garcia-Duran, Alejandro Rodriguez-Garcia, Manuel Molano-Mazón, Alexandre Hyafil, Srikanth Ramaswamy et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-10

**arXiv ID**: [2611.14498v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14498v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14498v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, q-bio.NC

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Biological visual systems compute reliably under tight metabolic and wiring constraints, whereas artificial vision systems consume far more energy and can be sensitive to adversarial perturbations. Whether networks with brain-like wiring are more robust at lower computational cost is unclear. We address this by benchmarking a task-optimized, continuous-time recurrent neural network of the fly visual system constrained by its synaptic connectome (FlyNet) against artificial neural networks. We compare it against CNNs, RAFT, and continuous-time recurrent neural networks without detailed connectome constraints (sCTRNN, CTRNN) on a collision-detection task within a differentiable 3D rendering pipeline. FlyNet is adversarially vulnerable but, relative to its compute, more robust to common video corruptions than RAFT, while using up to five orders of magnitude fewer FLOPs. The CTRNNs remain more robust than FlyNet across almost all tested perturbations, suggesting that connectome constraints benefit efficiency, through their low edge count, over robustness. Recurrence improves robustness to white-box attacks in RAFT and temporal context improves robustness to common corruptions. FlyNet's position and velocity representations degrade under adversarial textures but recover under counterfactual textures that restore correct classification while staying equally far from training, ruling out unfamiliar textures as the cause of failure. The reverse-phi illusion similarly disrupts these representations and inverts the same low-level motion statistic as the adversarial textures. Optimizing this input statistic alone, without access to the network, suffices to induce classification failure, attributing both perturbations to the same circuit vulnerability. Connectome-constrained models can thus surface adversarial failure mechanisms as testable hypotheses about biological perceptual vulnerabilities.

</details>

## 2. UGG: Unified Generative Geometry Model

**中文标题**: UGG: Unified Generative Geometry Model

**作者**: Amani Kiruga, Zhiyi Li, Ruojin Cai, Hansen Lillemark, Xiaoshan Wu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-10

**arXiv ID**: [2611.14440v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14440v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14440v1)

**项目主页**: https://ugg-model.github.io/

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.LG, cs.RO

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Understanding 4D scenes is a core challenge in visual intelligence, requiring models to jointly capture scene geometry, appearance, and dynamics from partial observations. However, existing approaches are subdivided into feedforward models for direct geometric prediction, and generative models for visual synthesis. We introduce UGG, a unified framework for 4D scene understanding that jointly models RGB video and geometric representations such as depth and camera pose. By assigning different noise levels across modalities and time, UGG formulates visual generation and geometric prediction as conditional completion within a single generative model. This flexible formulation enables the model to learn from diverse, heterogeneous datasets with different subsets of geometric and visual annotations. Furthermore, our generative formulation enables inference-time search over multiple plausible scene hypotheses, using geometric evidence extracted from observed RGB frames to improve geometric prediction without ground truth geometry. We demonstrate UGG's effectiveness over non-unified baselines across camera-controlled video generation, depth estimation, and camera pose prediction, including in challenging sparse observation settings. Code and visualizations are available at https://ugg-model.github.io/

</details>

## 3. OnlyAct: Extracting Action Representations from Foundation Models for World Models

**中文标题**: OnlyAct: Extracting Action Representations from Foundation Models for World Models

**作者**: Runjia Li, Minghao Chen, Junyu Xie, Philip Torr, Andrea Vedaldi et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-10

**arXiv ID**: [2611.14420v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14420v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14420v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video world models promise general-purpose interactive simulators, but their training is limited by action supervision: action labels are scarce, fragmented across incompatible specifications, and do not scale with the unlabeled video pretraining corpus. A common workaround jointly trains an action encoder to compress consecutive frames into a latent action and a decoder to reconstruct the next frame from the previous frame and that latent, forcing the latent to capture only new information. This has two limitations: learning the action space from scratch can limit generalization to unseen domains, and the bottleneck trades expressiveness for separation, with tighter settings losing nuance and looser ones risking scene leakage under domain shifts. We argue for a different starting point: rather than learning actions from scratch, we start from a pretrained foundation model (V-JEPA 2), whose embeddings of a frame pair already carry substantial action information after large-scale, diverse video pretraining. The remaining challenge is to extract the action while discarding the scene. We propose a label-free contrastive adaptation framework that pulls together frame pairs sharing the same motion under a different scene and pushes apart pairs from the same scene with different motion, reshaping the geometry of the pretrained space without imposing a reconstruction bottleneck. We introduce a unified evaluation protocol along three axes (action expressiveness, scene invariance, and action-conditioned video generation quality) across robot manipulation, camera navigation, game, and driving benchmarks, most of which are unseen during adaptation. Compared with annotation-free latent-action methods, our representations are substantially more expressive while matching their scene invariance, and they yield world models that transfer demonstrated actions to new scenes more faithfully.

</details>

## 4. From Video Frames to Robot Meta-Frames: A Unified Latent Interface for World Action Models

**中文标题**: From Video Frames to Robot Meta-Frames: A Unified Latent Interface for World Action Models

**作者**: Guoqiang Liang, Yiqi Lin, Yanzhe Chen, Kevin Yuchen Ma, Mike Zheng Shou

**发布日期**: 2026-11-10

**arXiv ID**: [2611.14365v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14365v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14365v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/showlab/MetaWAM

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.RO

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Pretrained video diffusion models (VDMs) offer a powerful foundation for World Action Models (WAMs), which jointly predict robot actions and future observations. Building such models requires injecting low-dimensional robot signals (e.g. proprioception, actions, and values) into a latent space shaped by natural videos, and a key design choice is the interface through which these signals enter: existing schemes treat it as a formatting detail, spending a separate latent frame on each signal or bolting on dedicated encoders and decoders. We argue that signal injection is instead a distribution-alignment problem: how well a representation matches the prior of the pretrained VDMs during denoising. Using the relative reconstruction error of a frozen VDM denoiser as a probe, we find that conventional spatial encodings sit far off this prior, whereas frequency-domain packing yields latents that the denoiser corrects far less. Motivated by this diagnostic result, we propose MetaWAM, which folds all robot signals into a single unified meta-frame: each signal is assigned to a disjoint frequency-domain channel band and lifted to the spatial latent by an inverse DCT, yielding a decodable interface aligned with the pretrained VDM prior without any added module or architectural change. A fixed band layout together with slot-aware routing lets the same meta-frame support policy, world-model, and value prediction over a shared set of robot variables. On RoboCasa, MetaWAM reaches 68.9% success while collapsing the per-signal frames into one and cutting inference latency by 39%, and attains 85.0% success on three real-world Franka tasks. Code and models are available at https://github.com/showlab/MetaWAM

</details>

## 5. TaoMate-R1: Temporal Role Specialization for Streaming Multi-Reference Video Generation

**中文标题**: TaoMate-R1: Temporal Role Specialization for Streaming Multi-Reference Video Generation

**作者**: Qijun Gan, Chenwei Zhang, Xinyang Li, Meiguang Jin, Junfeng Ma

**发布日期**: 2026-11-10

**arXiv ID**: [2611.14307v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14307v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14307v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/TaoLiveAIGC/TaoMate-H3.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Streaming multi-reference video generation establishes a scene from specified people and objects, then continues it under changing instructions. We introduce TaoMate-R1, a streaming multi-reference generator that organizes adaptation parameters through temporal role specialization. It assigns separate low-rank adapters to scene establishment and continuation on a frozen backbone. Each adapter performs both denoising and context writing for its role; Self Gradient Forcing jointly trains the roles through the history they construct. This allocation contrasts with operation-based specialization, which separates denoising from context writing across all chunks. A fixed-replay gradient analysis characterizes the update directions admitted by the two sharing schemes. On 32 multi-reference cases, we compare the schemes at the same two-adapter budget. Across ten approximately two-minute streams with changing instructions and shared teacher-audio conditioning, TaoMate-R1 reduces the mean boundary luminance jump from 1.96 to 0.613, with higher boundary feature similarity. It also achieves higher mean CLIP and DINO temporal similarity across short interactions, long streams, and public reference compositions. These results support grouping adaptation parameters by scene establishment and continuation for streaming multi-reference generation. Our code and model are released at https://github.com/TaoLiveAIGC/TaoMate-H3.

</details>

## 6. Elastic-WAM: Adaptive Spatiotemporal Imagination for Efficient Robot Control

**中文标题**: Elastic-WAM: Adaptive Spatiotemporal Imagination for Efficient Robot Control

**作者**: Haitian Li, Tianfu Li, Xuxi Zhao, Zizheng Liu, Yangcheng Yu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-10

**arXiv ID**: [2611.14257v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14257v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14257v1)

**类别**: cs.RO, cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

World-Action Models improve robot control by predicting future visual dynamics, but iterative video denoising incurs substantial inference overhead. Recent approaches reduce this cost through partial future denoising, yet typically rely on fixed refinement budgets. Our empirical observations reveal that preferred refinement depths vary across tasks and states, while different future regions exhibit varying relevance to action generation. Motivated by these findings, we propose Elastic-WAM, which adaptively allocates world refinement computation across temporal denoising time and spatial future space. A unified spatiotemporal refinement demand field governs when to stop world refinement, how far to advance each denoising step, and which regions to refresh. We parameterize this field with a shared temporal basis and spatial amplitudes, and learn it through a three-stage action-grounded supervised procedure. At inference, the learned field guides recurrent sparse world refinement to improve task success while reducing inference overhead. Evaluations across diverse simulation benchmarks and real-world long-horizon manipulation demonstrate state-of-the-art control performance with reduced inference latency.

</details>

## 7. DailyWildfireCAN: A Dataset and Benchmark for Next-Day Wildfire Spread Prediction in Canada

**中文标题**: DailyWildfireCAN: A Dataset and Benchmark for Next-Day Wildfire Spread Prediction in Canada

**作者**: Mahacine Ettahri, Hager Radi Abdelwahed, Toluwaleke Olutayo, Daniel Aloise, Gaétan Marceau Caron

**发布日期**: 2026-11-10

**arXiv ID**: [2611.14207v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14207v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14207v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Wildfires represent a critical ecological and socio-economic challenge in Canada, where vast boreal forests and a warming climate have amplified the frequency and severity of extreme fire seasons. Predicting next-day wildfire spread can support fire management, but requires capturing complex interactions among weather, fuels, and terrain. Deep learning has emerged as a promising approach to learning these relationships from heterogeneous environmental data, yet its development and evaluation depend on representative datasets. Existing deep learning research on wildfire spread relies predominantly on US-focused datasets, leaving Canadian fire environments underrepresented. To address this gap, we present DailyWildfireCAN, a multi-modal, multi-temporal dataset and benchmark for predicting next-day wildfire spread across Canada. The dataset tracks continuous fire progression over multi-day events at a high spatial and temporal resolution of 90-m and 24-hour, respectively. This resolution preserves fine-scale information relevant to fire-edge geometry, local fuel variations, and terrain effects, supporting research toward improved daily wildfire spread forecasting. DailyWildfireCAN covers 2,440 wildfire events across eight fire seasons 2017-2024 and integrates 33 features across diverse modalities, including topography, meteorology, land cover, vegetation and forest structure, human influence indices, and satellite imagery. We establish a comprehensive benchmark comparing mono-temporal and multi-temporal prediction models, accompanied by a multi-dimensional evaluation framework assessing robustness across wildfire characteristics, geographic regions, and temporal conditions, spatial prediction quality, and sensitivity to reference label variations.

</details>

## 8. Looking One Step Ahead: An Anticipation-guided Agent for Object State Readout in Videos

**中文标题**: Looking One Step Ahead: An Anticipation-guided Agent for Object State Readout in Videos

**作者**: Yuanhe Tian, Yan Song

**发布日期**: 2026-11-10

**arXiv ID**: [2611.14113v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14113v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14113v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Understanding the progress of an activity requires tracking how the objects involved change over time. Object state readout provides this information by describing a target object's states and transitions across video frames. Although multimodal large language models (MLLMs) generate detailed visual descriptions, these descriptions may leave the attributes needed to distinguish successive states insufficiently specified. In this paper, we propose look-ahead state readout (LASER), an anticipation-guided agent for object state readout that uses possible next-step changes to determine which attributes to inspect in the present. Given temporally ordered frames and a target object, LASER converts anticipated changes into targeted visual questions and inspects the current frame to establish attribute values. It then checks state readouts and forecasts against independent observations of subsequent frames, refining a state record used to generate the final description. Reusable textual observation skills guide this process and are refined by tracing discrepancies with reference captions to the responsible instructions. Candidate revisions are selected on separate videos based on improved caption quality without increased contradictions with reference facts. Model parameters remain frozen throughout adaptation and inference, and the revised skills remain fixed during evaluation. Experiment results and further analysis on OSCaR and PLM RCap show consistent improvements of LASER over strong baselines and existing approaches, which demonstrates the effectiveness of our approach.

</details>

## 9. Obscured Wildfire Flame Detection by Spatio-temporal Analysis of Smoke Patterns Using Frame-wise Transformers

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

In this paper, we propose a new paradigm in wildfire management for detecting both visible and obscured fire using Deep Learning (DL). The essence of our method is the joint spatial and temporal analysis of RGB videos, embracing the fact that temporal patterns of smoke motions in consecutive video frames can indicate obscured fire flames. This perspective contrasts the traditional approach of independently analyzing images and individual video frames. To this end, we propose a frame-wise transformer architecture that applies an attention mechanism between the consecutive frames while preserving spatial information within frames as opposed to regular attention mechanisms like the non-local block, which combines spatial and temporal information into a single vector. We also optimize the temporal pattern lengths under different spatial resolutions to achieve the highest performance while reducing the computational cost. To investigate the applicability of our method in aerial image processing, we applied it to a curated version of the FLAME2 dataset that includes dual-mode RGB/IR videos. The IR video feed is used to extract ground truth, while the model is trained only on RGB videos to maintain applicability to lowcost commercial drones (which are equipped only with regular cameras) and facilitate processing existing RGB-only datasets. Our proposed method achieves a Segmentation Foreground Dice score of 92.61%, an Object Detection Precision and Recall rate of 93.21% and 91.73%, and clip-level classification accuracy of 98.02%, respectively. We also investigated the efficacy and generalizability of our method through ablation study

## 10. CURRENT: A Recurrent Neural Network for Concurrent Spatial and Temporal Super-Resolution of 4D Flow MRI

**中文标题**: CURRENT: A Recurrent Neural Network for Concurrent Spatial and Temporal Super-Resolution of 4D Flow MRI

**作者**: Luuk Jacobs, Pietro Dirix, Sebastian Kozerke

**发布日期**: 2026-11-10

**arXiv ID**: [2611.14058v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14058v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14058v1)

**类别**: cs.CV, physics.med-ph

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Objective: To improve the spatial and temporal resolution of aortic 4D flow MRI through a unified deep learning framework for post-acquisition super-resolution (SR) of reconstructed velocity vector fields.   Methods: We propose CURRENT, a recurrent neural network that performs spatial and temporal SR with a single network architecture. Supervised training was conducted using paired low- and high-resolution datasets generated by embedding computational fluid dynamics simulations of aortic flow into in vivo thoracic MRI backgrounds, followed by controlled spatial and temporal downsampling. CURRENT was evaluated for two-fold spatial and temporal SR against 4DFlowNet and for two-fold spatiotemporal SR against sequential spatial and temporal SR pipelines based on 4DFlowNet. Image-based and k-space-based quantitative metrics were considered and conventional interpolation were used as baselines. ## 79. Physics-Informed Sinusoidal Representation Networks for Damage Identification in Ultrasonic Guided Wave Structural Health Monitoring

**中文标题**: Physics-Informed Sinusoidal Representation Networks for Damage Identification in Ultrasonic Guided Wave Structural Health Monitoring

**作者**: Lukas Jilke, Caspar Wasle, Norbert Hosters, Florian Raddatz, Marek Behr et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-11-09

**arXiv ID**: [2611.12299v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12299v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12299v1)

**类别**: cs.CE, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Ultrasonic guided wave (UGW) inspection has become an effective method in structural health monitoring (SHM). While machine learning has enhanced damage detection capabilities, purely data-driven models remain constrained by extensive labeled datasets and limited physical interpretability. Standard multi-layer perceptrons (MLPs) further suffer from spectral bias (F-Principle) in high-frequency regimes, prioritizing low-frequency components while failing to resolve the high-frequency oscillations critical for UGW reconstruction. Physics-Informed Sinusoidal Representation Networks (PI-SIRENs) address these limitations by embedding governing partial differential equations directly into the training process. Building upon SIRENs, which utilize periodic activation functions to inherently capture high-frequency spectral content, the proposed framework is designed to operate effectively in sparse ultrasonic measurement regimes. This work introduces a frequency-domain PI-SIREN framework tailored for the Mindlin-Reissner plate theory to reconstruct ultrasonic wavefields without temporal discretization. The model parameters are optimized using a hybrid strategy comprising three phases: first-order Adam, residual-adaptive refinement (RAR) and quasi-Newton L-BFGS. Numerical and experimental evaluations confirm that the model serves as a physics-consistent baseline derived from sparse data, functioning as a computationally efficient surrogate for damage identification in SHM and non-destructive testing (NDT). Integrating the surrogate into a damage identification pipeline detects and localizes a milled notch from sparse experimental measurements with a localization error of 1 % of the plate diagonal. In contrast, data-driven benchmarks and MLPs with hyperbolic tangent activation functions, with and without Fourier feature embedding, are unable to identify damage due to higher reconstruction errors.

## 1. Reading, Writing, Sharing, and Interacting with the World through Video

**中文标题**: Reading, Writing, Sharing, and Interacting with the World through Video

**作者**: Shengqiong Wu, Meng Luo, Bobo Li, Ziwei Liu, Shuicheng Yan et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.31668v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31668v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31668v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video foundation models have advanced rapidly in understanding and generating visual sequences, while video world models seek to capture the underlying dynamics of the world they depict. Despite their different objectives, both concern the relationship between video observations and an evolving world. This survey adopts world modeling as a unifying lens for examining these developments. Viewing an observed video as a partial, lossy, and temporally ordered projection of a latent temporal world, we organize the literature around four complementary operations: reading infers world properties from observed video; writing synthesizes video from specified or learned world conditions; sharing integrates understanding and generation through common modeling mechanisms; and interacting predicts and updates world states under actions and evolving observations. This perspective distinguishes temporal coherence in observed or generated sequences from consistency in the underlying world state. Across tasks, representations, models, datasets, and benchmarks, we trace a shift in emphasis from clip-level recognition and perceptual fidelity toward persistent state, controllable evolution, physical and causal validity, and evaluation grounded in interaction and downstream decisions, and we distill the open challenges that follow.

</details>

## 2. WorldForge V2: Eliciting Camera-Trajectory Alignment in Video Diffusion Models

**中文标题**: WorldForge V2: Eliciting Camera-Trajectory Alignment in Video Diffusion Models

**作者**: Chenxi Song, Yanming Yang, Tong Zhao, Ruibo Li, Joey Tianyi Zhou et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.31640v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31640v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31640v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Video diffusion models have learned rich 3D/4D priors, making them promising for scene generation and video re-rendering. However, their use in spatial tasks is limited by imprecise camera control, spatial and temporal inconsistencies, and entangled camera motion with scene dynamics. Existing approaches, such as task-specific fine-tuning and post-process warping, often introduce visual artifacts, generalize poorly, or incur high computational costs. We propose WorldForge V2 to achieve accurate camera control while preserving the generative capabilities of pretrained video models. We first develop a training-free guidance framework using warped references to iteratively refine generation along a target camera path. Optical-flow analysis identifies motion-related latent channels for selective guidance, helping separate motion control from appearance. A dual-path strategy further compares guided and unguided predictions to correct artifacts from imperfect references. Together, these components guide viewpoint changes while preserving scene appearance and object motion. To reduce refinement costs, v-cache approximates the correction using the preceding denoising prediction, avoiding an additional model evaluation. We further extend motion localization from latent channels to network blocks and introduce Trajectory LoRA, which concentrates adaptation on blocks most responsive to trajectory guidance. A two-stage training strategy teaches these adapters to denoise the states encountered during guided generation, using limited supervision effectively. This enables reliable camera control with very little training data, with a single training scene already supporting generalization to unseen scenes. Quantitative and qualitative evaluations across multiple datasets show that our method achieves state-of-the-art generation quality and trajectory accuracy in camera-controlled video generation.

</details>

## 3. CellTrack-Bench: Microscopic Cell Tracking Benchmark for Vision-Language Models

**中文标题**: CellTrack-Bench: Microscopic Cell Tracking Benchmark for Vision-Language Models

**作者**: Ilan Theodoro, Jordão Bragantini, Loïc A. Royer

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.31566v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31566v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31566v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/royerlab/celltrack-bench.

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Large language models have shown outstanding performance on text-first tasks such as coding, math, and writing. Recent efforts are shifting models' training to include scientific problems that often live in highly specialized domains beyond text, such as protein structure and biological images. However, in these scenarios, their general-purpose accuracy compared with specialized algorithms remains in question. One such domain is cell tracking, which requires spatiotemporal awareness of cell dynamics. To evaluate this, we created a new benchmark, CellTrack-Bench, which assesses Vision-Language Models' (VLMs) cell-tracking-related performance against specialized trackers. The full benchmark draws from 15 standard cell-tracking datasets across four imaging modalities and comprises 1,537 problems corresponding to 9,041 cell-linking decisions. We selected a hard subset requiring greater reasoning effort by identifying failures shared across distinct algorithmic trackers. With this proposed dataset, we: (i) evaluated different environment harnesses for VLMs; (ii) benchmarked 9 frontier closed-source models and 13 open-weight models; and (iii) provided a detailed analysis of their failure modes. Code, the stored model outputs, and a pipeline that regenerates the benchmark from the source datasets are available at https://github.com/royerlab/celltrack-bench.

## 4. MoG-VLM: Efficient Video Anomaly Detection through Motion-Guided Vision-Language Models

**中文标题**: MoG-VLM: Efficient Video Anomaly Detection through Motion-Guided Vision-Language Models

**作者**: Khaled Waleed Dawoud, Muhammad Zaigham Zaheer, Khan Muhammad, Muhammad Haris Khan

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.31478v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31478v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31478v1)

**代码仓库**: https://github.com/kdawoud91/MoG-VLM.

**类别**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have shown strong potential for video anomaly detection, but dense frame-level inference makes them impractical for real-time deployment. Existing acceleration commonly relies on uniform sampling, which ignores content dynamics and can either waste computation on static segments or miss localized anomalies. We propose MoG-VLM a motion-adaptive anomaly detection framework that leverages motion purely as a lightweight control signal to decide when VLM inference is necessary. To this end, we introduce Temporal Gradient Magnitude (TGM) which performs content-aware sampling to concentrate computation on informative regions while avoiding redundant processing. We further introduce a hierarchical dual-stream design in which dense multi-frame VLM reasoning is activated only around frames with elevated anomaly likelihood, via anchor-triggered context-window expansion with early termination. A fusion module then reconciles these instantaneous semantic cues with temporal action dynamics. Across four benchmarks, our method achieves 91.91%, 84.30%, 87.88%, and 78.96% AUC-ROC on XD-Violence, UCF-Crime, MSAD, and ShanghaiTech, respectively, establishing new state of the art on three benchmarks under the zero-shot, training-free setting. On XD-Violence, we improve over the previous best result by +0.57% while delivering a 1.31x inference speedup. These results demonstrate that content-aware allocation of VLM capacity, processing fewer frames more intelligently rather than all frames uniformly, is a more effective axis of progress than uniformly densifying VLM inference across frames. Code is available on GitHub: https://github.com/kdawoud91/MoG-VLM.

## 5. Ask About the Right Moment: Training-Free Object Identity Reasoning for Video-Language Models

**中文标题**: Ask About the Right Moment: Training-Free Object Identity Reasoning for Video-Language Models

**作者**: Di Yang, Xiaochang Li, Muyang Liu, Yufei Wang, Yanhai Xiong

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.31471v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31471v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31471v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Many questions about a video concern one physical object. Where does the ball end up after the cups are shuffled? Which card is the queen? How often was the mug covered? Video-language models (Video-LMs) recognise such objects in single frames but do not keep them apart over time. On real shell-game videos, open models from 8B to 235B parameters, their thinking variants, a reinforcement-learned tool agent and three training-free tool agents all stay within ten points of chance. We separate semantic identity binding from temporal identity maintenance. An object's identity should be fixed at a moment when the evidence for it is visible, and carried afterwards by a tracker. \emph{Track, Then Bind} implements this principle without training. A promptable video tracker follows every candidate object and draws it with a track number. A frozen vision-language model answers a static question about the identifying evidence, which binds the target to a track. The answer is then derived from the tracked state in text, without asking the model to reinterpret the original video during read-out. A single configuration covers all benchmarks. With Qwen3-VL-32B it solves both VET-Bench tasks, where a fine-tuned trajectory model reaches 92\% and 90\% and the same VLM voting over 16 samples stays at 30--32\%. It answers 55.4\% of the hardest Perception Test cup games, where the best published model answers 41.5\%, and, using track-derived timelines, 48.6\% of TOC-Bench questions, against 37.1\% for the same model answering directly. Drawing the same tracks on the video and asking the question directly falls 12--19 points short of the agent on real cup games.

</details>

## 6. AnyGameRenderer: Entity-Consistent Generative Rendering Across Games and an Entity-Level Benchmark

**中文标题**: AnyGameRenderer: Entity-Consistent Generative Rendering Across Games and an Entity-Level Benchmark

**作者**: Ruicong Liu, Liangyang Ouyang, Fengbo Lan, Zhixiang Wang, Kaipeng Zhang

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.31466v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31466v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31466v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

AI coding agents can now build small playable games in minutes, but the results usually look like programmer art: gray blockouts, colored geometric primitives, and flat semantic blobs. We present AnyGameRenderer, a video-to-video generative renderer that turns such footage into high-quality visuals with characters, motion detail, and effects. Throughout, it keeps the layout, camera, and every entity's position and timing intact.   Our AnyGameRenderer generalizes across a wide range of genres that agents build, such as tower defense, auto-battlers, top-down survivors, turn-based battles, kart racing, and crowd runners.   AnyGameRenderer takes plain video as its game-state input, with no complex representations such as engine G-buffers, per-object class labels, or low-poly proxies. Gray blockouts, colored primitives, semantic maps, and wireframes all work as input.   It also preserves entity correspondence: group counts stay correct, and entities appear and disappear on the right frame.   We implement AnyGameRenderer by LoRA fine-tuning MiniMax-H3 ref2va, a 33B reference-video-conditioned video generation model. Training uses $\sim$10k paired clips from three sources: (i) multi-stream rendering data from 38 agent-made games exported from GEAR, an AI game-creation tool; (ii) two-stream rendering data from Unreal Engine, with programmatically controlled crowds, count changes, and entity scales; and (iii) real gameplay recordings, with pairs reconstructed via 3D-box detection.   To evaluate the entity correspondence, we propose an entity-level benchmark of 59 held-out cases with 753 entities, scored against ground-truth boxes. Compared with the base model, AnyGameRenderer better preserves entity correspondence: temporal match rises from 50.9\% to 62.9\%, missed count changes fall from 34.7\% to 21.3\%, and fewer entities are hallucinated or left unrendered.

</details>

## 8. Stream4D: Streaming 4D Reconstruction with Training-free Motion Decoupling via Causal KV-Cache Attention

**中文标题**: Stream4D: Streaming 4D Reconstruction with Training-free Motion Decoupling via Causal KV-Cache Attention

**作者**: Wei Zhang, Ying Feng, Mengtong Wu, Shiqiang Gong, Huaqi Zhang et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.31320v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31320v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31320v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

4D scene reconstruction requires robust handling of dynamic objects, yet existing streaming methods commonly assume static scenes, leading to pose drift and ghosting artifacts over long sequences. We introduce Stream4D, a training-free, motion-decoupled streaming reconstruction framework that can be directly integrated into causal Transformer backbones (e.g., StreamVGGT, Stream3R). Our key observation is that causal attention implicitly encodes rich temporal motion cues within the KV cache. We adaptively aggregate these cues from multiple attention layers and across temporal windows, and fuse them with depth priors to handle challenging cases such as occlusions and textureless regions, producing precise dynamic masks that decouple static and dynamic regions. We then apply a soft key masking scheme that leverages these dynamic masks to suppress interference from dynamic regions in causal attention, thereby improving camera pose estimation accuracy. Stream4D inherits the efficiency of causal attention and remains compatible with existing streaming architectures. On diverse dynamic-scene benchmarks, Stream4D significantly improves camera pose estimation and static-scene reconstruction quality, yields reliable dynamic masks, and effectively mitigates long-sequence ghosting.

</details>

## 9. Retrieval-Augmented Online Textual Memory for Episodic Memory Video Question Answering

**中文标题**: Retrieval-Augmented Online Textual Memory for Episodic Memory Video Question Answering

**作者**: Raffaele Cali, Giuseppe Lando, Rosario Forte, Antonino Furnari

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.31284v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31284v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31284v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Long egocentric videos pose a central challenge for multimodal language models: the visual stream must be compressed into a memory representation that can be searched efficiently while still preserving the evidence needed for question answering. Building on an online textual-memory framework in which first-person videos are converted into temporally grounded captions, this work systematically studies the role of retrieval in episodic-memory video question answering. We evaluate how different retrieval units, query formulations, context budgets, and reranking strategies affect multiple-choice answering on QAEgo4D-Closed, isolating retrieval as the main variable while keeping the underlying generated memory fixed.   Our best configuration, Parent, matches fine-grained caption subchunks, then provides their full parent captions to the reasoner. It achieves 56.4 % accuracy, matching strong full-memory and retrieval baselines while exposing only a small fraction of the textual memory at question time. More importantly, our analysis shows that better temporal coverage does not necessarily translate into better answers: increasing the retrieval budget improves temporal Hit@K and Recall@K, but can reduce accuracy by introducing visually plausible distractors and uncertain caption evidence. These findings suggest that retrieval-augmented egocentric memory should not be optimized only for evidence recall, but also for the reliability, specificity, and answer-discriminative value of the retrieved context. The study motivates more structured episodic memories that move beyond raw caption retrieval toward evidence-aware representations for long-form egocentric reasoning.

## 10. Lightweight Frame Compensation for Real-Time Polyp Segmentation under Frame Skipping

**中文标题**: Lightweight Frame Compensation for Real-Time Polyp Segmentation under Frame Skipping

**作者**: Weihao Gao

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.31228v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31228v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31228v1)

**类别**: cs.CV

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Real-time polyp segmentation in computer-assisted colonoscopy is hampered by the difficulty of deploying high-accuracy models under strict latency budgets. Frame skipping, which performs inference every K frames and reuses or interpolates the rest, improves throughput but introduces motion blur and lesion misalignment that degrade temporal consistency. To close this gap, we propose the Lightweight Frame Compensator (LFC), a 77K-parameter plug-and-play module inserted before a frozen segmentation backbone. Operating on a three-frame window, LFC fuses consecutive frames via spatio-temporal attention into a single compensated frame, and is trained only on pseudo-labels generated by a differently seeded model, without any expert annotation. **作者**: Tim Nico Bauerschmidt, Isabel Valera, Jilles Vreeken

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.31114v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31114v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31114v1)

**类别**: cs.LG, stat.ML

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

In real-world applications, being able to explain why a prediction is made is often as important - if not more so - than achieving high accuracy. However, existing methods for sequential event prediction typically prioritize accuracy at the expense of interpretability. Neuro-symbolic approaches offer a promising direction to improve interpretability by learning symbolic rules, but extending them to next-event prediction is challenging due to the presence of temporal dependencies. We introduce RULES, a neuro-symbolic model that learns sequential rules directly from data, and combine it with a residual network to form RUNES (Rule Network for Event Sequences). RULES accurately recovers ground truth rules from synthetic data and discovers compact, interpretable rule sets on real data. RUNES combines these rules additively with a residual network, achieving competitive accuracy while providing transparent access to active rules and how residuals adjust predictions.

</details>

## 31. EEG-Captioner: Grounding EEG-to-Language Generation in Fundamental Signal Properties

**中文标题**: EEG-Captioner: Grounding EEG-to-Language Generation in Fundamental Signal Properties

**作者**: Leyuan Jin, Jiahao Mei, Yixuan Yuan, Shimeng Li, Mengyue Wu et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.30995v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30995v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30995v1)

**类别**: cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

Electroencephalography (EEG)-to-language models offer a promising approach for translating complex neural signals into human-readable descriptions. However, generating plausible language does not necessarily demonstrate that a model has learned to interpret the underlying EEG signal. A direct way to assess such interpretation is to probe objectively measurable properties of the signal itself. We therefore construct an EEG-language dataset of 160,781 segments annotated with signal-level question answering (QA) pairs, where each question targets one of five property families and requires a quantitative comparison involving spectral, spatial, temporal, or lateral signal properties. Each segment is also paired with a feature-grounded EEG caption. We further propose a Dual-Domain EEG Encoder that jointly models temporal waveforms and spectral-spatial representations, and introduce EEG-Captioner, a unified EEG-to-language framework trained with both signal-level QA and EEG caption supervision. Experiments show that the proposed encoder achieves the highest overall macro-averaged balanced accuracy (Macro-BA) among the compared EEG encoders, while EEG-Captioner achieves a Macro-BA of 88.81% on signal-level QA and improves caption factual consistency from 82.06% to 83.46% over caption-only language adaptation, demonstrating the complementary benefits of signal-level QA and caption supervision.

</details>

## 32. Energy and Latency First: How Deployment Constraints Reverse Time-Series Model Selection

**中文标题**: Energy and Latency First: How Deployment Constraints Reverse Time-Series Model Selection

**作者**: Shruti Bothe, Illyyne Saffar, Ayush K. Varshney

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.30965v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30965v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30965v1)

**类别**: cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

AI models, and time-series models in particular are typically ranked by accuracy and deployed under constraints absent from that ranking. In this paper, we study a real setting where these constraints are explicit and measurable: handover pre- diction in cellular networks, where inference runs on the User Equipment once per 40 ms measurement period and must complete before the network acts. The timing bound is hard: a late prediction is superseded by the measurement it was predicting, while energy is a cumulative budget spent for the life of the device. Neither appears in the objective these models are selected on. We evaluate eight architectures under a protocol that screens for deadline feasibility at the tail rather than the mean, then ranks the feasible models on measured inference energy, count- ing the feature computation that precedes the forward pass and is usually omitted. Gradient-boosted trees on handcrafted temporal features are the most accurate model (F1=0.974) and among the cheapest to run, while the neural architecture an accuracy-only benchmark would rank first (CNN-1D, F1=0.914) costs over 3 times more energy per inference under a realistic duty-cycled deployment pattern. We give three conditions under which this outcome should be expected, so the finding applies beyond telecommunications. Accuracy-only benchmarking does not merely under-report deployment cost. It can rank first a model that cannot be deployed.

</details>

## 33. PAIR: Preference-Aligned Intent Retrieval for Premium People Recommendation at LinkedIn

**中文标题**: PAIR: Preference-Aligned Intent Retrieval for Premium People Recommendation at LinkedIn

**作者**: Zhongkai Shangguan, Yanbin Kang, Yihan Cao, Ruijie Jiang, Qiannan Yin et al.

**发布日期**: 2026-10-22

**arXiv ID**: [2610.30941v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30941v1)

**PDF**: [下载链接](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30941v1)

**类别**: cs.IR, cs.LG

<details>
<summary><b>摘要 (Abstract)</b></summary>

General-purpose embeddings trained on profile similarity often misrank candidates in professional networks, where relevance is inherently complementary rather than symmetric. For example, job seekers usually engage with recruiters rather than peers with similar credentials. Current user recommendation systems are often built on profile similarity or social signals such as shared connections, which are effective in friend-oriented social networks where users tend to connect with similar people or within existing social circles. However, this does not hold in professional platforms like LinkedIn, where interactions are often goal-driven and cross-role interactions, requiring matching based on complementary intents rather than similarity or social proximity. In this paper, we propose Preference-Aligned Intent Retrieval, a retrieval framework that is designed to model user intent preference instead of profile similarity. Our approach is inspired by the preference-learning perspective of Direct Preference Optimization, but instantiates preference learning as an exposure-conditioned multi-negative contrastive objective over embeddings. This formulation simplifies training and scales efficiently to large candidate sets without requiring an explicit reference model. Furthermore, we incorporate user interaction history by aggregating click histories with positional encoding to capture temporal order, which enables constant-cost updates of user representations over a fixed history window, allowing new interactions to be incorporated without re-encoding history. As a result, personalization can be updated efficiently using lightweight CPU operations without repeated LLM inference during serving. We evaluate PAIR on LinkedIn's Premium retrieval task. A 0.6B parameter model achieves a MAR@100 of 0.45.

</details>
